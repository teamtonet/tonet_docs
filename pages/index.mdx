# Tonet Help

Tonet is a business phone system that runs in your browser. You get phone numbers, a line for each person, call routing, texting, team chat, voicemail and contacts. You pay by the day, with no contract.

## Start here

1. [Sign up](/sign_up). Pick a number and add at least $15 of credit.
2. [Add your team](/users). Each person gets an extension and a sign-in.
3. [Choose where calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go). Ring one person, a [team](/groups), or a [menu](/auto_attendant).
4. [Make a call](/dialer) from the dashboard.

## Where things are

Sign in at [dashboard.tonet.ai](https://dashboard.tonet.ai). The menu on the left has:

| Menu item | What's there | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Today's or this month's calls and texts, and who's online | Everyone |
| **Manage** | Phones, Menus, Schedules, Calendar, Users and Teams | Owners, managers, admins |
| **Calls** | Every call and voicemail, with recordings | Everyone |
| **Messages** | Texts with customers and chat with your team | Everyone |
| **Contacts** | Everyone you talk to, with notes | Everyone |
| **Analytics** | Answering, dialing and team numbers | Owners, managers, admins |

Owners and managers also see **Account** and **Billing** inside **Manage**.

## The top bar

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Dialpad icon | Opens the phone. See [Dialer & Calling](/dialer). |
| Signal bars | Your connection to Tonet. Click it to run a network test. |
| Bell | Missed calls, voicemails and messages from the last 7 days |
| Balance | Opens [Billing](/billing). Owners and managers only. |
| Your picture | Your status, [Profile](/profile), Refer & earn, [Enable click to call](/features/click_to_call), and Logout |

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything. Every account has one owner. |
| **Manager** | Everything, including Billing and Account settings |
| **Administrator** | Phones, menus, schedules, calendar, users and teams. No Billing or Account. |
| **Regular User** | Calls, messages and contacts. Sees their own calls and their teams' calls. |

Change someone's role in [Users](/users#change-a-role).

## Ways to take calls

- **In the browser.** Nothing to install. Allow the microphone when asked.
- **The [desktop app](/app).** Keeps your line on with the window closed. Linux now; Windows and Mac are coming.
- **A desk phone.** Any standard SIP phone works. See [IP Phones](/ip_phones).
