# Introduction

Welcome to Tonet's documentation. This guide covers everything you need to set up and manage your business phone system.

## What is Tonet?

Tonet is a business phone system built for small businesses. You get call routing, extensions, voicemail with AI transcription, SMS, call recording, auto attendant menus, and more - starting at $15 per user per month with no contracts.

After signing in at [dashboard.tonet.ai](https://dashboard.tonet.ai), you'll see the main dashboard.

## Overview Page

The Overview page is the first thing you see after logging in. It combines a real-time communications feed, team status, and live call monitoring in one view.

### Controls

- **Team / User toggle** (admin only) - Switch between viewing all team activity or just your own calls and messages. Your preference is saved between sessions.
- **Month / Today toggle** - Filter the feed to show the current month's activity or just today's.

### All Communications Feed

The main panel shows a chronological feed of all activity on your phone system. Each event is color-coded with a left border:

- **Green** - Answered calls
- **Red** - Busy calls
- **Blue** - Unanswered calls (no answer, ringing timeout)
- **Grey** - Failed calls

Each call entry shows direction, participants, duration, and disposition. If a call has a recording, you can play it inline. If a call has been transcribed, you can expand it to read the transcript right from the feed.

Message events show a preview of the text (first 280 characters) with a "Read more" option, plus any media attachments (images, files).

You can also add **notes** to the feed for your own reference.

### Search and Filters

Use the search bar at the top of the feed to search across all calls, messages, and transcriptions. You can toggle filters to show only:

- **Calls**
- **Text messages**
- **Transcripts**

Search results are highlighted and you can navigate between matches.

### Users Card

The sidebar card shows how many team members are currently online (e.g. "3 / 5 online") with a link to the [Users](/users) management page.

## Active Calls

Below the communications feed, the **Active Calls** panel shows all calls currently in progress on your phone system. Each active call displays the team member, the remote party's number, and a live duration counter.

Managers and owners can also **listen in** on active calls or **whisper** coaching to team members without the caller hearing. See [Call Monitoring](/analytics/call_monitoring) for full details.
