# Profile

Your personal settings - display name, profile picture, and browser notifications. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner of the dashboard and select **Profile** to open this page.

For company-level settings like timezone and business address, see [Account](/account).

The Profile page has two tabs: **General** and **Notifications**.

---

## General

### Profile Picture

Upload a profile picture that your team sees in [Chat](/chat), call logs, and anywhere your name appears in the dashboard. Click the avatar to upload a new image or remove the current one.

### Basic Details

- **Display Name** - The name your team sees. Change it and click **Save Changes** to update.
- **Email Address** - Your login email. Read-only - contact your administrator to change it.
- **Extension** - Your internal extension number for receiving calls. Read-only - your administrator assigns this in [Users](/users).

---

## Notifications

Get notified about incoming calls, texts, and voicemails - even when the dashboard isn't your active tab.

### Status

Check if notifications are set up:

- **Browser Support** - All modern browsers support desktop notifications
- **Permission** - If you haven't allowed notifications yet, click **Request Permission** and accept the prompt.

<Callout type="info">
  If you previously denied notification permission, you'll need to reset it in your browser settings. In Chrome: click the lock icon in the address bar, find Notifications, and change it to Allow.
</Callout>

### Notification Types

Choose what you want to be notified about:

| Type | What it does |
|------|-------------|
| **Incoming Calls** | Desktop notification when a call comes in on any of your [phone numbers](/phones) |
| **Text Messages** | Notification when an SMS arrives on a number you have [permissions](/phones) for |
| **Voicemail** | Notification when a new voicemail is left |

All three are enabled by default. Turn off any type you don't want notifications for.

<Callout type="info">
  You won't get a notification popup for something you're already looking at. For example, no SMS notification while you're on the [Chat](/chat) page.
</Callout>

### Test Notifications

Use the test buttons to verify notifications are working:

- **Test Call** - Sends a sample incoming call notification
- **Test SMS** - Sends a sample text message notification
- **Test Voicemail** - Sends a sample voicemail notification

If test notifications don't appear, make sure you've allowed notifications in your browser and that Do Not Disturb is off on your computer.
