# Chat

Chat handles both SMS text messages to customers and internal direct messages between your team - all in one place.

SMS conversations are shared across your team. When a customer texts your business number, every team member with [permissions](/phones) on that number sees the conversation and can respond. No messages slip through the cracks because someone was on break or out sick - whoever is available picks it up. The customer just sees one number and one conversation.

Go to **Chat** in the sidebar to open your conversations.

## SMS vs DM

Toggle between the two modes using the **Chat / SMS** switch:

- **SMS** - Text messages sent and received through your Tonet [phone numbers](/phones). Messages go over the carrier network to any phone number. Every team member authorized on the number shares the same conversation - so your whole team can see and respond to customer texts. Requires [10DLC verified](/phones) numbers.
- **Chat (DM)** - Instant messages between team members on your Tonet account. No phone number needed - just search for a user by name or extension.

The sidebar lists all your conversations with **SMS** and **DM** filter buttons so you can show one type, the other, or both.

## Starting a New Conversation

Click **New** in the sidebar to compose a new message.

### SMS

1. Toggle to **SMS** mode
2. **From** - Select which of your phone numbers to send from. Only [verified](/phones) numbers with SMS permissions appear here.
3. **To** - Click **Add recipient** and enter the phone number you want to text
4. Type your message and hit send

<Callout type="info">
  SMS currently supports one recipient per conversation. Your phone numbers must be [10DLC registered](/phones) before you can send SMS. Numbers without 10DLC verification won't appear in the From dropdown.
</Callout>

### DM (Direct Message)

1. Toggle to **Chat** mode
2. Search for team members by name or extension
3. Select one or more recipients
4. Type your message and hit send

## Sending Messages

Type your message in the input field at the bottom and press **Enter** or click the send button.

### Attachments

Attach files by clicking the image or file button, or paste a screenshot directly into the message input. You can also drag and drop files into the chat.

**SMS/MMS limits:**
- Up to 10 files per message
- 5 MB total size limit
- Supported types: images (JPEG, PNG, GIF), videos (MP4), audio (MP3, OGG), and documents (PDF, vCard)

**DM limits** are more generous - up to 100 MB for videos and 25 MB for documents.

### Emoji and GIFs

Click the emoji button to open the picker. It includes a GIF search so you can find and send animated GIFs directly in the conversation.

### Replies

Reply to a specific message to keep conversations organized. The reply shows a preview of the original message above your response.

## Conversation Sidebar

Click the sidebar icon in the top right to open additional panels:

- **Files** - All attachments shared in the current conversation
- **Timeline** - Bookmarked messages for quick reference
- **Info** - Conversation details and participants

<Image src="/images/chat/chat_info.png" alt="Chat with Conversation Info sidebar open showing participants, message count, and conversation timeline" width={1500} height={780} style={{borderRadius: '8px', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}}/>

### Bookmarks

Bookmark important messages to find them later. Bookmarked messages appear in the Timeline panel with optional notes. Use this to pin key decisions, action items, or reference information within a conversation.

---

## Outbound Phone Number

When sending SMS, the **From** selector at the bottom of the chat lets you switch between your verified phone numbers mid-conversation. The currently selected number is the one your messages are sent from.

Only phone numbers where you have outbound text [permissions](/phones) and that are [10DLC verified](/phones) are available.
