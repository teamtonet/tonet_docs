# Mobile App

Take your business phone system with you. The Tonet mobile app gives you the same calling, texting, and voicemail features you get in the [dashboard](/index) - right on your phone. Your phone numbers, contacts, and call history stay in sync across all your devices.

Available for [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tonet/id6755547036) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.tonet).

---

## Getting Started

Download the app from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with the same email and password you use for the [web dashboard](https://dashboard.tonet.ai).

---

## Home

<Image src="/images/app/home.webp" alt="Tonet mobile app home screen showing messages and call history icons" width={300} height={650} style={{borderRadius: '8px', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}}/>

The Home tab is your starting point. It shows quick counts for unread messages and missed calls so you can see at a glance what needs attention.

### Conversations

Tap messages to open your conversations. Three filters at the top let you switch views:

- **All** - Every conversation, both team messages and SMS
- **Team** - Internal messages with your team members only
- **SMS** - Customer text messages, organized by phone number

Tap any conversation to open it. Type a message and send, or attach an image. Messages sync across all your devices - anything you send from the app shows up in the [web dashboard Chat](/chat) too.

To start a new conversation, tap the compose button and choose whether you're messaging a team member or sending an SMS to an external number.

<Image src="/images/app/new_conversation.webp" alt="New Conversation screen with Team Message and Text Message SMS options" width={300} height={650} style={{borderRadius: '8px', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}}/>

### Call History

View your complete call log, grouped by date. Each entry shows:

- Caller or recipient name and number
- Call direction (inbound or outbound)
- Duration
- Whether it went to voicemail

Tap any call to call the number back. Voicemails have a play button to listen right from the list.

## Active Calls

When you're on a call, a call screen appears with:

- Caller name and number
- Call duration timer
- **Mute** - Mute or unmute yourself
- **Speaker** - Switch to speakerphone
- **Hold** - Put the call on hold
- **Hang up** - End the call

### Call Transfer

Transfer an active call to another team member or external number. Tap transfer, search for the person by name or extension, and select them. The call hands off automatically.

### Conference Calls

Merge multiple active calls into a conference. Select which calls to add and the app connects everyone together.

---

## Directory

Browse your team and contacts in one place:

- **Team** - All team members with their extensions. Tap to call or message.
- **Contacts** - Your phone's contacts. You'll need to allow Tonet access the first time. Tap to call directly using your business number.

Use the search bar to find anyone quickly.

---

## Profile

Manage your app settings:

- **Display Name** - Edit the name your team sees
- **Call From** - Choose which of your [phone numbers](/phones) to use for outbound calls. If you have multiple numbers, tap **Change** to pick a different one.
- **Permissions** - See which permissions are enabled. Tap any one to open your phone's settings.
- **Log Out** - Sign out of the app

---

## Push Notifications

The app sends push notifications for:

- **Incoming calls** - Even when the app is in the background
- **New text messages** - SMS and team messages
- **New voicemails** - When someone leaves a message

<Callout type="info">
  On Android, make sure battery optimization is disabled for Tonet. Some Android manufacturers aggressively kill background apps, which can prevent incoming call notifications. Check your device's battery settings if calls aren't coming through.
</Callout>

---

## Syncing with the Dashboard

Everything stays in sync. Calls you make from the app appear in [Call History](/analytics/call_logs) on the web dashboard. Messages sent from the app show up in [Chat](/chat). Contacts you add in the dashboard are available in the app. Changes to your [phone numbers](/phones), [groups](/groups), and routing settings take effect on all devices immediately.
