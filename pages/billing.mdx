# Billing

Tonet uses a prepaid balance model. You add funds to your account, and charges are deducted automatically based on your plan. No contracts, no surprise invoices.

Go to **Account > Billing** to manage your billing. Use the **Billing Month** dropdown to view charges and payments from any previous month.

## Account Information

Shows your business name and email address on file.

## Payment Method

Click **Manage Your Cards** to add a credit or debit card, update expiration dates, or remove cards. This opens a secure Authorize.net window - Tonet never sees or stores your full card number.

If you have multiple cards, swipe between them. The currently displayed card is the one used for top-ups.

---

## Topup

Top up manually or set up automatic recharging.

### Recharge

Enter the amount you want to add to your balance (minimum $15) and click **Add Funds**. The charge goes to your currently selected card.

If a payment is declined, you'll need to wait 30 seconds before retrying.

### Auto-Recharge

Toggle **Auto Recharge** on to automatically top up your account when your balance gets low. Two fields appear:

- **Balance Falls** - The threshold that triggers a recharge. When your balance drops to this amount, a top-up is initiated automatically.
- **Recharge To** - The amount your balance is brought back up to. Must be at least $10 more than the Balance Falls threshold.

Click **Confirm** to save your auto-recharge settings. You can toggle it off at any time.

<Image src="/images/billing/auto_recharge.png" alt="Auto-Recharge settings with Balance Falls and Recharge To fields" width={600} height={500} style={{borderRadius: '8px', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}}/>

<Callout type="info">
  Auto-recharge prevents service interruptions. If your balance hits zero, outbound calls and SMS stop working until you add funds. Setting a threshold like $5 with a recharge-to of $25 keeps your system running without manual intervention.
</Callout>

## Usage Summary

Your spending breakdown for the selected month, shown as a donut chart with totals per category:

| Category | What it covers |
|----------|---------------|
| **Users** | Daily per-user charge based on your plan |
| **Calls** | Inbound and outbound call minutes |
| **SMS** | Inbound and outbound text messages |
| **Phones** | New number purchases, toll-free numbers, and monthly number maintenance |
| **Storage** | Call recording storage |
| **Other** | Any charges that don't fit the above categories |

## Usage Details

Every individual charge on your account for the selected month, exportable to a spreadsheet.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Date and Time** | When the charge occurred |
| **Type** | Inbound Call, Outbound Call, Tollfree Call, SMS, User Day, Number Maintenance, New Number, New Toll-Free Number, Storage, Setup Fee, or Other |
| **Amount** | The dollar amount deducted |
| **Description** | Details like the phone number called or the user charged |

## Payment History

Every payment made to your account:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Date and Time** | When the payment was processed |
| **Amount** | The dollar amount charged to your card |
| **Status** | Whether the payment was approved or declined |
| **Method** | The payment method used |

Declined payments flash red briefly so you can spot issues quickly.

---

## How Billing Works

Charges are deducted from your prepaid balance as you go. What you're charged for depends on your plan - check [Usage Details](#usage-details) for a full breakdown at any time.

When your balance gets low, top up manually or let [auto-recharge](#auto-recharge) handle it. If your balance hits zero, outbound calls and SMS stop working until you add funds.
