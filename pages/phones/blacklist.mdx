# Blacklist

The Blacklist tab lets you block specific phone numbers from calling your business. Blocked callers hear a busy signal and cannot reach any of your lines.

Go to **Manage > [Phones](/phones)** and click the **Blacklist** tab.

<Image src="/images/phones/blacklist.png" alt="Blacklist tab showing blocked numbers with reasons" width={1400} height={500} style={{borderRadius: '8px', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}}/>

## Blacklist Table

The table shows all blocked numbers:

- **Number** - The blocked phone number
- **Reason** - Why this number was blocked (e.g. "Spam caller", "Robocall", "Telemarketer")
- **Remove from Blacklist** - Click the trash icon to unblock the number

## Adding a Number

1. Click the **+ Add Number to Blacklist** button (top right, orange)
2. Enter the phone number to block
3. Enter a reason (helps you remember why you blocked it later)
4. The number is blocked immediately across **all** your phone lines - the caller hears a busy signal on their next attempt

## Removing a Number

Find the number in the list and click the trash icon in the **Remove from Blacklist** column. The number is unblocked immediately and callers can reach you again.

<Callout type="info">
  The blacklist applies to all phone numbers on your account. You cannot block a number on just one line - it's all or nothing. If you're getting spam on a specific number, blocking the caller here stops them across your entire phone system.
</Callout>
