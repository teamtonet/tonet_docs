# Sign Up

Create your Tonet account and start making calls in minutes. No contracts, no commitments - just pick a plan, fill out the form, and you're in.

Visit [tonet.ai/signup](https://tonet.ai/signup) to get started.

<Image src="/images/sign_up/signup_form.png" alt="Tonet signup form showing plan selection, account details, and business location fields" width={600} height={750} style={{borderRadius: '8px', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}}/>

## 1. Choose a Plan

You'll pick a plan first:

| | Pay-as-you-go | Unlimited |
|---|---|---|
| **Calls** | Per-minute billing | Unlimited |
| **Texts** | Per-text billing | Unlimited |
| **Base fee** | Per user/month | Per user/month |
| **Billing** | Daily, active days only | Daily |
| **Best for** | Low-volume or seasonal businesses | Teams with consistent call volume |

Both plans include all features - [call recording](/analytics/call_logs), [voicemail transcription](/analytics/call_logs#voicemails), [auto attendant](/auto_attendant), [SMS](/chat), and everything else in the dashboard. The only difference is how you pay for usage.

For most businesses, **Unlimited** is the best value - predictable monthly costs with no surprises on your bill, no matter how many calls you make.

## 2. Fill Out the Registration Form

### Account Details

- **Contact Name** - Your full name
- **Business Name** - Your company name. This is your account name across the dashboard.
- **Industry** - Your business industry
- **Email** - Your login email. Make sure you have access - this is how you sign in.
- **Phone Number** - Your contact phone number
- **Password** - Minimum 8 characters

### Business Location

- **Time Zone** - Your business timezone. Your [call logs](/analytics/call_logs), [schedules](/schedule), and [calendar](/calendar) all use this timezone. You can change it later in [Account](/account) settings.
- **Country** - Your business country
- **Street Address** - Business street address
- **Apt / Suite / Etc** - Optional unit or suite number
- **City** - Business city
- **State** - Required for US addresses
- **Zip Code / Postal Code** - Postal code

### Additional Information

- **How Did You Hear About Us** - Select from the dropdown (Facebook, LinkedIn, Reddit, X/Twitter, Google, or Other)
- **Promo Code** - Optional. Enter a promo code if you have one for a discount.

## 3. Create Your Account

Click **Create Account** to submit. If anything is missing, you'll see a message on the field that needs fixing.

Once you're registered, you're automatically signed in and taken straight to the dashboard.

---

## Activate Your Account

Before you can start making calls, you'll need to add a card and make a small deposit.

1. Go to [Billing](/billing) in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Card** to add your credit or debit card
3. Make your first top-up (minimum $20)

<Callout type="info">
  After your first payment, your phone system is set up automatically within seconds - no waiting for approval.
</Callout>

## Set Up Your Phone System

Once your account is active:

1. **Get a phone number** - Go to [Phones](/phones) and click **Purchase New Number** to browse local and toll-free numbers. Pick a number and it's yours instantly.
2. **Add team members** - Your admin account is created automatically. Go to [Users](/users) to invite your team. Each user gets their own extension and login.
3. **Start calling** - Open the [dialpad](/index#dialpad) from the sidebar and make your first call right from the browser.

From there, set up [groups](/groups) for team ring strategies, create an [auto attendant](/auto_attendant) for professional call routing, configure [schedules](/schedule) for business hours, and explore [SMS](/chat) messaging - all included with your account.
