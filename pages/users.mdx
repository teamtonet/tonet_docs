# Users

Each person on your team is a user. A user has an extension, a sign-in, and the numbers they can call from.

Owners, managers and admins manage users in **Manage › Users**.

## Add a person

1. Go to **Manage › Users** and click **Add**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Full name**
   - **Email address**: they sign in with it.
   - **Extension**: Tonet suggests the next free one, starting at 101.
   - **Outbound Numbers**: the numbers they can call and text from. Pick at least one.
   - **User Role**: see [Roles](#roles).
3. Click **Create User**.

Tonet emails them a link to set their password. Then they can sign in at [dashboard.tonet.ai](https://dashboard.tonet.ai).

<Callout type="info">
  Each person costs $10 or $15 a month, depending on your plan, charged by the day. The first day is charged when you add them, so your balance needs at least 33¢. Delete the person and the daily charge stops.
</Callout>

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything. The person who signed up. The owner can't be changed or deleted. |
| **Manager** | Everything, including **Billing** and **Account**. Manages everyone except the owner. |
| **Administrator** | Phones, menus, schedules, calendar, users and teams. No Billing or Account. Can add admins and regular users. |
| **Regular User** | Makes and takes calls, texts, chats and uses contacts. Sees their own calls and their teams' calls. |

## Change a role

1. In **Manage › Users**, open the person's **⋯** menu and click **View**.
2. Under **Role**, pick the new role.

You can't change your own role or the owner's.

## Change what a person can use

- **Which numbers they call and text from:** open the number in [Phones](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number) and use its **Permissions** tab.
- **Which teams they're on:** see [Teams](/groups).
- **Their display name:** each person can change their own in [Profile](/profile).

A person's email and extension can't be changed in the dashboard. Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) if you need that.

## Set up a desk phone

Each person has **SIP Information** for connecting a desk phone: a user name, password, domain and STUN server.

1. Open the person's **⋯** menu and click **View**.
2. Copy the details from the **SIP Information** box.

Owners and managers can see this box for anyone. See [IP Phones](/ip_phones) for setup steps.

## Delete a person

1. Open the person's **⋯** menu and click **Delete**.
2. Tonet checks where calls go. If numbers, menus, schedules, calendar events or teams still send calls to this person, it lists them. Change those first.
3. Click **Delete user**.

Their daily charge stops. Their past calls stay in **Calls**. Only support can bring back a deleted user.

You can't delete yourself or the owner.

## Find people

The list shows each person's status, extension, and the device they're on. Use the tabs **All**, **Online**, **Break** and **Offline**, or **Search users**. Switch between cards and a list with the view buttons at the top.
