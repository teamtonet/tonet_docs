# Teams

A team is a few people who share calls, like "Sales" or "Front desk". Send a call to the team and it rings them. Each team also gets its own extension and a shared voicemail box.

Owners, managers and admins manage teams in **Manage › Teams**. Changes save as you make them.

## Make a team

1. Go to **Manage › Teams** and click **New team**.
2. Type a name and click **Create team**.
3. Click **Add members**, find each person, and tick them.
4. Choose how calls ring: **Ring together** or **Queue**.

On a wide screen you can also drag people from the row at the top onto a team.

## How calls ring

| Choice | What callers get |
|---|---|
| **Ring together** | Everyone on the team rings at once. The first to answer takes the call. |
| **Queue** | Callers hold until someone on the team is free. |

## Send calls to a team

A team only gets calls when something sends them there. Pick **Teams** in any of these:

- A number's **Go To**: [Phones](/phones#choose-where-calls-go)
- A key in a [menu](/auto_attendant), like "Press 1 for sales"
- A route in a [schedule](/schedule)
- A [calendar](/calendar) event

The team's card lists these under **Gets calls from**. If it says **Nothing sends calls here yet**, no calls reach the team.

## What a team's card shows

- How many members are online
- Its hours, taken from the schedules that send calls to it
- **Shared voicemail**, and the team's extension
- **Gets calls from**: the numbers and menus that send calls to it
- This week's calls to the team, how many were missed, and calls placed by members
- **Team chat**: opens a chat with everyone on the team

## Change a team

- **Rename:** click the name, type, and press Enter.
- **Members:** click **Edit members**.
- **Ringing:** switch between **Ring together** and **Queue**.

## Team voicemail

Team members find the team's voicemails on the **Calls** page. Use the **Voicemail** filter.

To change the team's greeting, go to **Calls**, open the **⋯** menu, click **Voicemail greeting and email**, and pick the team's mailbox.

## Delete a team

1. Open the team's **⋯** menu and click **Delete team**.
2. If calls still reach the team, Tonet lists where from. Change those first, then click **Check again**.
3. Click **Delete**. You have 5 seconds to click **Undo**.
