Sign in at [dashboard.tonet.ai/login](https://dashboard.tonet.ai/login). Use the same email in the dashboard, the [desktop app](/app) and the [phone app](/phone-app). New to Tonet? See [Sign up](/sign_up).

## Ways to sign in

| Way | How |
|---|---|
| **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** | Pick the account with your Tonet email. Work, school and personal Microsoft accounts work. |
| **Email me a sign-in link** | No password needed. See below. |
| Password | Type your **Email Address** and **Password**, then click **Log In**. |

The Google and Microsoft buttons show only when available.

### Sign in with an emailed link or code

1. Type your **Email Address** and click **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. The page says **Check your email**. Open the email "Your Tonet sign-in link".
3. Click **Sign in to Tonet** in the email. Or type its code in **6-digit code** and click **Sign in**.

- The link and the code each work once, for 15 minutes.
- The link signs in the browser that opens it. Email open on your phone? Type the code on your computer.
- No email? Check spam. After 30 seconds, click **send it again**. Nothing is sent to an email with no Tonet login.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email Address** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email "Reset Your Tonet Password". Its link works once, for 2 hours.
4. Type a **New Password**, then type it again in **Confirm Password**. Use at least 12 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
5. Click **Reset Password**. Tonet signs you in.

Never had a password? These steps set one. The phone app needs it.

If the page says **This link no longer works**, click **Send me a new link**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Resetting your password signs you out everywhere else: other browsers, the desktop app and the phone app. Sign in there again with the new password.
</Callout>

New team members set their first password from their welcome email. Its link works for 10 days.

## How long you stay signed in

| Where | Signed in for |
|---|---|
| A browser or the desktop app | 30 days |
| The phone app | 1 year |

It counts from when you signed in, so you sign in again then, even if you use Tonet daily.

Each browser and app you sign in on is listed in [Signed-in devices](/profile#see-where-youre-signed-in). Your calls ring on [up to 3 of them](/phone-app#one-line-on-up-to-three-devices) at once.

## Sign out

Click your picture at the top right, then **Logout**. Only this browser signs out. For other devices, use [Signed-in devices](/profile#see-where-youre-signed-in).

## Change your password

1. Open **Profile › Security**. Under **Change Password**, type your **Current Password**.
2. Type a **New Password** of at least 12 characters, then type it again in **Confirm New Password**.
3. Click **Update Password**.

Your other devices stay signed in. Sign out any you don't recognize under **Signed-in devices**.

If it says **Your login has no password yet, so there's nothing to change.**, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.

## Sign in to the desktop app

The [desktop app](/app) opens the same sign-in page. With an emailed sign-in, type the code in the app: the link signs in your browser instead.

With Google or Microsoft, you finish in your web browser:

1. Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**. The app says **Finish in your browser** and shows a code.
2. Sign in to Google or Microsoft in the browser that opens.
3. The browser asks **Sign in to the Tonet app?** If it shows the same code as the app, click **Confirm**.
4. The browser says **You're signed in**, and Tonet comes back to the front.

Finish on the same computer and network as the app. Didn't start a sign-in there? Click **Cancel**.

## Sign in to the phone app

The [phone app](/phone-app#sign-in) takes your **Email** and **Password** only. Tap **Login**. No password yet? Tap **Forgot password?** in the app to set one.

## If you can't sign in

| The page says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Incorrect email or password** | Check the email. No password? Use Google, Microsoft or an emailed link, or set one. |
| **No Tonet account uses … yet** | That Google or Microsoft account has another email. Sign in another way. **Create account** starts a new account. |
| **That code isn't right. Use the one in the latest email from Tonet.** | Type the code from the newest email. |
| **Too many wrong codes** or **That code has expired or was already used** | Click **send it again**. |
| **That sign-in link expired or was already used** | Type the email's code where you asked for it, or send a new link. |
| **The provider didn't confirm the email on that account** … or **Google and Microsoft sign-in aren't available right now** | Use an emailed link or your password. |
| **That sign-in took too long. Please try again.** | Start again. |
| **Finish signing in with a browser on the same computer and network as the Tonet app.** | Start again, and finish on the app's computer. |
| **User Deactivated. Contact your Admin** or **Your user has been deactivated** | Your login is turned off. Ask an owner or manager on your account. |
| **Account is suspended** or **This account is suspended. Contact support.** | Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai). |
| **An error occurred. Please contact support.** | Too many tries can cause it. Wait a few minutes, then try again. |

An account locked because its balance reached $0 can still sign in. Add funds in [Billing](/billing).
