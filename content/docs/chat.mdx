**Messages** holds two kinds of conversations: texts with customers, sent from your business numbers, and team chat with the people on your account. Open **Messages** in the left menu.

- **Texts** go to your customers' phones from your business numbers. Everyone who can use a number sees its texts and can answer them. Texts cost 3¢ each.
- **Team chat** stays inside your Tonet account. Chat with one person or several.

## Send a text

1. In **Messages**, click **New**.
2. Switch to **SMS**.
3. Next to **From:**, pick the number to text from.
4. Click **Add recipient**, type the number or pick a recent caller, and click **Add**.
5. Type your message and press **Enter**.

A text goes to one number. Group texts aren't supported.

In a text conversation, the number you're texting from shows next to the message box. Pick another number there and your next text starts a separate conversation from that number.

You can also text from a [contact's page](/contacts).

## Can't send texts? Pending carrier verification

US carriers only deliver business texts from registered numbers (A2P 10DLC). Until your number is approved, Tonet shows:

| You see | Where | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending carrier verification — texts won't be delivered** | Next to **From:** on a new text | You can use a number, but none is approved for texting yet |
| **Pending Carrier Verification** | The number button next to the message box | Same as above |
| **No Text Numbers** | The number button next to the message box | No number is assigned to you for texting |

- Hover the warning to read why. Owners, managers and admins can click it to open **SMS Registration** and [register the number](/phones/sms-registration).
- While you wait, calls, voicemail and team chat work as usual, and texts customers send you still arrive.
- **No Text Numbers?** Ask an owner, manager or admin to [give you the number](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number).

## Start a team chat

1. Click **New**.
2. In **Search Users**, find each person and click them.
3. Type your message and press **Enter**.

Or, in the **Team** list in the left menu, hover a person and click the message icon.

## Write notes to yourself

**Notes** is a chat with only you in it. Use it for reminders, links and files. Your team can't see it.

- In the **Team** list in the left menu, you're at the top, marked **(you)**. Hover your name and click the message icon (**Notes to yourself**).
- Or click your name and click **Notes**.

Once you write something, **Notes** shows in your conversation list like any other chat.

## Write and send

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Send | Press **Enter** |
| Start a new line | Press **Shift+Enter** |
| Add an emoji or GIF | Click the smiley button. It has **Emoji** and **GIFs** tabs. |
| Attach files | Click the picture or paper-clip button, paste, or drag files in |
| Format a team chat message | Click **Format** for bold, italic, lists, quotes and code |
| Answer one message | Hover it and click **Reply** |

A message you haven't sent stays in its box, in this browser, when you switch conversations.

## File limits

**Texts:**

- Up to 10 files and 5 MB in total per text.
- Each picture up to 2 MB, each video up to 3 MB, each audio file up to 2 MB, and each PDF or other file up to 1 MB.
- Use JPEG, PNG or GIF pictures, MP4 video, MP3 or OGG audio, or PDF.

**Team chat:**

- Pictures up to 10 MB each, documents up to 25 MB, audio and video up to 50 MB.
- Pictures, PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, text and CSV files, audio and video.

## Did my text arrive?

Under each text you send, an icon shows how far it got. Hover it for the word.

| Shows | Means |
|---|---|
| **Queued** or **Sending** | On its way |
| **Sent** | Handed to the carrier |
| **Delivered** | The carrier delivered it |
| **Not delivered** | It didn't arrive. The reason shows next to it, for example **Recipient replied STOP and is unsubscribed**. |

Texts never show **Seen**.

If a message couldn't be sent at all, it's marked **Not sent**, or says why. Click **Retry** to send it again, or **Discard** to remove it. See [Why texts don't deliver](/phones/sms-registration/not-delivered).

## See who read your team chat message

Under your latest message, **Seen** shows once the other person has read it. In a chat with several people, it says **Seen by** and their names. Hover it for the times.

While a teammate types, you see "… is typing".

## Edit or delete a message

You can change your own team chat messages. Texts can't be edited or unsent.

**Edit:**

1. Hover your message and click **Edit** (the pencil).
2. Change the text in the box at the bottom. It says **Editing message**.
3. Press **Enter**. Press **Esc** or click **Cancel** to stop.

Everyone sees the message marked **edited**. Shortcut: press the up arrow in an empty message box to edit your last message.

**Delete:**

1. Hover your message and click **Delete** (the bin). It goes right away.
2. A notice says **Message deleted**. Click **Undo** within 5 seconds to bring it back, or **Delete now** to skip the wait.

After that, everyone sees **Message deleted** in its place.

## React to a message

Hover a message and click **React**. Pick an emoji, or click **+** for more. Click a reaction under the message to add yours or take it back. Hover it to see who reacted.

Reactions on a text are only seen by your team, never by the customer.

## Save a message and find it later

1. Hover the message and click **Save** (the star). It's marked **★ Saved**.
2. To add a note, click the star again. Your note and your saved messages are only seen by you.

Find everything you saved under **Saved**, above your conversation list. Click **Open in chat** to jump to the message, or **Unsave** to remove it.

## Tag messages with #tags

Type a word with **#** in any message, like #quote or #urgent. Then:

- Click a #tag in a message to see the other messages in that conversation with the same tag.
- Open **#Tags** above your conversation list to see all your tags, most used first. Pick more than one to see messages with any of them.

## The Details panel

Click **Details** at the top of a conversation.

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **About** | Who's in the conversation. For a text: their number, where it's from and the time there, **Call** and **Contact** buttons, which of your numbers you're texting from, and when the conversation started. |
| **Files** | Every photo, video, document, audio file and link sent in the conversation. Filter by type or search. **Open files full size** shows pictures, videos and PDFs across the chat, just for you. |
| **Saved** | Messages you saved in this conversation |
| **#Tags** | Tags used in this conversation |

## Find a conversation

- **Search numbers, names or messages** at the top of the list.
- **SMS** and **DM** show or hide texts and team chats. The number in brackets is how many there are.
- **My numbers** shows texts for only some of your numbers. It appears when you can use more than one.
- **Chats**, **Saved** and **#Tags** switch what the list shows.

## Who sees which messages

| Conversation | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Texts on a number | Everyone who can use that number. Each reply shows who sent it. Owners, managers and admins choose who can use a number in [Phones](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number). |
| Team chat | Only the people in it |
| **Notes** | Only you |
