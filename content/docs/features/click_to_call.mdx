- **Click to call:** click a phone link (a `tel:` link) on a web page and Tonet calls it.
- **Highlight to dial:** a small Windows program. Highlight any number on your screen and press a key to call it.

Both place the call from the Tonet dashboard. Keep [dashboard.tonet.ai](https://dashboard.tonet.ai) open and signed in, in the same browser.

## Turn on click to call

1. Sign in at [dashboard.tonet.ai](https://dashboard.tonet.ai).
2. Click your picture at the top right, then **Enable click to call**.
3. When your browser asks, allow Tonet to open phone links.

Now click any phone link. A tab opens for a moment, passes the number to your Tonet tab, and closes. The call starts right away.

## If a phone link doesn't call

- Check that a Tonet tab is open and signed in, in the same browser. Without one, the link's tab closes after a second and nothing is dialed.
- Check that your browser lets Tonet open phone links. In Chrome, go to **Settings › Privacy and security › Site settings**, open **Protocol handlers**, and make sure Tonet is allowed.

## Highlight to dial (Windows)

1. [Download Highlight to Call](/files/tonet.ai_highlight-to-call.exe). It's a Windows program (.exe). There's no Mac or Linux version.
2. Open it. A small window called **Highlight to Call by tonet.ai** opens and stays on top.
3. Highlight a phone number anywhere and press **F6**. Tonet calls it.

To use a different key, click the **Hotkey** box, press the key you want, and click **Set Hotkey**.

- Leave the window open. You can minimize it. Closing it quits the program.
- It dials the digits in what you highlight. If there are none, it says **No numbers found in selection**.
- It opens the call in your default browser, so keep Tonet open and signed in there.
