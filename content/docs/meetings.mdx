Meetings are voice meetings in the dashboard. Talk, share your screen, chat, and read what's said as it's said. People from outside can join from their browser or by phone. There's no camera video.

Everyone on your account can use **Meetings** in the sidebar.

## Start or schedule a meeting

1. Go to **Meetings** and click **Start now** or **Schedule**.
2. Type a **Title**.
3. In **People**, pick teammates, or type an email address or phone number and pick it from the list.
4. To schedule, set the **Date**, **Start** and **End**, then **Repeats** (**Doesn’t repeat**, **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**) and **Remind everyone**.
5. Click **Start meeting** or **Schedule meeting**.

| Invited | Start now | Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| Teammates | A **Join** card on any dashboard page, once you join | A calendar invite by email, a notice in the bell, and a **Join** card at reminder time |
| Email address | An email with the link | A calendar invite with the link and how to call in |
| Phone number | A text with the link | A text, and another just before it starts |

A meeting holds up to 15 people, guests included. Your account can invite up to 100 outside people a day. Texts to guests are charged like any text you send.

On a task, **Add meeting** schedules one about it.

## The Meetings page

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Happening now** | Live meetings you're part of, with **Join** |
| **Upcoming** | Your scheduled meetings, the desk phone number, **Copy link**, and **⋯** to cancel |
| **Past** | Length, who came, and Tonet AI's wrap-up. **Didn’t happen** meetings have **Reschedule**. |

You can cancel only before a meeting starts. For a repeating one, pick **Cancel this one** or **Cancel this and later ones**.

## Join a meeting

1. Open the meeting from **Meetings**, the **Join** card, or its link.
2. Tick **Join muted** if you like, and click **Join meeting**.

You talk through your Tonet softphone in the browser or the [desktop app](/app), so it has to be connected. From a desk phone, dial the number shown on the meeting, like 860030001.

A scheduled meeting opens 15 minutes before its start and closes at its end, unless people are still in. Anyone on your account with the link can join.

You can visit other pages while you're in. The top bar shows the meeting, with **Back**.

## In the meeting

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mute** | Mutes your microphone |
| **Share screen** | Shares a screen, window or tab. **Present instead** takes over from whoever is sharing. |
| **Add people** | Invites more people. See [below](#invite-people-from-outside). |
| **Tonet AI** | Switches Tonet AI on or off |
| **Leave** | Hangs up. You can come back while it's open. |
| **End for everyone** | Ends it for all. Nobody can rejoin. |

**What’s said** shows each person's words with their name and time, the chat, and Tonet AI's notes beside the line they're about. Type below it to message everyone.

While someone shares, their screen fills the room. **Chat** and **Transcript** open a side panel with **Chat**, **Action items** and **Transcript** tabs.

Words are written down from each person's own microphone in the browser or app. People on a desk phone or calling in are heard but not written down.

**Action items:** anyone in the meeting can add one, pick who does it, or remove one with ×. With Tonet AI on, it adds what you agree on.

**Meters** above **What’s said** show **Mixed up on a fact**, **Agreeing** and **Decision made**. Click **Meter** to add your own yes/no question.

## Invite people from outside

Click **Add people**. Under **Guests from outside**:

- **Copy** copies the guest link. **Copy invitation** copies the link and call-in details.
- **Lock the meeting** stops new guests joining, by link or by phone.
- **New link** replaces the guest link. The old one stops working.
- **Bring a call in** moves a call you're on into the meeting. Click **Bring in**.

Guests open the link, type **Your name**, and click **Join meeting**. Then they wait. You'll see "… is waiting to join" with **Let in** and **Not now**, on any dashboard page if you host or were invited.

<Callout type="info">
  Guests see what's said and typed from when they joined, never before. They can message everyone and ask Tonet AI, but can't see a shared screen.
</Callout>

### Let guests call in by phone

Go to **Manage › Phones**, click a number, open **Route & settings**, and in **Go To** pick **Meeting call-in** under **Features**. Or send a [phone menu](/auto_attendant) key there. Callers enter the meeting's 6-digit code, then wait to be let in.

## Tonet AI in a meeting

Tonet AI comes on by itself while your account uses AI. Anyone in the room can switch it with **Tonet AI**, and the chat says who did.

- **Ask it:** type @Tonet, or start a sentence with "Tonet," out loud. Asking turns it on.
- **Unasked**, it speaks up only with something it looked up, with numbered sources. Click **Reply**, **Useful**, **Speak less** or **Not useful** on its messages.
- **Guide it**, like "@Tonet keep answers short". **Remember for our future meetings** keeps that.
- **Off means off.** It never reads what was said while it was off.
- **With a guest in,** it uses only what was said since they joined, and nothing about your business.

It's free: Tonet pays for it. It still counts toward your daily AI limits, and it can't join when AI is off for your account. See [What Tonet AI does](/ai).

## After the meeting

When everyone has left for 2 minutes, or someone clicks **End for everyone**, Tonet AI posts a **Wrap-up**: **DECIDED**, **WHO DOES WHAT** and **STILL OPEN**. This needs Tonet AI on and enough said while it was on. Ask "@Tonet wrap up" for one sooner.

Click **Make 1 task** (or **Make 3 tasks**) to turn open items into tasks, each for its person, on [Overview](/overview#keep-track-of-your-tasks).

Open a meeting under **Past** to read its wrap-up and everything said and typed. Meetings aren't recorded as audio.

## Who can do what

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Start, schedule and join meetings | Everyone |
| Let guests in | Anyone on your account |
| Switch Tonet AI, add action items, lock the meeting | Anyone in the meeting |
| Cancel, **End for everyone**, **New link** | The host, owners and managers |
| Add meters | Owners, managers and admins |
| Read the transcript, chat and wrap-up, make tasks | The host, invitees and anyone who joined. Not other owners or managers. |
