A calendar event sends a number's calls somewhere else for a set time, like a holiday, a closure or a team day out. While the event is on, it beats the number's [schedule](/schedule) and **Go To**.

Owners, managers and admins manage events in **Manage › Calendar**.

## Add an event

1. Go to **Manage › Calendar** and click **New Event**. Or click a day on the calendar, or drag across several days, to start an event with those dates.
2. In **Add Event**, fill in:
   - **Title**, like "Thanksgiving"
   - **Destination**: where calls go during the event. Pick from **Users**, **Teams**, **IVRs** (menus), **Voicemail** or **Features**.
   - **Start date** and **End date**, each with a time
3. Click **Confirm**.

## Turn the event on for a number

An event changes nothing until a number picks it.

1. Go to **Manage › Phones** and click the number.
2. Open the **Route & settings** tab.
3. In **Calendars**, tick the event. A number can use several events.
4. Click **Save Settings**.

New events aren't added to numbers on their own. Repeat these steps on every number that should follow the event.

## How Tonet picks where a call goes

When a call comes in, Tonet checks in this order:

1. An event picked in the number's **Calendars** that is happening now
2. The number's [schedule](/schedule)
3. **Go To**

## Change the view

Use **View** at the top: **Month**, **Week**, **Day** or **Agenda** (a list of the month's events). The arrows next to it move back and forward.

## Change or delete an event

- **Change it:** click the event, make your changes in **Edit Event**, and click **Confirm**.
- **Delete it:** click the event, then click the bin at the bottom left of **Edit Event**. It's deleted at once, with no undo, and taken off every number that used it.

## Example: a holiday

1. Make a "Holiday" [menu](/auto_attendant) with your holiday greeting. Need words? Copy a [holiday script](/guides/menu-and-voicemail-scripts#holiday-greeting-scripts).
2. Click **New Event**:
   - **Title:** Christmas
   - **Destination:** **IVRs** › Holiday
   - **Start date:** Dec 24, 5:00 PM
   - **End date:** Dec 26, 8:00 AM
3. Click **Confirm**.
4. On each number, tick "Christmas" in **Calendars** and click **Save Settings**.

From Christmas Eve at 5 PM until 8 AM on Dec 26, callers hear your holiday menu. Then your usual hours apply again.

<Callout type="info">
  **Busy** under **Features** gives callers a busy signal and nothing else. A short holiday greeting tells them when you're back and how to leave a message.
</Callout>
