You make and take calls right in the Tonet dashboard or the [desktop app](/app). Click the dialpad icon in the top bar, type a number or a name, and click **Call**. The first time, your browser asks to use your microphone: allow it.

## Make a call

1. Click the dialpad icon in the top bar. The **Phone** panel opens.
2. Under **From**, pick the number people will see.
3. In **Number or name**, type a phone number, or start typing a contact's or teammate's name and pick them.
4. Click **Call**.

- With the box empty, the panel suggests people to call: missed calls, recent calls and texts, and upcoming tasks.
- **From** stays your outgoing number until you change it, for calls from your desk phone too.
- **Text** starts a text to the number. **Add contact** saves it, or **Edit contact** if it's already saved.
- Press **Esc** to close the panel.

You can also call with **Call back** on the [Calls](/analytics/call_logs) page, **Call** on a [call's page](/calls/call-page) or a contact's page, or [from any app](/features/click_to_call).

<Callout type="info">
  **From** says **No outgoing number**? Nobody has given you a number to call from. Owners, managers and admins: tick yourself on the number's **Permissions** tab ([Phones](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number)). Regular users: ask an admin. Calls to a teammate's extension work without one.
</Callout>

## Use the keypad

Click **Keypad** in the Phone panel. Before a call, the keys type the number. During a call, they send tones, for menus like "press 1". With two calls up, pick which call gets the tones.

## Answer, decline or block a call

A ringing call shows as a card at the bottom left. It shows the line they called (**To** …), who's calling (their contact or caller ID name, the number, where it's from and the time there), their tags, and what they're **Probably calling about** when Tonet knows. A red **Robocaller** line shows if an earlier call from that number was a robocall.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Answer** | Takes the call |
| **Decline** | Turns the call down |
| **Block** | Ends the call and blocks the number for your whole account. Its calls and texts won't reach anyone. Click **Undo** within 8 seconds to take it back. |

Anyone on the account can block. See [Blocked numbers](/phones/blacklist) for the list. Click **Compact** for a smaller ringing card, **Expand** for the full one.

To get a browser notification when a call rings while you're in another tab or app, turn on **Incoming Calls** in [Profile](/profile) › **Notifications**.

## Mute, hold, transfer or end a call

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mute** | Turns your microphone off. **Unmute** turns it back on. |
| **Hold** | Puts the call on hold. The timer reads **On hold**. **Resume** brings it back. |
| **Transfer** | Sends the call to someone else. See below. |
| **End** | Hangs up |

The card also shows your line, the call timer, and two lines that move when **Your mic** and the **Caller** make sound. Drag the card by its handle to move it.

If your internet drops for a moment, the card says **Reconnecting…**, then **Connection back** or **Connection lost**.

## Transfer a call

1. Click **Transfer**.
2. In **Transfer to**, pick a person, a team or a contact, or type any phone number.
3. Click **Transfer**.

The call goes straight to them and ends on your side. You don't talk to them first.

## Take a second call

A second call rings on its own card. When two calls are connected and neither is on hold, the card warns **2 calls can hear you**:

- **Hold the other** puts the other call on hold.
- **Merge** joins them, so everyone hears each other.

The call you last clicked gets the full card. The others shrink to the caller and their buttons.

## Start a conference call

1. With two or more calls, click **Conference calls** in the Phone panel.
2. Click **Start conference**.

Everyone on your calls hears each other. If anyone hangs up, the conference ends and your other calls stay connected to you, each on its own. Change your microphone before or after a conference, not during one.

## When a call doesn't go through

A card says what happened and offers to try again. It stays until you click its button or **Dismiss**. A missed incoming call goes to the [bell](#the-bell) instead.

| The card says | What happened | Button |
|---|---|---|
| **Busy** | The line is busy. | **Call again** |
| **No answer** | No one answered. | **Call again** |
| **Call declined** | The call was declined. | **Call again** |
| **Number unavailable** | The number can't be reached right now. | **Try again** |
| **Couldn't connect** | The call couldn't connect. Check your internet connection. | **Try again** |
| **Microphone unavailable** | Tonet couldn't use your microphone. Check it's plugged in and allowed. | **Try again** |
| **Call didn't go through** | The call couldn't be completed. The carrier's code shows below, like 503 Service Unavailable. | **Try again** |
| **Call dropped** | Your internet connection was lost during the call. | **Call back** |
| **No sound on that call** | No sound reached you. Your network may be blocking call audio. | **Call back** |

## Choose your microphone and speaker

Click **Devices** at the top of the Phone panel, or the settings icon on a call's card.

- **Microphone**: pick one, click **Test microphone** and speak to see the level.
- **Speaker / headphones**: pick one and click **Test speaker** to hear a sound.

Changes apply to the call you're on and to later calls in this browser. Ringing and notification sounds follow your computer's sound settings.

<Callout type="info">
  Blocked the microphone by mistake? Allow it for dashboard.tonet.ai in your browser's site settings, then try again.
</Callout>

## Check your connection

The signal bars in the top bar show how quickly your computer reaches Tonet's call server. Hover over them for the exact time.

| Bars | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green, full | Good: 800 ms or less there and back |
| Yellow, 2 bars | Long delay: people may talk over each other |
| Red, 1 bar | Very long delay, over 1.5 seconds: conversations will be hard |
| Red, crossed out | Calls can't connect from this network |

Click the bars to open **Network Quality**, then click **Run Test** for a full check.

## If calls ring in another tab

- One tab per browser holds your phone. Other tabs show **Phone is on in another tab**. Click **Use here** to move it. Calling from a tab moves it too.
- Your calls ring on up to 3 devices at once, counting browsers and desktop apps. When all 3 are taken, the top bar says **Not ringing here · your line is full**. Click **Fix**, then **Free up** next to a device, or **Reconnect** to clear the line.

## Your call card and Notes

Choose what your call card shows: click the checklist icon on a call (**Choose what your call card shows**), or go to [Profile](/profile) › **Call card**. Pick from tags, what they're probably calling about, your business's fields, mic and caller levels, where they are and their time, open work, last conversations and contact details.

- To hide one part, hover over it on the card and click its ×. **Undo** brings it back.
- Your choice is saved to your account. It changes only your screen, in the browser and the desktop app.
- **Default** puts the card back the way it started.

**Notes** on a call's card opens its review: jot down what matters and tag the call while you talk. Your notes stay with the call after you hang up. See [After-call review](/calls/after-call-review).

## The bell

The bell in the top bar holds the last 7 days: missed calls to you or your teams, voicemails, texts, team messages, comments on calls and task reminders. Its red number counts what you haven't read, plus every unheard voicemail.

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Everything |
| **Unread** | What you haven't opened |
| **Needs attention** | Customers waiting on your team: a text nobody answered, or a new voicemail |

- Click an item to open it. **Mark all read** marks everything read.
- In **Needs attention**, use **Reply**, **Call back** or **Listen**. A text clears by itself once someone on your team replies or has an answered call with that customer. A voicemail stays until you click **Listen**, ✓ (handled) or ✕.
- **Dismiss all** empties the list. It doesn't delete any call, text or voicemail.
- Don't want **Needs attention**? Open the bell's **⋯** menu and turn off **Show "Needs attention"**.

Read, handled and dismissed items are remembered in this browser.
