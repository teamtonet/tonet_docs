|---|---|
| **No Service** on a Yealink phone's screen | [No Service](#no-service) |

## No Service

A Yealink phone shows **No Service** when no line on it is registered. Yealink gives two causes: the phone isn't on the network, or its line failed to register.

1. Press **OK** on the phone. If the status screen shows no IP address, the phone isn't on your network. Check the network cable at both ends, or the [Wi-Fi](/ip_phones/yealink/t54w#connect-the-t54w-to-wi-fi) on a T54W.
2. If it shows an IP address, the line didn't register. Go to [Register Failed](#register-failed).

## Register Failed

The phone is on your network, but Tonet's phone server didn't accept its line. Check these in order.

**1. The details match SIP Information exactly.** Copy them again from **Manage › Users** › the person › **SIP Information** ([how](/ip_phones#find-your-sip-details)):

- The user name, and the authentication ID if the phone has one, are the **User Name**: a `u` and a number, like `u12345`. Not the extension, not an email address.
- The password is the **Password**. Use the copy button, or click the eye to check what you typed.
- The server includes the port. Grandstream phones take the whole **Domain**. On a Yealink, the part before the colon goes in **Server Host** and the number after it in **Port**.

**2. Domain shows `None:None`.** Your account's phone server isn't set up. Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).

**3. SIP ALG is on in your router.** It can change the phone's traffic so the line can't register. [Turn off SIP ALG](/ip_phones/sip-alg).

**4. A firewall blocks the phone.** Yealink lists firewalls and access lists between the phone and the server as a cause. Let the phone reach the server and port in **Domain**.

**5. Settings left from another provider.** A phone from another provider can keep that provider's settings, or run its firmware. Factory reset the phone, then enter only Tonet's details. Your phone's guide has the steps.

**6. Old firmware.** Yealink's checklist for failed registration includes updating the firmware. Your phone's guide shows how.

Then restart the phone and check its status again:

| Phone | Where to see if it registered |
|---|---|
| Yealink | Web page: **Account › Register › Register Status** says **Registered**. The line's icon is green. |
| Grandstream GRP2612, GXP2130, GXP2170 | Web page: **Status › Account Status › SIP Registration** |
| Grandstream HT801, HT802 | Web page: **Status › Port Status › Registration** says **Registered**. The **Phone** light is on. |

## One-way audio or no audio

The call connects, but one side hears nothing, or nobody does. Yealink and Grandstream both point to the network first.

1. **Turn on STUN in the phone.** Server `stun.tonet.ai`, port `3478`, and set the line's NAT setting to **STUN**. Your [phone's guide](/ip_phones#setup-guides) has the steps.
2. **Turn off SIP ALG on your router.** See [Turn off SIP ALG](/ip_phones/sip-alg).
3. **Check firewalls.** Yealink says that when the server sends the audio and the phone still gets none, the cause is usually the network, like a firewall. Grandstream says to make sure firewall rules let the audio (RTP) through.
4. **Rule out the handset.** Try the call on speaker or a headset. If those work, the handset or its cord is faulty. Check the handset is plugged in and that the other person isn't on mute.
5. **Several Grandstream GRP or GXP phones on one router?** Keep **Use Random Port** set to **Yes**, the default. Grandstream says it's usually needed when several phones sit behind the same router. It's on the same page as **STUN Server**.

## Still stuck?

- In the dashboard, click **Report an issue** at the bottom of the left menu. Under **Where did it happen?**, pick **Desk phone**, describe it in **What happened?**, and click **Send report**. We reply by email.

Say which phone model you have and what its screen or status page shows.
