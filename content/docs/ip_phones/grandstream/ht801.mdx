The HT801 (one phone port) and HT802 (two phone ports) connect regular analog phones to Tonet. Each phone port signs in as one person's line.

The default web sign-in is **admin** / **admin**. On the V2 models, the password is printed on the sticker on the back of the unit.

<Callout type="info">
  Tonet doesn't support fax. Connect phones only.
</Callout>

**Which version do you have?** The HT801 V2 and HT802 V2 have **NET** printed by the network port, and their web page calls them **HT801V2** and **HT802V2**. Where the steps differ, this page says **V2**.

## What you need

- The adapter and its power adapter.
- A regular (analog) phone and its cord for each port you use.
- A network cable to your router.
- A computer on the same network.
- The [SIP details](/ip_phones#find-your-sip-details) of the person who'll use each phone: **User Name**, **Password** and **Domain**.

## Connect the adapter

1. Plug the phone into the phone port. On the HT802, the first phone goes in port **1** and the second in port **2**.
2. Connect the adapter's network port to your router.
3. Plug in the power last.

The **Power** light and the network light come on. The **Phone** light stays off until the line registers.

## Grandstream ATA default IP address

By default, the HT801 and HT802 get their IP address from your router (DHCP), so it depends on your network. To hear it:

1. Pick up the phone connected to the adapter.
2. Dial `***`. Wait for "Enter the menu option".
3. Press `02`. The adapter reads out its IP address.

## HT801, HT802 and V2 default password

| Model | Username | Password |
|---|---|---|
| HT801, HT802 | **admin** | **admin** |
| HT801 V2, HT802 V2 | **admin** | Printed on the sticker on the back of the unit |

The first time you sign in, the adapter makes you choose a new password: 8 to 20 characters, with at least one number, one uppercase letter and one lowercase letter. (On the first HT801 and HT802, this starts with firmware 1.0.53.3.)

1. On the computer, open a browser and type the adapter's IP address, like `192.168.1.50`. On a V2, type `https://` first, like `https://192.168.1.50`.
2. Sign in as **admin** with the default password.
3. Set a new password when asked, and write it down.

**Lost the new password?** [Factory reset](#factory-reset-the-ht801-or-ht802) the adapter. It goes back to its default password.

## Enter your Tonet line

1. Open the port's settings:
   - **HT801, HT802:** click the **FXS PORT** tab (on the HT802, **FXS PORT1** for the first phone).
   - **V2:** go to **Port Settings**, pick the FXS port (on the HT802 V2, **FXS PORT1** for the first phone), then **General Settings**.
2. Fill in:

| Field | Enter |
|---|---|
| **Account Active** | **Yes** (on V2, tick it) |
| **Primary SIP Server** | The whole **Domain**, including the colon and port, like `pbx.example.com:5080` |
| **SIP User ID** | The **User Name** from SIP Information |
| **Authenticate ID** | The **User Name** from SIP Information |
| **Authenticate Password** | The **Password** from SIP Information |
| **Name** | The person's name |
| **NAT Traversal** | **STUN** |

3. Click **Apply** at the bottom of the page. On V2, click **Save and Apply**.
4. Restart the adapter: unplug the power for a few seconds. Many port settings only take effect after a restart.

## Turn on STUN

You set **NAT Traversal** to **STUN** on the port page above. Now enter the STUN server:

- **HT801, HT802:** open the **ADVANCED SETTINGS** tab. In **STUN Server is**, enter `stun.tonet.ai:3478`. Click **Apply**.
- **V2:** go to **System Settings › Basic Settings › ATA Settings**. In **STUN Server**, enter `stun.tonet.ai:3478`. Click **Save and Apply**.

## Check that it registered

- On the web page, the **Status** page has a **Port Status** table. **Registration** should say **Registered** for your port. **Not Registered** means a setting is wrong.
- The **Phone** light is on when the line is registered, and off when it isn't.

A dial tone doesn't prove the line works: the adapter can play dial tone even when it isn't registered. Not registered? See [Register Failed](/ip_phones/troubleshooting#register-failed).

## Set up the second phone on an HT802

1. Plug the second phone into port **2**.
2. Repeat [Enter your Tonet line](#enter-your-tonet-line) on **FXS PORT2**, with the SIP details of the person who'll use that phone.
3. Check the **FXS 2** row in **Status › Port Status**, and the **Phone 2** light.

## Factory reset the HT801 or HT802

A factory reset erases every setting, including your lines, and brings back the default password.

**With the reset button:**

1. Unplug the network cable.
2. Push a pin into the reset hole and hold it for about 7 seconds.
3. Take out the pin.

**From the phone:**

1. Dial `***`, then `99`. Wait for the voice to say "reset".
2. Key in the adapter's MAC address, printed on the bottom of the unit. Numbers stay as they are. For letters, press: A = `22`, B = `222`, C = `2222`, D = `33`, E = `333`, F = `3333`. For example, `000b8200e395` is keyed as `0002228200333395`.
3. The adapter restarts and resets after about 15 seconds.

**From the web page:**

- **HT801, HT802:** on the **BASIC SETTINGS** tab, set **Reset Type** to **Full Reset** and click **Reset**.
- **V2:** under **Maintenance**, open **Restore Factory**, set **Reset Type** to **Full Reset** and click **Reset**.

An adapter locked by a previous provider may only restart when you press the reset button. Ask that provider to release it.

## Update the firmware

1. Download the firmware from [grandstream.com/support/firmware](https://www.grandstream.com/support/firmware), under **HandyTone ATA's**. In October 2026 the latest is 1.0.65.3 for the HT801 and HT802, and 1.0.15.3 for the V2 models.
2. Upload it:
   - **HT801, HT802:** on the **ADVANCED SETTINGS** tab, pick the file (`ht801fw.bin` or `ht802fw.bin`) and click **Upload Firmware**.
   - **V2:** go to **Maintenance › Upgrade**, open the **Firmware** tab, and under **Upgrade via Manual Upload**, upload the file (`ht80xv2fw.bin`).
3. Wait a few minutes. Don't unplug the adapter. Then check the version on the **Status** page.
