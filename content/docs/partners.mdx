The Tonet partner program pays you for sending businesses to Tonet. You don't need to use Tonet yourself. Make a free login, share your link, and earn $10 when each business first pays, then 10% of every card payment it makes, for as long as it stays.

**Where:** join at [dashboard.tonet.ai/partners](https://dashboard.tonet.ai/partners), the **Earn with Tonet** page. After that, your link, rewards and payouts are on [Refer & earn](/refer).

## What you earn

| Who | Gets | When |
|---|---|---|
| The business you refer | $10 of Tonet credit | After its first card payment goes through |
| You | $10 | When that first card payment goes through |
| You | 10% of each card payment it makes | For as long as it stays with Tonet |

For example, a business that adds $50 by card each month earns you $15 in its first month ($10 plus $5), then $5 a month: $70 in its first year.

- **Only card payments count.** Crypto top-ups, credits and promotions earn nothing.
- **Refunds come back off.** If a payment is refunded, voided or declined, its reward is taken back, $10 included. If you were already paid, it comes off your next payout.
- **Some rewards wait.** Rewards from a suspended account are held, and so are rewards from a sign-up made from the same internet address as your own account, until Tonet checks it.

## Who can join

Anyone with a Tonet login, whether or not they pay for Tonet. Your link and rewards belong to you as a person, not to the account you work in.

| You are | How to start |
|---|---|
| New to Tonet | Make a partner login at [dashboard.tonet.ai/partners](https://dashboard.tonet.ai/partners). No number, no payment. |
| Already on a Tonet account, in any role | You're already a partner. Click your picture at the top right, then **Refer & earn**. |
| A VA working in a client's account | Use your own **Refer & earn** page. Nobody else on the account sees it, and rewards are paid to you. |

## Become a partner

1. Go to [dashboard.tonet.ai/partners](https://dashboard.tonet.ai/partners).
2. Under **Become a partner**, click **Continue with Google**. Or type your **Email**, click **Email me a sign-in link**, then open the link or type the 6-digit code.
3. Rather use a password? Click **Use a password**, choose one of 8 characters or more, and click **Continue**.
4. Tonet opens **Refer & earn**, with your link ready.

- **Already have a login?** Click **Sign in** at the bottom of the page instead. You land on **Refer & earn**.
- A partner login has no phone system. Until it has one, it opens on **Refer & earn**.

## Share your link or code

Your link looks like `https://dashboard.tonet.ai/api/r/your-code`. Under **Your partner link**, pick where it **Opens**: **Tonet home**, **VA page** or **Sign-up form**. Then click **Copy link**, **Share on WhatsApp** or **Email it**.

| How the business finds you | It's yours when |
|---|---|
| It opens your link | It signs up within 90 days, in the same browser |
| It types your code in the [Have a code?](/sign_up#have-a-code) box | It enters the code while signing up |
| It used neither | You email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) within 7 days of its sign-up |

- If someone opens several partners' links, the latest one counts. A typed code counts over any link.
- Until your link is first used, you can click **Change code** and pick 3 to 24 letters, numbers or hyphens. Codes can't contain "tonet".

**What doesn't count:** your own email or account, accounts you create for yourself or pay for, businesses already on Tonet, and accounts closed for fraud or abuse.

## How you get paid

| Method | Minimum | When it's paid |
|---|---|---|
| **Tonet account credit** | Any amount | Overnight, added to your account's balance |
| **GCash**, **PayPal** or **Wise** | $25 | Each reward waits 45 days in case of a refund or chargeback, then goes out with the monthly payout |

- **Account credit** only goes on an account you own or manage. It pays for Tonet service, so if you only refer, pick a cash method.
- **Cash under $25** carries over to the next month.
- **First cash payout:** Tonet checks your partner account once, and may ask you to confirm who you are.
- **Currency:** rewards are earned in US dollars. GCash payouts are changed to pesos on the day they're sent.

Choose your method under **How you get paid** on **Refer & earn**, and tick **I agree to the partner program terms** the first time. Tonet emails you each time a payout is credited or sent.

**Refer & earn** also shows your link opens, sign-ups, paying accounts, rewards on hold and ready to pay, each account you referred and every payout. See [Track your referrals](/refer#track-your-referrals).

## The rules

- Be honest about what Tonet does and what it costs.
- No spam, no fake reviews, and no ads that bid on the Tonet name.
- Don't present yourself as Tonet.
- You're responsible for any taxes on your rewards.
- Tonet can change or end the program with 30 days' notice. Rewards already earned are paid under the terms in place when you earned them.
- Rewards earned by breaking the rules aren't paid.

Read the full terms at [tonet.ai/partner-reseller-program](https://tonet.ai/partner-reseller-program/#terms).
