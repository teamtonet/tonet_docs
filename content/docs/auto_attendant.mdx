A menu answers calls with your greeting and lets callers pick where to go: "For sales, press 1. For service, press 2." It's also called an auto attendant or IVR. Each key sends the caller to a person, a [team](/groups), a voicemail box, another menu or an outside number.

Owners, managers and admins manage menus in **Manage › Menus**.

## Make a menu

1. Go to **Manage › Menus** and click **New menu**.
2. Type a **Name**, like "Main menu".
3. Click **Upload/Record Audio...** and add your greeting. See [Add the greeting](#add-the-greeting).
4. Under **Routes**, click **Add Route**. Next to **Press**, pick a key. Next to **For**, click **Destination** and pick where that key goes.
5. Repeat step 4 for each key.
6. Click **Save**.

A new menu gets no calls yet. [Connect it to a number](#connect-a-menu-to-a-number) next.

Need words for the greeting? Copy a [menu script](/guides/menu-and-voicemail-scripts).

## Add the greeting

Every menu needs a greeting. You can't save a menu without one.

1. Click **Upload/Record Audio...**.
2. Either:
   - **Record:** click the microphone, speak, then click it again to stop. Click play to listen. Allow the microphone if your browser asks.
   - **Upload an MP3:** drag the file into the box, or click **Click to upload**.
3. Click **Upload**.
4. Click **Save** on the menu.

Tonet turns your recording into phone audio. If it can't read a file, it tells you and keeps the greeting callers hear now.

To change a greeting later, open the menu, do the same steps, and save.

## Keys you can use

| Key | Pick in the list |
|---|---|
| 1 to 9, 0 | **One** to **Nine**, **Zero** |
| `*` | **Star** |
| `#` | **Pound** |
| No key pressed | **No_response** |

Each key can be used once per menu. Keys already used are greyed out.

## Where a key can send callers

**Destination** opens **Select Route Destination**. Pick a category, or search by name or extension:

| Category | Sends callers to |
|---|---|
| **Users** | One person |
| **Teams** | A [team](/groups). It rings together or queues, as the team is set. |
| **IVRs** | Another menu |
| **Voicemail** | A person's or a team's voicemail box |
| **Features** | **Busy** (callers hear a busy signal), or **Phone Number** to forward calls to an outside number |

For **Phone Number**, type the number in **Forward to Phone Number** and click **Set Phone Number**.

## What callers hear

- The greeting plays as soon as the call reaches the menu. Callers can press a key at any time. They don't have to wait for the end.
- Callers can also dial a person's extension while the greeting plays.
- **If they press nothing:** with **No_response** set, they go there. Without it, the greeting plays again, up to 3 times in all. Then Tonet says goodbye and ends the call.
- **If they press a key you didn't set:** they hear that it's not valid, then the greeting again. After 5 wrong keys, Tonet ends the call.

Want a "press 9 to hear this again" option? Point key 9 at this same menu, under **IVRs**. A new menu shows up in that list once it's saved.

## Make a menu inside a menu

A key can open another menu, for example "For service, press 2" leading to a service menu with its own options. This is a multi-level auto attendant.

1. Make the second menu first, like "Service menu", with its own greeting and keys.
2. Open your main menu. Point a key at "Service menu" under **IVRs**.
3. Click **Save**.

Tip: give the second menu a key back to the main menu, so callers who picked wrong can go back.

## Connect a menu to a number

1. Go to **Manage › Phones** and click the number.
2. Open the **Route & settings** tab.
3. In **Go To**, pick **IVRs**, then your menu.
4. Click **Save Settings**.

More on routing a number: [Choose where calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go).

You can also play a menu only at certain times:

- In a [schedule](/schedule) route, like your main menu during business hours.
- In a [calendar](/calendar) event, like a holiday menu on the day.

## The Menus page

Switch between **Cards** and **List** at the top. Your browser remembers which one you picked.

Each card shows:

- The menu's name
- **Answers** and the numbers whose calls come to this menu: through **Go To**, or right now through a schedule or calendar event. Otherwise it says **Not answering a number yet**.
- A player for the greeting, or **No greeting recorded yet**
- Each key and where it goes. **No choice** is where callers go when they press nothing.
- How many options the menu has, and **Edit menu**

Open a card's **⋯** menu for **Edit** and **Delete**. In **List**, use **Edit IVR** and **Delete IVR**.

## Delete a menu

1. Open the menu's **⋯** menu and click **Delete**. In **List**, click **Delete IVR**.
2. Tonet checks where calls go. If numbers, other menus, schedules or calendar events still send calls to the menu, it lists them. Change each one, then click **Check again**.
3. Click **Delete IVR**.

This can't be undone.
