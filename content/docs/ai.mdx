Tonet's AI listens to your recorded calls after they end. It writes down what was said, sums each call up, tags it and finds what was promised. Across all your calls, it spots leads going cold and what callers keep asking. It learns how your business answers, and does tasks in your account when you ask it to.

There's nothing to set up. It works on every recorded call, in the dashboard, the [desktop app](/app), desk phones and the phone app.

## After every call

| What Tonet does | Where you see it |
|---|---|
| **Writes down every word**, with who said it and when. Card numbers, Social Security numbers and PINs are hidden. | The transcript on [a call's page](/calls/call-page#read-and-search-the-transcript) |
| **Sums up the call** in a few sentences, with **Sources**: the moments it's based on | **Call review** on the call's page, and one line in [Calls](/analytics/call_logs) |
| **Lists next steps** that were agreed. One click makes a task or a calendar event. | **Next steps** in the call review |
| **Suggests tags** for the call, like "booking". Keep the ones you like. | The [after-call review](/calls/after-call-review) |
| **Marks moments** on the recording: a price said, a next step, a transfer, both talking at once | The talk bars above the transcript |
| **Translates the transcript** into English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Tagalog, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese, German or Swedish | **Translate** on the call's page |
| **Writes voicemails out as text** | [Voicemail](/calls/voicemail) and the bell |
| **Flags robocallers**, with **Block number** right there | The after-call review |

## Across all your calls

| What Tonet does | Where you see it |
|---|---|
| **Finds leads going cold**: someone who asked for a quote or a call back in the last 30 days and hasn't heard from you for 7 days | [What Tonet noticed](/overview#see-what-tonet-noticed-in-your-calls) on Overview |
| **Spots what callers keep saying**: questions, objections, competitors and complaints that came up with at least 3 callers this week | What Tonet noticed |
| **Coaches each person**: their strongest habit and biggest gap this week, out of five (introducing themselves, asking about needs, answering objections, proposing a next step, confirming details) | What Tonet noticed, under **Coaching** |
| **Listens for your topics**: up to 20 things you care about, like "Asked about financing". Tonet tags the calls and counts them. | **What to listen for on calls**, in [your AI settings](/account#change-ai-settings) |
| **Learns your answers**: what your team tells callers, your hours and menus, and your website | [Knowledge](/knowledge) |

## During a call

Calls in the dashboard and the desktop app open in the [live call view](/calls/live-call), a full page where Tonet works alongside you while you talk.

| What Tonet does | Where you see it |
|---|---|
| **Writes the call out as you talk**, line by line | The live transcript in the middle of the page |
| **Fills in the call's details as it hears them**: what they're calling about, what was promised, a quote, the next step | **This call**, on the left, marked ✦ |

## In meetings

[Meetings](/meetings) are voice meetings in the dashboard, with screen sharing and outside guests. **Tonet AI** comes on by itself: it writes down what's said, adds the action items you agree on, and answers when you type **@Tonet** or say "Tonet," out loud. When the meeting ends it posts a **Wrap-up**: what was decided, who does what, and what's still open. One click turns the open items into tasks. Tonet pays for it.

## Whenever you ask

[Ask Tonet](/ask-tonet) is the chat at the bottom right of the dashboard. It answers questions about Tonet for free, and it can work in your account:

It can find calls by what was said, count missed calls, change where a number's calls go, make schedules and teams, add contacts and tasks, and write emails and replies. It can't buy numbers, take payments or delete users. See [what Ask Tonet can do](/ask-tonet#what-ask-tonet-can-do).

## What Tonet's AI reads

| Reads | Never reads |
|---|---|
| Recorded calls and voicemails | Your team chat, unless someone allows it in Ask Tonet |
| Texts with your customers | Calls made while AI was off, even after you turn it back on |
| Your settings, contacts, tasks and notes | |

Transcripts are made on Tonet's own servers. Summaries, tags and answers are written by an outside AI provider. Knowledge leaves out passwords, PINs, card and bank numbers, Social Security numbers and health details.

A call needs a recording for any of this, and a summary needs at least 30 seconds of talking. Calls that aren't recorded get no transcript, summary or tags.

## What it costs

Most of it costs nothing. Tonet pays for transcripts, summaries, tags, translations, voicemail text, What Tonet noticed and what Knowledge learns.

What you ask for yourself uses your account's **included AI credit**: [Ask Tonet](/ask-tonet)'s tasks, and web look-ups Tonet makes during a call. Every month the account gets **$5, plus $2 for each active person**. Getting help with Tonet in Ask Tonet is always free.

When the credit runs out, those pause until it renews next month. The owner can turn on **Keep AI running after the included credit** and set a **Monthly cap**: extra use then comes out of your balance, never more than the cap.

## Turn it off, or cap it

Owners and managers control AI for the whole account in **Knowledge › Your business** (or **Manage › Account › AI** on accounts without Knowledge):

![AI on this account and AI use: the switch, what the AI reads and never reads, and this month's included credit](/images/ai/ai-settings.webp)

- **Use AI on this account** turns it all off or on. Off, summaries, tags and Ask Tonet's tasks stop for everyone, and nothing from your account goes to the AI provider. Recordings, transcripts and call history keep working.
- **AI use** shows how much of this month's credit is used, and **What it was spent on this month**.
- **Daily limits** are a safety net under the credit: a cap per day for the whole account and for each person. They reset at midnight Eastern.
- **Call review** in **Manage › Account › Calls** turns summaries off on their own, or sets whether Tonet fills tags in or only suggests them.

See [Change AI settings](/account#change-ai-settings).
