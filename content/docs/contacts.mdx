Contacts holds everyone your business talks to. Each contact has a page with every call, text and note in one place, so whoever picks up knows the history.

Open **Contacts** in the left menu.

**Who sees what:** owners, managers and admins see every contact. Regular users see the contacts assigned to them or to their teams.

## Find someone

- Type in **Search names, numbers, companies, tags…**
- Or pick a view at the top:

![Contacts, with views at the top and a row for each contact](/images/contacts/contacts-list.webp)

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every contact you can see |
| **Mine** | Contacts assigned to you |
| **Due today** | Contacts with a next step due today or overdue |
| **Leads** | Contacts marked as leads |
| **Not saved** | Numbers you've talked to that aren't saved as contacts yet |
| **Added by Tonet ✦** | Contacts Tonet added for you. Shown when there are some. |

Click **+ View** to make your own view. Describe who you want, like "leads in Chicago", and Tonet turns it into filters you can check and change. Save it for **Only me** or **Everyone**.

## Add a contact

1. Click **New contact**.
2. Choose **Person** or **Business**.
3. Enter a name, phone number, and optionally an email, company and tags.
4. Click **Create**, or **Create and add another**.

If the number is already a contact, Tonet tells you.

## A contact's page

Click a contact's name to open their page. From there you can:

- **Call** or **Text** them.
- Change their details in the **Details** box. Click a detail to change it. You can add more phone numbers, emails, an address, a website and a relationship (Lead, Customer, Vendor, Partner or Other).
- See every call, text and note under **Conversations**. Use the **Calls**, **Texts** and **Notes** tabs, or search what was said.
- Write a note for your team. Type in **Write a note about … for your team**, then press Enter. Your team sees notes; the contact never does.

To change everything at once, open **More** and click **Edit everything**.

## Tags and owners

- **Tags:** add up to 20 tags to a contact, like "vip" or "residential". Search and views can use them.
- **Owner:** the people or teams a contact belongs to. The owner decides who can see the contact. Owners, managers and admins set it by clicking the **Owner** column in the list.

To tag or assign many contacts at once, tick them in the list and use the bar that appears.

## Set a next step

The **Next step** column in the list shows what's next with each contact, like "Call back · tomorrow". It's their soonest open task. A task with no date shows only when they have nothing dated. A late one turns orange. One from an earlier day says how many days late, like "overdue 2 d".

Each next step is a task, so it's also under **Upcoming** on [Overview](/overview#keep-track-of-your-tasks).

To add or change one, click the contact's **Next step** cell. It reads **+ Next step** when it's empty.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| The text box | What to do. A new one starts as **Call back**. |
| **When** | **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Mon** (next Monday), or pick a date and time. A new one starts at 5 PM today, or 5 PM tomorrow when it's already past 5. If Tonet knows where the number is, it shows the time for you and for them. |
| **Remind** | **At the time**, **15 min before** or **Off**. The reminder comes in the bell. |
| **For** | Who does it. Only owners and managers can pick someone else. |
| **Add** or **Save** | Saves it |
| **Done** | Marks it done. The column then shows their next one, if there is one. |
| **Snooze** | Reminds again tomorrow at 9 AM. The due time stays the same. |

**Who sees what:** you see the next steps for you and your teams. A next step for a teammate shows in their list, not yours.

## Contacts Tonet adds (✦)

After a call, voicemail or text with a new number, Tonet adds a contact for it, marked ✦.

- Click **Keep** on their page to keep it, or **Remove** to delete it. Tonet won't add a removed number again.
- Editing, tagging or noting a ✦ contact keeps it too.
- Tonet never adds your own numbers, blocked numbers or spam callers.

To stop this, an owner or manager turns off **Tonet adds contacts for people you talk to** in **Manage › Account**, on the **Calls** tab. Contacts already added stay.

## Look up a number

Find out who's behind a number, then save what you find as a contact. You don't need a contact first.

- **Who can use it:** everyone.
- **Price:** 5¢ per new search, from your balance. Nothing is charged until you click.

| Where | Click |
|---|---|
| **Contacts**, at the top | **Look up a number**. Type the number, with its country code if it's outside the US and Canada, then click **Continue**. |
| A contact's page, in the **Lookup** box | **Look up**, or **Look up again** |
| A call from a number that isn't a contact | **Look up caller · 5¢** on the call card, under **Who is this?** It starts right away. |
| The dialpad, after a full number | **Look up number · 5¢** |

A search runs two checks, and the 5¢ covers both. In **Contacts** both start ticked: untick one if you like, then click **Search · 5¢**. Results show as each check finishes.

| Check | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Caller details** | The caller ID name, the carrier, the line type (like mobile or landline) and the country |
| **Public information** | What public web pages say about the number: **Name**, **Website**, **Email**, **Business address**, **About**, **Other phone numbers**, **Opening hours** and **Social profiles**. Each one links to its **Source**. |

Each result says how sure it is: **Exact number found · identity unverified**, **Possible match · review carefully**, or **No public match found**. Click **Search deeper · 5¢** to search more pages.

### Save what you find

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Create contact** | Starts a contact with the details found. On the call card, it saves right away, or asks for a **Contact name** first. |
| **Update contact** | Shown when the number is already a contact. Adds missing details and a **Lookup** tag. Keeps names, notes and anything already filled in. |
| **Create a separate contact** | Makes a new contact even though the number is saved |
| **Add contact manually** | Shown when nothing usable was found |

When the number belongs to one saved business and the lookup found that exact number, Tonet fills its empty fields by itself: **Published details saved to your contact. Your edits are kept.** The contact's **Lookup** box says what Tonet filled. Change any of it in **Details**.

### What's free

| Search | Cost |
|---|---|
| A new search, one check or both | 5¢ |
| **Search deeper** | 5¢ more |
| A search that found no match | 5¢ |
| A number looked up in the last 7 days | Free: **Saved result · free**. A no-match result is kept for 15 minutes only. |
| A search where no check finished | Free |

- You need at least 5¢ in your balance, and the account can't be locked. If not, you see **This lookup needs 5¢ in your active account balance.** [Add funds](/billing#add-funds), then try again.
- Each account has a daily limit on new searches. After it: **The account reached its daily lookup limit. Saved results remain free.**
- Lookups show as **Caller lookups** under **Spending** in [Billing](/billing#see-what-you-spent).

<Callout type="info">
  A lookup shows public information, not proof of who someone is, and it may be out of date. Check the **Source** links before you save.
</Callout>

## Import contacts

Owners, managers and admins can import.

1. Click **Import**.
2. Drop a CSV file, or paste rows from a spreadsheet. Up to 50,000 rows.
3. **Match columns.** Tonet matches your columns to fields. Change any that are wrong. **Phone** is the only column you must have.
4. Choose what happens when someone is already a contact.
5. Optional: tag everyone in this import, or give out owners.
6. Check how the first rows look, then click **Import**.

Made a mistake? Click **Undo this import**. You can undo it for 7 days.

<Callout type="info">
  Using Excel or Google Sheets? Save the sheet as CSV first, or copy the rows (headers included) and paste them.
</Callout>

## Export contacts

Click **Export**. You get a CSV file of the contacts you can see.

## Block a number

Blocking is done in **Manage › Phones › Blocked**, or with **Block** when a call rings. See [Blocked numbers](/phones/blacklist).
