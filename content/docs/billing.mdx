Tonet is prepaid. You add credit to your balance, and Tonet takes small charges from it each day. There is no contract and no invoice.

- **Who can use it:** owners and managers.
- **Where:** **Manage › Billing**, or click your balance in the top bar.

## What you pay

| Item | Pay as you go | Unlimited |
|---|---|---|
| Each person on calls | $10 a month (33¢ a day) | $15 a month (50¢ a day) |
| Calls to and from US and Canada numbers | 1¢ a minute | Included |
| Calls that come in on a toll-free number | By the minute | By the minute |
| Texts | 3¢ each | 3¢ each |
| Each phone number | $2.50 a month | $2.50 a month |

- A local number costs $2.50 when you buy it, then $2.50 about every 29 days.
- A toll-free number costs $3.00 for its first month, then $2.50 a month.
- Picture messages and recording storage have their own rates.
- Your exact rates are on the Billing page. Click **More account rates** to see them all.

## How charges work

- Each person is charged once a day. Delete a person and their daily charge stops.
- Each number is charged about every 29 days.
- Calls and texts are charged as they happen.

## Add funds

1. Go to **Manage › Billing** and click **Add funds**.
2. Under **Payment method**, pick **Credit card** or **Crypto**.
3. Enter an amount in whole dollars. The minimum is $15.
4. Click **Add Funds**.

**First time paying by card?** Click **Add card** first. Your card details go straight to Authorize.Net; Tonet never sees the full number. After you save the card, click **Continue to amount**.

<Callout type="info">
  If a card payment is declined, you can try again after 2 minutes. Some payments are checked by hand. You'll see **Payment pending review**, and the money is added once it's approved.
</Callout>

## Pay with crypto

1. Click **Add funds** and choose **Crypto**.
2. Pick a **Coin** and a **Network**. You can pay with USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH, SOL, LTC or TON.
3. Enter the amount. The minimum is $15.
4. Send the exact amount to the **Address** shown. If a **Memo** is shown, include it.
5. Send it before the rate lock runs out. The page shows a countdown.

Your balance goes up once the payment is confirmed on the network. This can take a few minutes. Click **Check payment** to look again.

## Use a code

In **Add funds**, type your code in the **Have a code?** box and click **Apply**. Partner, promo and team codes all go here.

## Turn on auto-recharge

Auto-recharge adds funds by card when your balance gets low, so calls never stop.

1. On the Billing page, click **Auto-recharge off** under your balance.
2. Turn on **Enable auto-recharge**.
3. Set **When balance falls below (USD)**, for example $10.
4. Set **Bring balance up to (USD)**. It must be at least $10 more than the first amount.
5. Click **Save settings**.

You need a saved card for auto-recharge.

## When your balance reaches $0

- Outgoing calls and texts stop until you add funds.
- Daily charges pause.
- Accounts left at $0 for a long time can lose their numbers. If a number is missing after you top up, contact [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).

## See what you spent

- **Billing period:** pick a month.
- **Spending:** your charges for that month. Switch between **By item** and **By user**.
- **Payment history:** every payment, its status and how you paid. Click **Receipt** to download a PDF.

Tonet also emails a receipt for each payment to the account owner, and to the person who paid if that's someone else.
