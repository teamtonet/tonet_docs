Refer & earn pays you for sending businesses to Tonet. The business you refer gets $10 of Tonet credit. You get $10, then 10% of every card payment it makes, for as long as it stays with Tonet.

**Where:** click your picture at the top right of the dashboard, then **Refer & earn**.

Everyone on a Tonet account has their own link, whatever their role. Your page is yours alone: nobody else on your account sees it. Rewards are yours too, paid to you by GCash, PayPal or Wise, or as credit on an account you manage.

**Not on Tonet?** Refer & earn is your page in the [Tonet partner program](/partners). It's the same program, open to anyone: people with no Tonet account join with a free login at [dashboard.tonet.ai/partners](https://dashboard.tonet.ai/partners), with no number and no payment.

## How the rewards work

| Who | Gets | When |
|---|---|---|
| The business you refer | $10 of Tonet credit | After its first card payment goes through |
| You | $10 | When that first card payment goes through |
| You | 10% of each card payment it makes | For as long as it stays with Tonet |

- **Only card payments count.** Crypto top-ups and credits earn you nothing, and the business's $10 waits for its first card payment.
- **Refunds come back off.** If a payment is refunded, voided or declined, its reward is taken back. If you were already paid, it comes off your next payout.
- **You can't refer yourself.** A sign-up with your own email, or the account you work in, isn't credited to you.
- **Some rewards wait.** Rewards from a suspended account are held. So are rewards from a sign-up made from the same internet address your own account was created from, until Tonet checks it.

## Share your link

1. Open **Refer & earn**.
2. Under **Your partner link**, pick where the link **Opens**: **Tonet home**, **VA page** or **Sign-up form**.
3. Click **Copy link**, **Share on WhatsApp** or **Email it**.

- **90 days to sign up:** anyone who signs up within 90 days of opening your link is credited to you. If they open another partner's link after yours, the latest link counts.
- **Your code works too:** someone signing up on another device can type your code in the [Have a code?](/sign_up#have-a-code) box. A typed code counts over a link. Click **Copy code** to copy it.
- **Missed your link?** If a business you sent signed up without your link or code, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) within 7 days of their sign-up.

## Change your code

You can pick your own code until your link is used for the first time.

1. Click **Change code**.
2. Type 3 to 24 letters, numbers or hyphens.
3. Click **Save**.

Once someone has opened your link or signed up with it, your code can't change.

## Choose how you get paid

1. Under **How you get paid**, pick a method:

   | Method | How it works |
   |---|---|
   | **Tonet account credit** | Added to the balance of the account you're signed in to, in any amount. Owners, managers and administrators can choose it. |
   | **GCash**, **PayPal** or **Wise** | Cash, from $25. Enter your GCash mobile number, or your PayPal or Wise email, and the **Full name on that account**. |

2. The first time, tick **I agree to the partner program terms**.
3. Click **Save payout method**.

Rewards are earned in US dollars. GCash payouts are changed to pesos on the day they're sent.

## When you get paid

| Method | When |
|---|---|
| **Tonet account credit** | Added to your balance overnight. Tonet emails you when it's added. |
| **GCash**, **PayPal** or **Wise** | Each reward is held 45 days in case of a refund or chargeback. Then it goes out with the monthly payout, once you have at least $25 ready. |

Before your first cash payout, Tonet checks your partner account once.

If the page says **Payouts are on hold for your partner account**, contact support.

## Track your referrals

The page shows:

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Totals | **Link opens**, **Sign-ups**, **Paying accounts** and **Earned so far** |
| **Your rewards** | **On hold**, **Ready to pay**, **Being sent** and **Paid to you** |
| **Accounts you referred** | Each account, shortened for their privacy, with when it joined, **Signed up** or **Paying**, and what you earned from it |
| **Payouts** | Each payout's date, method, amount and status: **Credited**, **Sending**, **Sent** or **Cancelled** |

## Change where partner emails go

Payout notices and partner news go to your sign-in email at first. To use another email, like a personal one:

1. Under **Where we email you**, type it in **Email for partner messages**.
2. Click **Save email**.
3. Open the confirmation link Tonet sends to that address.

Until you confirm it, messages keep going to the old address.
