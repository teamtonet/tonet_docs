A schedule sends a number's calls to different places by day and time. For example, Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 5 PM, calls ring your front desk. Any time the schedule doesn't cover goes to the number's **Go To**, so after-hours calls can reach voicemail or an after-hours menu.

Owners, managers and admins manage schedules in **Manage › Schedules**.

## Make a schedule

1. Go to **Manage › Schedules** and click **New Schedule**.
2. In **Add Schedule**, type a **Title**, like "Business hours", and click **Confirm**. The schedule opens.
3. Click **Add Route**. A new row appears. If its fields aren't open, click the arrow at the start of the row.
4. Fill in the route:
   - **Start time** and **End time**
   - **Destination**: where calls go during these hours. Pick from **Users**, **Teams**, **IVRs** (menus), **Voicemail** or **Features**.
   - **Week Days**: pick at least one day.
5. Click **Update**.
6. Add more routes the same way, like a shorter day on Saturday.

A route can't run past midnight: its **End time** can't be earlier than its **Start time**. Leave the night to the number's **Go To**.

## Apply a schedule to a number

A schedule does nothing until a number uses it.

1. Go to **Manage › Phones** and click the number.
2. Open the **Route & settings** tab.
3. In **Schedule**, pick your schedule.
4. In **Go To**, pick where calls go when no route covers the time: voicemail, an after-hours [menu](/auto_attendant), or a person.
5. Click **Save Settings**.

Each number uses one schedule. Several numbers can share it. To stop using a schedule, pick **(none)**.

## How Tonet picks where a call goes

When a call comes in, Tonet checks in this order:

1. A [calendar](/calendar) event happening now, if the number uses it
2. The schedule: the first route that covers this day and time
3. **Go To**

If two routes overlap, the one higher in the list wins.

Schedule times follow your account's time zone. Owners and managers set it in **Manage › Account**, on the **General** tab, under **Time Zone**.

## Example: business hours

A front desk open weekdays 9 to 5 and Saturday mornings.

Routes in a schedule called "Business hours":

| Start time | End time | Week Days | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:00 AM | 5:00 PM | Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday | **Teams** › Front desk |
| 10:00 AM | 2:00 PM | Saturday | **Teams** › Front desk |

On the main number: **Schedule** is "Business hours" and **Go To** is an after-hours menu.

Calls in open hours ring the front desk team. Every other hour, including nights and Sundays, goes to the after-hours menu. For holidays, add a [calendar](/calendar) event.

## See your week

Use **View** at the top:

- **Week View** draws each route on the week, with its times and destination. Pick a schedule in **Schedule**. Click a block to edit that schedule. On a small screen, it shows one day.
- **List View** lists your schedules with how many routes each has, and **Edit Schedule** and **Delete Schedule** buttons.

## Change a schedule

- **Rename it:** change **Schedule Name** and click **Update**.
- **Change a route:** click the arrow at the start of its row, make your changes, and click **Update**.
- **Remove a route:** open it and click **Delete Route**.

## Delete a schedule

1. Open the schedule and click **Remove Schedule**. In **List View**, click **Delete Schedule**.
2. If numbers still use it, Tonet lists them. On each number, pick another schedule or **(none)**, then click **Check again**.
3. Click **Delete schedule**.

This can't be undone.
