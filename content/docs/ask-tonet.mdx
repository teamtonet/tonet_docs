Ask Tonet is the **Ask Tonet** button at the bottom right of the dashboard. Use it to:

- **Get help** with Tonet: how something works, or why something isn't working. It's free.
- **Run a task**: have Tonet do something in your account, or write something for you.
- **Talk to a person** on Tonet's support team.

Everyone on your account can use it. What it can see and change depends on your role.

## Open Ask Tonet

1. Click **Ask Tonet** at the bottom right of any page. The button changes to **Close**.
2. Under **How can we help?**, pick **Get help** or **Run a task**.

![Ask Tonet open: How can we help? with Get help, Run a task, and a conversation to pick up](/images/ask-tonet/ask-tonet-start.webp)

| Choice | Use it to | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Get help** | Ask how Tonet works, or fix something that isn't working | Free |
| **Run a task** | Have Tonet do something in your account, or write something for you | Counts toward your account's [AI use](#where-your-ai-settings-are) |

A conversation you start any other way, like **New conversation**, counts as a task.

- **On the Messages page** the button is hidden. Click **✦** in the top bar instead. ✦ also shows on other pages when you have unread replies, with the number of unread messages.
- **Make the window bigger:** on a wide screen, drag the handle at its top left corner. Double-click the handle to reset the size.
- **Pick up where you left off:** reopen Ask Tonet within 5 minutes and you're back where you were. After that it opens on **How can we help?**, where **Continue conversation** takes you back.

## Get help with Tonet

1. Click **Ask Tonet**, then **Get help**.
2. Type your question, like "How do I send calls to voicemail after hours?"
3. Click **Start conversation →**.

Ask Tonet answers in the chat and points you to the page in the dashboard where you do it.

It can also check your account for common setup problems:

| Your role | What it checks |
|---|---|
| Owners and managers | A balance at $0 or below, no phone numbers, and teams with no members |
| Admins and regular users | Whether you have a number you can text from |

The check reads your settings. It doesn't test calls or sound. Owners and managers can also ask about their balance and what a charge was for.

Get help never changes anything in your account. To have something done, use **Run a task**.

## Have Ask Tonet do a task

1. Click **Ask Tonet**, then **Run a task**.
2. Say what you want in your own words, like "Add extension 104 to the Sales team" or "Find this week's calls where someone asked about prices".
3. Click **Start conversation →**.

Ask Tonet works out the steps and does them. You see what it's doing while it works. Click **Stop work** to stop it. A step already underway may still finish.

Each change shows under the chat with its result, such as **Verified**, **Already set**, **Needs attention** or **Failed**. Click it for the details and a **Review in Tonet** link.

### What Ask Tonet can do

| Task | Who can |
|---|---|
| Find calls by number, date or words said, up to 90 days at a time, and read their transcripts | Everyone, for calls they can see |
| Count calls and texts, and list missed calls and texts still waiting for a reply | Everyone, for what they can see |
| Look up people and extensions, who's online, teams and your numbers | Everyone |
| Look up your menus | Owners, managers and admins |
| Answer questions about your own business, like your prices or hours, from [Knowledge](/knowledge) | Everyone, when your account has Knowledge |
| Add contacts, notes and tags | Everyone, for contacts they can see |
| Change who owns a contact, up to 50 at a time | Owners, managers and admins |
| Create tasks and reminders | Everyone. Owners and managers can give them to others. |
| Send a team message from you | Everyone |
| Write emails, replies and promotions, or translate a message | Everyone |
| Rename a number, change where its calls go, or give it a schedule | Owners and managers |
| Make a schedule and set its hours | Owners and managers |
| Make a team, or add and remove its members | Owners and managers |
| Choose who can use a number | Owners and managers |
| Fill in a port request as a draft | Owners and managers |
| Check your balance, explain a charge, or check a port or texting registration | Owners and managers |

Admins can't change settings through Ask Tonet. They make those changes in **Manage**.

A port draft is never submitted for you. Open **Manage › Phones › Port a Number**, add your account number and PIN, and submit it yourself. See [Port a Number](/phones/port_number).

### When Ask Tonet checks with you first

- It changes things only when you clearly ask. A question, an idea or a request for a draft changes nothing.
- A clear request is enough. It doesn't ask you to confirm it again.
- When something is unclear, like two people with the same name, it asks you first.

<Callout type="warn">
  Ask Tonet is AI and can misread a request. Check the result under the chat, and talk to a person if something looks wrong.
</Callout>

### Let Ask Tonet read your team chat

Your team chat is private. Ask Tonet reads your own team chats only after you click **Allow** on the card it shows in the conversation. That lasts 2 hours, in that conversation only. Click **Don't** to say no. It never reads chats you're not part of.

## What Ask Tonet can't do

- Buy or release numbers, take payments, add or delete users, or submit a port or texting registration. It tells you where to do these yourself.
- See or change anything your role can't.
- Test your calls. Its checks only read your settings.

Never type passwords, verification codes, card numbers or your port PIN into the chat. Ask Tonet doesn't need them.

## Talk to a person

1. Open a conversation, or start one with **Get help**. Click **Talk to a person** under the message box, or send a message that just says "talk to a person".
2. Tonet alerts its support team. You'll see **Checking if someone from the team is free…**
3. If someone sees it, the chat says **Someone from the team will be with you shortly.** When they join, their name shows at the top.

**If no one is free,** Ask Tonet stays with you, and the team still sees the chat. Describe the problem in full, and someone replies when they're back. Don't want to wait? Click **Answer me now instead**.

If someone said they're coming but hasn't written within 2 minutes, Ask Tonet keeps helping until they do.

While a person is in the chat:

- They can see what you type before you send it.
- Your messages show **Sent ✓**, then **Seen ✓✓** once the team has read them.
- They may ask to see your screen. You choose a screen, window or tab. They can see it but not control it. Click **Share screen** or **Not now**, and **Stop sharing** to end it.
- Click **Back to Ask Tonet** to go back to the AI.

When someone from Tonet writes while Ask Tonet is closed, their message shows in a card above the button. Click **Reply** to answer, or **×** to hide it.

You can also email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).

## Ask about a missed call from the bell

In the bell's list, click **Ask Tonet** under a missed call, voicemail or message. Ask Tonet opens with a request about it already typed: suggest a next step, without texting or calling anyone yet. Change it if you like, then send it. Nothing is sent until you do.

**Review in Ask Tonet**, at the bottom of the list, asks it to look over your recent missed calls and unread messages and say what needs attention.

## Find, rename or archive a conversation

- Click **Conversations** to see your conversations. Switch between **Current** and **Archived**.
- Ask Tonet names each conversation for you. Click **Rename** to change the name, up to 80 characters.
- Click **Archive** to put a conversation away. This ends a chat with a person and stops any task in it. Click **Restore** to pick it up again.
- You can have up to 200 conversations that aren't archived.

Your conversations are your own. Others on your account don't see them. Tonet's support team can.

## Why Ask Tonet didn't answer

When Ask Tonet can't reply, it says why. It doesn't pass your question to support on its own, so click **Talk to a person** if you want one.

| Message starts with | What to do |
|---|---|
| **AI is off for this account** | Owners and managers: click **Turn on AI** right in the chat. Everyone else: ask an owner or manager. You can still talk to a person. |
| **Your daily … allowance cannot cover this AI reply** (or **Your account's daily …**) | A daily AI limit for you or your account is used up. Click **Why this reply was blocked** to see which one and when it resets. Limits reset at midnight Eastern. |
| **Your shared included AI credit is used** | This month's AI credit is used up, so tasks wait until it renews. **Get help** still works. The owner can check it in [your AI settings](#where-your-ai-settings-are). |
| **AI tasks are unavailable while your account is suspended, locked or has an outstanding balance** | Add funds in [Billing](/billing). **Get help** still works. |
| **This conversation is too long for another AI reply** | Start a new conversation. |
| **That reply was interrupted** | Send your message again, or talk to a person. |

### Where your AI settings are

The AI switch, the AI credit and the daily limits are under **AI on this account** and **AI use**:

- In **Knowledge**, on the **Your business** tab.
- If your account doesn't have Knowledge: in **Manage › Account**, on the **AI** tab.

Owners and managers change them. Daily limits are set for the whole account and for each person.

## If Ask Tonet won't open

- While it loads, the Tonet mark moves in the window. The first open after an update can take longer.
- If it can't load in 30 seconds, it says **Chat could not finish loading**. Click **Try again**, or **Email support**.
- If your connection drops, the top of the chat says **Reconnecting… Your draft is safe.** Click **Try again** if it doesn't come back.
