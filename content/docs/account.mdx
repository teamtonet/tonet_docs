**Manage › Account** holds the settings for your whole business, plus your own password. Only owners and managers can open it. Administrators and Regular Users don't see it.

| Tab | What you set there |
|---|---|
| **General** | Your business time zone and address |
| **Calls** | What happens after a call, Tonet's tags, contacts Tonet adds, review fields, and how long recordings are kept |
| **Security** | Your own password |
| **AI** | What Tonet knows about your business, and AI use. This tab shows only on accounts without **Knowledge**. See [Change AI settings](#change-ai-settings). |

## Open account settings

1. Click **Manage** in the left menu.
2. Click **Account**.

To change the time zone, you can also click the clock at the top of the dashboard. It opens this page at **Time Zone**.

## Change your time zone or business address

1. On the **General** tab, go to **Location**.
2. Pick your **Country** and **Time Zone**.
3. Fill in **Street Address** and **Apt / Suite / Etc**. The city, state and postal code boxes change to match the country.
4. Click **Update**.

![Manage › Account, General tab: basic details and location](/images/account/account-general.webp)

The time zone sets the clock at the top of the dashboard. **Calls**, **Analytics** and **Billing** use it to decide what counts as today, this week and this month.

**Full Name** and **Email Address** under **Basic details** belong to the account and can't be changed here. Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) if you need to change them.

## Choose what happens after a call

After an answered call, Tonet can show a review: the call's summary, its tags, a note and any fields you add. On the **Calls** tab, under **Call review**, pick:

![Account › Calls: call review, Tonet AI for tags and fields, and a preview of a call review](/images/account/account-calls.webp)

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Required** | The review stays until someone clicks **Done**. |
| **Optional** | Reviews nobody touches fold into **Recent calls**. This is the default. |
| **Off** | No review after calls. |

Everything on the **Calls** tab saves as soon as you click. The **Preview** beside it shows a made-up call with your choices. Switch it between **During the call** and **After the call**.

## Let Tonet fill in tags or only suggest them

Under **Tonet AI, for tags and fields**, pick:

| Choice | What Tonet does |
|---|---|
| **Fill them in** | Adds up to 3 call tags and 2 contact tags, marked **Tonet**. You remove any that are wrong. |
| **Suggest them** | Shows its tags dashed. Click one to keep it. This is the default. |

Call tags describe one call. Contact tags stay on the person and show on every call with them.

## Stop Tonet adding contacts

After a call, voicemail or text with a new number, Tonet adds a contact for it, marked ✦ in [Contacts](/contacts).

To stop this, turn off **Tonet adds contacts for people you talk to** on the **Calls** tab. It's on until you turn it off. Contacts already added stay.

## Choose what a call review shows

Under **What a call review shows**, each row has switches:

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| **In review** | Shows the row in the review |
| **Required** | **Done** waits until it's filled in. Only for rows shown in review. |
| **AI fills** | Tonet fills in or suggests these tags, as you chose above. Off, it does neither. Tags only. |

The built-in rows are **Summary**, **Call tags**, **Contact tags** and **Note**. Turn off **Summary** and Tonet stops writing call summaries for your business.

To add a field of your own:

1. Click **＋ Add field**.
2. Pick a ready-made field, like **Disposition** or **Birthday**, or click **Create your own…**.
3. For your own field, type a **Name** and pick a type: **Text**, **Choice**, **Number**, **Date**, **Link** or **Yes / No**. For **Choice**, type each choice and press Enter. Tick **Show in call review** and **Required to finish a review** if you want them.
4. Click **Add field**.

A field goes on every contact, for your whole team. You can have up to 30. Use a field's **⋯** menu to edit, move or remove it.

What shows on the card while you're on a call is each person's own choice, in their [Profile](/profile).

## Choose how long recordings are kept

1. On the **Calls** tab, go to **Recordings**.
2. Pick a time in **Keep recordings for**: 1, 7, 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days, or **Forever**.
3. If the new time is shorter, Tonet asks first. Click **Delete older recordings**.

Recordings older than that are deleted within about 10 minutes. This can't be undone. Call history, notes and transcripts stay.

- New accounts keep recordings for 90 days.
- Some accounts can't go below 90 days. The card says so. Contact Tonet support to change that.
- If your plan charges for recording storage, keeping recordings for less time can lower that charge.

## Change your password

The **Security** tab changes your own password, the one you sign in with.

1. Type your **Current Password**.
2. Type a **New Password**, then type it again in **Confirm Password**.
3. Click **Update**.

A new password needs:

- At least 12 characters
- An uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number
- A symbol. Any symbol counts, like `-` `.` `!` or `#`. An underscore (`_`) doesn't.

The hint under **New Password** names only a few symbols, but any symbol works.

Changing your password doesn't sign out your other devices. After the change, Tonet opens **Profile › Security** and tells you how many other devices are still signed in, so you can sign out any you don't recognize. See [Profile](/profile#sign-out-of-another-device).

<Callout type="info">
  Never set a password because you sign in with Google or an emailed code? Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page to set one.
</Callout>

## Change AI settings

AI settings are in **Knowledge › Your business**. If **Knowledge** isn't in your left menu, they're on this page's **AI** tab instead. Both show the same settings. On **Knowledge › Your business**, everyone on your team can see them, but only owners and managers can change them or see **AI use**.

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **What Tonet knows about…** | A summary of your business that Tonet writes from your settings and calls. Ask Tonet uses it to answer questions about your business. |
| **What to listen for on calls** | Up to 20 topics you want to hear about, like "Asked About Financing". Tonet tags calls where one comes up and counts them. |
| **AI on this account** | The **Use AI on this account** switch, and what Tonet's AI reads |
| **AI use** | How much of this month's included AI credit is used, and **Daily limits** |

**Fix what Tonet knows.** Click the pencil next to any part, change it, and click **Save**. Tonet won't rewrite a part you edited; it shows **Edited**. To hand it back, click **Let Tonet update this**.

**Turn AI off.** Turn off **Use AI on this account**, then click **Turn off AI**.

- Summaries, tags and Ask Tonet stop for everyone on the account.
- Nothing from the account goes to the AI provider. Calls made while AI is off are never read by AI, even after you turn it back on.
- Recordings, transcripts and call history keep working.

Some accounts can't turn AI off. The switch then says **This account keeps AI on**. Contact Tonet support about it.

**What Tonet's AI reads:**

| Reads | Never reads |
|---|---|
| Recorded calls and voicemails | Your team chat, unless someone allows it in Ask Tonet |
| Texts with your customers | |
| Your settings, contacts, tasks and notes | |

Transcripts are made on Tonet's own servers. Summaries, tags and answers are written by an outside AI provider.

**Set daily limits.** Daily limits cap AI use per day, for the whole account and for each person. They reset at midnight Eastern. Under **AI use**, open **Daily limits** and click **Change the limits**. Each person's limit can't be more than the whole account's.
