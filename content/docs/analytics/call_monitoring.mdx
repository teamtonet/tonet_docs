Owners and managers can listen to a teammate's call while it happens, or whisper to the teammate without the other person hearing. Both start from **Live now** on the **Overview** page. There's nothing to set up.

## Live now

**Live now** sits at the top of **Overview** while teammates are on calls, with the number of calls next to it. When nobody is on a call, it isn't there.

Each call has a card with:

- an arrow for incoming or outgoing, and the other person's contact name or number
- how long the call has been going
- the teammate on the call, with their extension
- a box to leave the teammate a note
- **Listen in** and **Whisper**

Your own calls aren't on Live now. Live now shows calls made and taken in the Tonet dashboard and the [desktop app](/app), not calls on desk phones.

Administrators and regular users don't see Live now.

## Listen in on a call

1. Go to **Overview**.
2. On the call's card in **Live now**, click **Listen in**.

You hear both sides of the call. Nobody on the call hears you, and no beep plays when you start. A bar above the cards says **Currently Monitoring: Listening to** and the teammate's name.

To stop, click **Stop listening** on the card, or the red stop button on the bar. Listening also stops when the teammate's call ends.

## Whisper to your teammate

Click **Whisper** on the call's card. Your teammate hears you. The other person on the call doesn't. Click **Stop whisper** to stop.

<Callout type="info">
  You can't switch between Listen in and Whisper while one is on. Click **Stop listening** or **Stop whisper** first, then pick the other. While you're listening or whispering, the buttons on the other cards are off too.
</Callout>

Listening and whispering use the phone in your dashboard, like a call. Your phone has to be connected, and the tab has to stay open.

## Leave a note for your teammate

Type in **Add a note for** … on the call's card. Your teammate sees it on their call card while they talk. The note stays with the call: it shows in the transcript on the [call's page](/calls/call-page), at the moment you wrote it.

## Next step

After the call, open it on the [Calls](/analytics/call_logs) page to hear the recording, or [share it](/analytics/secure_call_sharing) for coaching.
