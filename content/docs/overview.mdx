Overview is the home page of the dashboard. It shows your calls, texts and tasks for today or this month, who's online, and what needs you next.

![The Overview page: recent calls and texts, people online and What Tonet noticed](/images/overview/dashboard-overview.webp)

Click **Overview** at the top of the left menu. Everyone has it.

| Part | What's there | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Live now** | Calls happening right now, with **Listen in** and **Whisper** | Owners and managers |
| **All communications** | Calls, texts and tasks for today or this month | Everyone, for what they can see |
| **Users** | How many people are online | Everyone |
| **What Tonet noticed** | Leads going cold, what callers keep saying, and coaching | Everyone. Regular users see their own. |
| **Upcoming** | Your tasks and reminders | Everyone |

## See today's or this month's activity

At the top of the page, click **Today** or this month's name. **Today** is the default.

- Today starts at midnight, and the month on the 1st, in your account's time zone.
- This browser remembers your choice.

Owners and managers also have **Team** and **User**:

| Choice | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Team** | All the calls and texts you can see |
| **User** | Only yours: calls on your extension and texts you sent |

## Read the activity list

**All communications** lists everything in the period, newest first, grouped by day. Each tab shows how many there are:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Everything below |
| **Calls** | Calls in and out |
| **Missed** | Incoming calls nobody picked up, and calls from a teammate to you that you missed |
| **Texts** | One line per text conversation per day |
| **Tasks** | Tasks and reminders for you and your teams |

- **Click a call** to open it right there: its summary, tags, recording and transcript, when it has them.
- **Click a text** to open the conversation in **Messages**.
- **Click a task** to open it.
- **Search:** click the magnifying glass and type a name, a number, or words said on a call.

The list shows 60 at a time. Click **Show … more** at the bottom for the next ones.

Regular users and administrators see the calls they can see: their own and their teams'. See [Roles](/users#roles).

## Listen in on a live call

**Owners and managers only.**

When someone on your team is on a call, **Live now** shows at the top of Overview, with the number of calls. It's not there when nobody else is on a call. It lists calls made or taken in the dashboard or the desktop app. Your own call isn't listed.

Each card shows:

- Whether the call is incoming or outgoing
- Who's on the other end: a contact's name, the number, or a teammate and their extension
- How long the call has been going
- Who on your team is on it

On a card:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Listen in** | Listen to the call |
| **Whisper** | Talk to your teammate during the call |
| **Stop listening** or **Stop whisper** | Ends it |

Both use the phone in your dashboard. You can listen in or whisper on one call at a time; the other buttons wait until you stop. While you're on, a **Currently Monitoring** bar says who you're listening or whispering to, with a stop button.

**Send your teammate a note:** type in **Add a note for …** and press **Enter**. They see it on their call as you type, and it's saved with the call. Press **Esc** to take back a note you haven't sent.

## See who's online

The **Users** card shows how many people are online right now, out of everyone on the account, like **3 / 8 online**. Online means their status is **Available** and Tonet is open on one of their devices. See [Set your status](/profile#set-your-status).

Owners, managers and admins can click **See all Users** to open [Users](/users).

## See what Tonet noticed in your calls

**What Tonet noticed** points out things in your calls worth acting on. It checks for leads going cold every day, and for patterns and coaching every week.

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Leads going cold** | Someone who showed interest on a call in the last 30 days, like asking for a quote or a call back, and hasn't been called or texted for 7 days. A lead with an open task isn't listed. |
| **What callers keep saying** | Questions, requests, objections, competitors, complaints and lost sales that came up with at least 3 different callers this week |
| **Coaching** | Each person's strongest habit and biggest gap on this week's calls, compared with the rest of the team. Tonet checks five habits: opening with an introduction, asking about needs, answering objections, proposing a next step, and confirming details back. |

Each item says what Tonet saw, and often what to **Try**. On an item:

- Click **See the call** to read the calls behind it, then **Open call** for one.
- On a lead, click **Call back** to call them, or **Create task** to do it later.
- Click **×** to dismiss it.

Each section shows 3 items. Click **Show … more** for the rest.

**Who sees what:** owners, managers and admins see items for the whole team. Regular users see their own leads, their own coaching under **Your calls this week**, and **What callers keep saying**. Everyone sees only items built from calls they're allowed to open.

What Tonet noticed is built from your calls. Accounts that chose no AI or no call recording when they signed up see "AI is off for this account." here instead.

## Keep track of your tasks

**Upcoming** lists the tasks and reminders given to you or to a team you're on.

- **To do** shows what's open. **Done** shows what's finished.
- Tick a task's box to mark it done. Untick it to reopen it.
- Click a task to open it and change it.
- Click the calendar button to see the month. Days with something due have a dot. Click a day to see what's on it.

**Add a task:**

1. Click **+**.
2. Type **What needs to happen?**
3. If you like, click **Assign someone**, **Contact**, **Due date** or **Remind** to add them.
4. Click **Save**.

Owners and managers can give a task to anyone or to a team. Everyone else can only make tasks for themselves.

**Suggested from calls:** click it at the bottom of **Upcoming** to see follow-ups Tonet found in your calls. Click **Review** to turn one into a task, or **Dismiss** to drop it.
