Your profile holds the settings that are only yours: your name and photo, the devices you're signed in on, your notifications and your call card. Everyone can change their own, whatever their role.

**Where:** click your picture at the top right of the dashboard, then **Profile**.

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **General** | Your display name and profile picture |
| **Security** | **Signed-in devices**: everywhere you're signed in |
| **Notifications** | Pop-up notifications for calls, texts and voicemail |
| **Call card** | What your screen shows during a call |

Settings for the whole business, like the time zone, are in [Account](/account). Only owners and managers can open it.

## Change your display name

1. Open **Profile**. You're on **General**.
2. Under **Basic Details**, type a new **Display Name**, up to 100 characters.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Your **Email Address** and **Extension** are shown, but you can't change them in your profile. See [Users](/users#change-what-a-person-can-use).

## Add a profile picture

1. On **General**, click **Upload Photo**.
2. Pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP image, up to 5 MB.
3. Zoom and drag until it looks right, then click **Save**.

It saves right away. Your picture shows at the top right of the dashboard, with a dot for your status. To change it, upload another one.

## Set your status

Your status tells your team whether you're around.

1. Click your picture at the top right.
2. Click your status, under your name and email.
3. Pick one:

| Status | What your team sees |
|---|---|
| **Available** | **Available** while Tonet is the window you're using, **Away** while you're in another window, and **On a call** during a call |
| **Away** | **Away**, until you change it |
| **Busy** | **Busy**, so they know you're occupied |
| **Appear offline** | You as offline, to keep your availability private |

When Tonet isn't open on any of your devices, you show as offline.

## See where you're signed in

Open **Profile › Security**. **Signed-in devices** lists every browser and app where you're signed in, the most recently used first. Each row shows:

- The browser and system, like **Chrome on Windows**, or **Tonet desktop app**
- **This device** next to the one you're using now
- The country it signed in from, the day it signed in, and when it was last active

If Tonet support signed in to help with your account, that shows as **Tonet support**.

## Sign out of another device

1. On **Security**, find the device under **Signed-in devices** and click **Sign out**.
2. Click **Sign out** again to confirm.

That device loses access within 15 minutes. To use Tonet there again, it has to sign in again.

To sign out everywhere except where you are now, click **Sign out of all other devices**, then **Sign out all others**. Every other browser, app and Tonet support session has to sign in again.

<Callout type="warn">
  Don't recognize a device? Sign it out. If someone may know your password, sign out and use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page to set a new one. Setting a new password that way also signs you out of every other device.
</Callout>

## Turn on notifications

Tonet can pop up a notification on your computer when a call, text or voicemail comes in. It works while Tonet is open in your browser, even in a tab you're not looking at.

1. Open **Profile › Notifications**.
2. Under **Status**, check **Permission**. If it doesn't say **granted**, click **Request Permission** and allow notifications when your browser asks.
3. Under **Notification Types**, turn on the ones you want. All three start on.
4. Click **Test Call**, **Test SMS** or **Test Voicemail** to see a sample.

| Switch | Notifies you about |
|---|---|
| **Incoming Calls** | Calls ringing you, and calls you missed |
| **Text Messages** | Texts from customers, and messages from your team |
| **Voicemail** | New voicemails |

- The switches are saved in this browser only. Set them again on each computer or browser you use.
- While you're on **Messages**, texts and team messages don't pop up.
- In the [desktop app](/app), you can pause text, chat and voicemail notifications for an hour from the tray. Calls still ring.

<Callout type="info">
  **Permission says denied?** Notifications for Tonet are blocked in your browser, so allow them in the browser itself. In Chrome, click the site information icon in the address bar and turn on **Notifications**.
</Callout>

## Choose what your call card shows

During a call, a card shows who you're talking to. You choose what else it shows. Your choice is only for you, and it follows you to every computer and the desktop app.

1. Open **Profile › Call card**.
2. Under **Show on my calls**, tick what you want on every call.
3. To start over, click **Back to the default**.

![Profile › Call card: the fields you can show on your calls, like Probably calling about and Live transcript](/images/profile/call-card-fields.webp)

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Tags** | A saved contact's tags. Click **+** to add one. |
| **Probably calling about** | What they're likely calling about: something promised on an earlier call, an open task, or the last call |
| **Your business's fields** | Your business's own contact fields, filled in during the call. Pick each field under it. |
| **Live transcript** | What's being said, line by line. See [the live call view](/calls/live-call#on-the-small-card). |
| **Note box** | A box to write a note during the call, or ask Tonet about it |
| **Mic and caller levels** | How loud your microphone and the caller are |
| **Where they are, their time** | Their company, where they are, and the time there |
| **Open work** | Open tasks with this person |
| **Last conversations** | Your last call with them, or a note that this is their first call |
| **Contact details** | Their email, other numbers, who they usually talk to, and the latest note |
| **Look up caller** | For a number that isn't a contact, **Look up caller · 5¢** finds who it is. See [Look up a number](/contacts#look-up-a-number). |

You can also change it during a call: click **▤** (**Choose what your call card shows**) on the card, or hover a field and click its **×** to hide it. Click **Undo** right after if you hid the wrong one.

**Probably calling about**, **Open work** and **Last conversations** are read from your past calls. Accounts that chose no AI or no call recording when they signed up don't get them.
