Your voicemails are on the **Calls** page, on the call each one was left on. Click **Unheard voicemail** to see new ones. Tonet writes every message out as text and can email it to you.

## Change your voicemail greeting

**Record it by phone:**

1. From your Tonet line, dial **\*865**. That works from the dashboard, the desktop app or your desk phone. In the [phone app](/phone-app), voicemails play right in **Home › call history › Voicemails**.
2. Your voicemail menu answers. It doesn't ask for a PIN.
3. Follow the voice prompts to record your greeting.

Shortcut: on the **Calls** page, open **⋯**, click **Voicemail greeting and email**, then **Dial Voicemail to Record**. It dials \*865 for you.

**Or upload a recording:**

1. On the **Calls** page, open **⋯** and click **Voicemail greeting and email**.
2. Under **Select Mailbox**, pick your mailbox or the mailbox of a team you're on. A mailbox that has its own greeting shows **Has Greeting**.
3. Click **Choose Audio File** and pick a WAV, MP3 or OGG file of 10 MB or less.
4. Click **Upload Greeting**.

Under **Current Greeting**, **Listen** plays the greeting and **Delete** removes it. A mailbox with no greeting of its own plays the standard one.

Callers hear your greeting when you don't answer. \*865 opens only your own mailbox, so a team's greeting is changed by uploading a file.

## Listen to voicemail

1. Go to **Calls** and click **Voicemail**, or **Unheard voicemail** for new messages.
2. Click the voicemail button on the call. It shows the message's length, and it's filled in while the message is unheard.

- Listening marks a message heard. **Mark heard** does it without listening.
- The call's own page shows the voicemail too, under **They left a voicemail**, with **Call back**, **Mark heard** and **Delete**.
- The bell's red number counts unheard voicemails until they're heard. Its **unheard voicemails · open** link opens the **Unheard voicemail** list.

## Read voicemail as text

Tonet transcribes every voicemail. The text shows in the call's details, on the call's page and in the voicemail email, and on the call's row when the call has no summary. While Tonet works, it says **Transcribing…**.

## Get voicemail by email

**Voicemail to Email** is on for everyone to start. Each email comes from noreply@tonet.ai, titled **New Voicemail from** and the caller ID. It shows who called, how long the message is, when it came in (in your account's time zone) and the text, with the recording attached as a WAV file.

To turn it off or on, open **Calls › ⋯ › Voicemail greeting and email** and use the **Voicemail to Email** switch. It's your own setting.

A team's voicemail goes by email to every member of the team who has Voicemail to Email on.

## Who sees which voicemails

Everyone sees only:

- their own mailbox, and
- the mailbox of each [team](/groups) they're on.

That includes owners and managers. To see a team's voicemail, be on the team. Each team has its own shared mailbox.

## Delete voicemail

- **One message:** on the **Calls** page, open the call's details and click **Delete voicemail**, or click **Delete** on the call's page. The call stays in the list. A deleted message can't be brought back.
- **Old messages:** with **Voicemail** or **Unheard voicemail** picked, click **Delete N heard voicemails older than 30 days**. The emails you already got aren't affected.
- **Many at once** (owners, managers and admins): click **Select**, tick the calls, then **Delete N voicemails**.

A mailbox holds up to 1,000 messages, and each message can be up to 2 minutes long.
