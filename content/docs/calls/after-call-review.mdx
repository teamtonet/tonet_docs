The call review is where a call's notes, tags and Tonet's summary go. You start it during the call with **Notes** on the call card, and finish it when you hang up. It's saved with the call and shows on the [call's page](/calls/call-page).

Only answered calls get a review.

## What's in a review

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Tonet writes it after the call. Click **Edit** to change it. **Use Tonet's** puts Tonet's back. |
| **Call tags** | Words for this call, like "booking" or "follow up". Dashed ones are Tonet's suggestions: tap one to keep it, or × to hide it. |
| **Contact tags** | Words for the person, like "VIP". They stay on the contact and show on every call with them. |
| Your business's fields | Your account's own fields, like a booking date, if it added any |
| **Call note** | Your own words about the call |

Changes save as you type. After the call, the bottom of the review has **Read transcript**, **Full call** (opens the call's page), **+ Task**, and **Block number**. If Tonet thinks the caller was a robocaller, the review says so and offers **Block number** up front.

## When the review shows

**During a call:** click **Notes** on the call card. Jot down what matters and tag the call while you talk. The card says **Your notes stay with this call after hangup.**

**After the call:** it depends on the account's review mode.

| Mode | After an answered call |
|---|---|
| **Optional** (the default) | A review you opened stays open. Others wait until Tonet's summary is ready, then show in **Recent calls** at the bottom left with a **Review** button. Rows you haven't touched go away after 10 seconds. Hover over the list to keep it. A call with nothing to review, like a very short one, doesn't show. |
| **Required** | The review opens when the call ends and stays until someone clicks **Done**. |
| **Off** | No review after calls, and no **Notes** button. Notes already saved stay. |

**Any time later:** open the call's page. The **Call review** box shows the summary, tags, next steps and note, with **Edit**.

The call card's review is for calls in the dashboard and the [desktop app](/app). For a call on a desk phone, write the review on the call's page.

## Finish a review

- **Done for now** (Optional) or **Done** (Required) saves the review and closes it.
- If something is marked Required and still empty, the card says what's missing, like **Add a call tag to finish**, and **Done** waits.
- If Tonet's summary isn't ready yet, Tonet asks **Save and close while AI is pending?** Click **Save and close** to keep your notes. Tonet's summary won't replace what you wrote.
- In Optional mode, **Keep open** keeps the review on screen, and × closes it without finishing.

## Who can write a review

Only people who were on the call can write its review. Once someone saves it, only they can change it. Everyone who can see the call can read it.

## Set the review mode

Owners and managers:

1. Go to **Manage › Account** and open the **Calls** tab.
2. Under **Call review**, pick:

| Mode | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Required** | It stays until someone presses Done |
| **Optional** | Untouched ones fold into Recent calls |
| **Off** | No review after calls |

The change saves right away. A preview beside the settings shows how the card and review will look.

## Choose what the review shows

On the same tab, **What a call review shows** lists each part of the review:

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| **In review** | The part shows in the review |
| **Required** | **Done** waits for it |
| **AI fills** | Tonet fills it from the call |

- **Summary**: turn off **In review** and Tonet stops writing summaries.
- **Call tags**, **Contact tags** and **Note** can each be shown or hidden. Tags can also be required.
- Click **Add field** to add your own field, like a booking date, and choose whether the review shows it or requires it.

Under **Tonet AI, for tags and fields**, pick what Tonet does with what it learns from a call:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Suggest them** (the default) | Tonet's tags wait, dashed. You tap one to keep it. |
| **Fill them in** | Tonet adds its tags, marked Tonet, and you remove wrong ones. Up to 3 call tags and 2 contact tags a call; any more stay suggestions. |

What each person sees on their own call card while talking is their choice. See [Your call card](/dialer#your-call-card-and-notes).
