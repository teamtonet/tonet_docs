Every call has its own page with the recording, what was said, Tonet's summary and how the call went. Open it from [Calls](/analytics/call_logs): click → in the **Table**, or **Open call** in a call's details. Its address is dashboard.tonet.ai/calls/ followed by the call's ID.

- **At the top**: **← Calls**, one line saying what happened, then the date, time, line and talk time.
- **Buttons**: **Call** (or **Call back** after a missed call), **Share**, and **⋯** for downloads and **Report a problem**.
- **On the left**: the recording, and the transcript under it.
- **On the right**: the **Call review** with Tonet's summary, the voicemail if one was left, **Who this is**, and **How the call got there**.

Owners and managers can open every call. Administrators and regular users can open their own calls and their teams' calls. Anyone else gets **Could not load this call**.

## Move to the next call

Use ‹ and › next to **← Calls** to go to the newer or older call. Click the count in the middle (like **3 of 16**) to choose which calls to step through: **Mine** or **Everyone**, and the **Day**, **Week** or **Month** of this call.

## Play the recording

- Click ▶ to play. Click the speed (**1×**) for 1.5× or 2×.
- The two bars show who talked when: your side on top, theirs below, grey while the phone system (a greeting, menu or queue) was talking. Click anywhere on them to play from there.
- Next to the names, each side's share of the talking.
- Marks above the bars point at moments: a next step, a price said, a transfer, or both talking at once. Hover over one to see it. Click it to play from just before.
- **Line good**, **Line fair** or **Line poor** says how the connection was. Hover over it for details. When it wasn't good, a **Connection** box shows loss, jitter and delay. Click **What it means**.

Keys: **Space** plays or pauses, **←** and **→** move 5 seconds, **J** and **K** jump line by line.

If a call has no recording, the page says so where the player would be.

## Read and search the transcript

The transcript shows each side's words with their times. As the call plays, the line you hear is lit up and the transcript follows it. Scroll and it stops following. Click **Follow** to pick it back up.

- Click a line to play from there.
- Type in **Find in this call** to find words. It says how many were found, or **Not said**.
- Click the copy icon to copy the transcript.

Card numbers, social security numbers and PINs said on the call are hidden in the transcript and in what you copy or download. Phone numbers stay.

A transcript is made from the recording after the call, so calls with no recording have none.

## Comment on a moment

Hover over a line and click **Comment**. Type what the team should know about that moment. Type @ to mention someone. The people on the call and anyone you mention get a notification in the bell. Comments, and notes written during the call, show in the transcript at the moment they're about.

## Translate the transcript

Pick a language in **Translate**, above the transcript:

English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Tagalog, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese, German or Swedish.

The translation shows line by line, with **Translated by Tonet AI**. Click a line to play from there. **Show the original** switches back. The call's own language is marked **· original**.

Translation uses Tonet AI, so it needs Tonet AI on for your account.

## Read Tonet's summary

The **Call review** box shows Tonet's summary of the call, marked **AI draft**. It reads **Edited** once someone changes it.

- **Sources** lists times in the call. Click one to jump to that part of the transcript.
- Tags from the call show under the summary.
- **Next steps** lists what was agreed. **+ Task** makes a task from one. When a time was said, **+ Calendar** adds it as an event.
- **Note** is the note from the call's review.
- **Edit** opens the review to change it. Only people who were on the call can write the review. Once someone saves it, only they can change it.

While Tonet works, the box says **Tonet is writing the summary…**. Summaries need a recording with enough conversation and Tonet AI on for your account. See [After-call review](/calls/after-call-review).

## The rest of the page

| Box | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **They left a voicemail** | The voicemail on this call, with its text. **Call back**, **Mark heard** and **Delete**. Only for mailboxes you can see. |
| **Who this is** | Their saved contact, or **Not saved** with **Save as contact** |
| **How the call got there** | Each step: the line it came in on, who it was sent to, who answered and how fast, transfers, forwarding, voicemail, and when it ended |

## Share the call

Click **Share**. Tonet makes a public link and copies it. Anyone with the link can listen and read without a login. The button then reads **Shared**, with **Copy public link** and **Stop**. See [Share a call](/analytics/secure_call_sharing).

## Download or report a call

Open **⋯**:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy team link** | Copies this page's address. It opens only for people on your account who can see the call. |
| **Download recording** | Saves the audio. Only on recorded calls. |
| **Download transcript** | Saves the transcript as a text file, with times and names |
| **Report a problem** | Sends the call to the Tonet team to look at. It then reads **Reported**, or **Reported by** and how many people. |
