The live call view puts your call on a full page. What's said shows as a live transcript, Tonet fills in the call's details, and answers to the caller's questions show up beside them. There's nothing to set up.

<LiveCallDemo />

## Open the live call view

Calls open in it by themselves: it's how every account takes calls, unless you picked the small card. A call you place, or one ringing in, waits as a tab in the top bar until it's answered.

- On the small call card, click the expand icon (**Full page**).
- **‹ Dashboard** puts your calls in the top bar as tabs while you use the rest of Tonet. Hover over a tab for the last lines, Tonet's newest notes, **Mute**, **Hold** and **End**. Click **Open the call** to go back.
- **⋯** › **Small card** switches to the small card. Your next calls open the way you last picked.

## What's on the page

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Left** | Who it is, then **This call**: **Calling about**, **Promised**, **Quote**, **Next step**, **When** and **Call tags** |
| **Middle** | What's said, line by line. Scroll up to read back; **Back to now** catches up. |
| **Right** | Tonet's answers and your team's notes, level with the line they belong to |
| **Bottom** | The box to ask Tonet or write a note, then **Mute**, **Hold**, **Keypad**, **Transfer**, **Text** … and **End** |

**✦ Tonet fills these as you talk · × removes**: what Tonet heard is marked ✦. Click × to remove it, or click any value to type your own. Contact details you type save to the contact right away. A number with no contact gets one.

## Answers beside the caller's question

When the caller asks something your [Knowledge](/knowledge) answers, the answer shows beside their question, with where it came from. One Tonet isn't sure of says **worth checking**.

When Knowledge has no answer, Tonet says **Looking it up…** and checks what it knows about your business. For questions about the world outside it, like another company's prices or a public rule, it also searches the web. The answer is marked **Tonet AI · worth checking**, with links to its pages. Tonet looks things up at most 5 times a call, and never searches the web about the caller's own order, account or appointment.

## Ask Tonet or write a note

Type in the box at the bottom (**Ask Tonet, write a note, or say “text them …”**):

- **A question**, ending in ? or starting with words like what, how or can: Tonet answers on the right.
- **Anything else** is a note: **Your note · your team sees it**. Notes stay in the transcript on [the call's page](/calls/call-page).
- **"text them …"**: Tonet writes the text and shows it with **Undo**. After 5 seconds it's sent from the call's line. To write your own, click the text icon at the bottom.

To ask about something said, click the line or select some words, then click **✦ Ask Tonet**. The answer goes beside it, marked **Tonet AI · you asked about this**.

## Transfer with a note

1. Click **Transfer** at the bottom.
2. In **Find a teammate, team, contact or number**, pick who. Teammates show **Free**, **On a call**, **Busy**, **Away** or **Offline**.
3. For a teammate or team, Tonet writes a short note from the call: who's calling, what they need, what was promised. It says **Tonet wrote this · edit**. Change it if you like.
4. Click:
   - **Talk to** … **first** (teammates only): the caller goes on hold and you call your teammate to fill them in.
   - **Send** … **now**: the call goes straight to them.

The note shows on your teammate's ringing card and stays on their call card. A contact or typed number gets the call with no note.

## After the call

The page shows **Call ended**, Tonet's summary and the next step. Click **Edit the summary** to change it.

Then Tonet checks the call against its recording (**Tonet checks it in about 40 s**). It corrects what it filled in, says why, and never changes what you typed. Each change has **Undo**, or click **Undo all**, here or later on the call's page.

You don't have to click anything. The call's tab goes by itself, unless your pointer is on it or you're typing.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Skip Tonet’s check** | Saves it as it is. Tonet won't check it against the recording. |
| **Done** | Closes it now. What you typed is saved, and Tonet still checks it. |

If your account's call review is **Required**, or a required tag or field is empty, the tab waits for **Done**. See [After-call review](/calls/after-call-review).

## On the small card

**Live transcript** shows the newest line. Click **Open** for the whole call and its notes. Type in **Add a note…**, or click ✦ first to ask Tonet about the call.

To hide them, untick **Live transcript** or **Note box** in [Profile](/profile) › **Call card**. Managers still see the transcript.

## What managers see

Owners and managers see each teammate's call in **Live now** on **Overview**, under **Live transcript and notes**.

- Type in **Add a note for** … to write to your teammate. It shows on their page as you post it. Click ✦ first to ask Tonet instead.
- Click the expand icon (**Full page**) to read along on your teammate's page. You can't end, hold, transfer or text from it. **Esc** goes back.
- **Listen in** and **Whisper** work there too. See [Listen in on calls](/analytics/call_monitoring).

## Where it works

| Calling from | Live call view and live transcript |
|---|---|
| The dashboard in your browser | Yes |
| The [desktop app](/app) | Yes |
| A desk phone, or the [phone app](/phone-app) | No. A recorded call gets its transcript and summary after it ends. |

A line set not to record gets no transcript: **This line doesn’t record calls, so there’s no transcript.** With Tonet AI off for your account, the live transcript still works, but Tonet doesn't fill, answer or write.
