You pick a number, sign in, tell Tonet how you'll use it, and add credit. It takes a few minutes. There is no contract.

Start at [dashboard.tonet.ai/start](https://dashboard.tonet.ai/start).

## The plans

| | Pay as you go | Unlimited |
|---|---|---|
| Each person on calls | $10 a month (33¢ a day) | $15 a month (50¢ a day) |
| Calls to and from US and Canada numbers | 1¢ a minute | Included |
| Texts | 3¢ each | 3¢ each |
| Each phone number | $2.50 a month | $2.50 a month |

- Texts are not included in either plan.
- Calls that come in on a toll-free number are charged by the minute on both plans.
- You pay by the day from your credit. See [Billing](/billing) for how charges work.

Which plan fits? Tonet suggests Pay as you go if you mostly take calls, and Unlimited if you make calls or do both. To change plans later, ask support at [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).

## Sign up, step by step

1. **Pick your number.** Choose **Local** or **Toll-free**. For a local number, type a state, city, area code or ZIP, then pick a number. Tonet holds it for you for 15 minutes.
2. **You.** Click **Continue with Google**, or type your email. Tonet emails you a sign-in link and a 6-digit code. Open the link or type the code. Rather have a password? Click **Use a password**.
3. **Calls & team.** Answer two questions: **How will you use Tonet?** (Making calls, Taking calls, or Both) and **Who's calling?** (Just me, 2–5, 6–20, or 21+). Check your **Business name** and **Time zone**.
4. **Pay.** Choose a plan and how many people will be on calls. Pick your credit: $15, $25, $50, $100, or another amount. The minimum is $15. Then pay by **Card** or **Crypto**.

Some people also see a **Preferences** step after picking a number. It lets you turn call recording, transcripts, call summaries and tags, voicemail as text, and the daily spam-label check on or off.

<Callout type="info">
  Your credit is not a fee. It goes into your balance and pays for your number and your first days. The Pay step shows how many days it covers.
</Callout>

## After you pay

Tonet buys your number and builds your phone system. This takes about a minute. When you see **You're live**, click **Open Tonet**.

Try calling your own cell phone first to hear how it sounds.

Some new accounts are checked by hand before the line goes live. If that happens, you'll see **Thanks, your payment is in.** It usually takes a few hours, and Tonet emails you when it's ready.

<Callout type="info">
  Running out of time on the 15-minute hold? Click **Keep it 15 more minutes**. If the hold runs out, pick the number again or choose another.
</Callout>

## Have a code?

On the Pay step, enter a partner, promo or team code in the **Have a code?** box and click **Apply**. The box says what the code gives you.
