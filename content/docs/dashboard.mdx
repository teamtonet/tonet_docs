The dashboard at [dashboard.tonet.ai](https://dashboard.tonet.ai) is where you make calls, read texts and run your phone system. The [start page](/#where-things-are) lists what's in the left menu. This page explains everything around it.

## Phones and Team

Under the menu, **Phones** lists every number on the account, and **Team** lists everyone on it. You're at the top of **Team**, marked **(you)**. Below you: who's available, then on a call or busy, then away, then offline.

The **+** buttons sit beside each heading. The others show when you hover over a row.

| Button | What it does | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Add Phone** (**+** beside **Phones**) | Opens the form to [buy a number](/phones#buy-a-number) | Owners and managers |
| **Edit Phone** (pencil) | Opens the number's **Route & settings** | Owners and managers |
| **View Timeline** | Opens the number's **Activity**: its calls and texts | Everyone |
| **Add User** (**+** beside **Team**) | Opens the form to [add a person](/users#add-a-person) | Owners and managers |
| **Call …** (phone) | Calls that teammate's extension from the dashboard | Everyone |
| **Message …** | Opens a team chat with them. On your own row it's **Notes to yourself**. | Everyone |

With no numbers yet, or nobody else on the team, owners and managers see a **+** under the heading to add the first one.

Click a person's picture or name for their card: their extension, status, the numbers they can use, and **Call** and **Message**. Owners, managers and admins also see their role, email, teams, last call and this week's calls, with **Open page**. Owners and managers also see what they've cost this month.

The dot on each picture is that person's [status](/profile#set-your-status):

| Dot | Status |
|---|---|
| Green, with a tick | **Available** |
| Yellow, with a clock | **Away** |
| Red, with a line | **Busy** |
| Red, with a phone | **On a call** |
| Grey, with an × | **Offline** |

Click **Phones** or **Team** to fold the list away, and again to open it. Drag the bar between two sections to give one more room. This browser remembers both.

## The bottom of the left menu

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Report an issue** | Opens a form to tell us about a problem. See below. |
| Moon or sun | Switches to **Dark mode** or **Light mode**. Tonet starts light, and this browser remembers your choice. |

On a phone, both are at the bottom of **More**.

**Report an issue** asks:

| In the form | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Where did it happen?** | **Dashboard in a browser**, **Desktop app**, **iPhone app**, **Android app** or **Desk phone** |
| **Version** | Filled in for you. Click **Change** if it happened somewhere else. |
| **Your email** | Where we reply |
| **When** | **Just now** to start. Change it if it was earlier. |
| **What happened?** | What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead |
| **Screenshot or recording** | Optional. Drop, paste or choose an image or video, up to 8 MB. |
| **Include technical details** | Ticked to start. Sends the app and dashboard version, your system, recent errors on the page and your account. No call audio or messages. |

Click **Send report**. You get a report number, and we reply by email. Reports that help us fix a real problem may earn a bonus.

## The top bar

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Menu button (☰), top left | Shrinks the left menu to icons, or opens it out again. This browser remembers it. |
| ‹ and › | **Back** and **Forward**. Only in the [desktop app](/app); in a browser, use the browser's own. |
| Clock | Your account's time. Owners and managers can click it to change the [time zone](/account#change-your-time-zone-or-business-address). |
| A meeting | A meeting you're in, or one starting within 15 minutes, with **Join**, **Back** or **Open**. See [Meetings](/meetings). |
| Call tabs | Calls you set aside with **‹ Dashboard** while you use the rest of Tonet. See [the live call view](/calls/live-call). |
| Dialpad icon | Opens the phone. See [Make and take calls](/dialer). A red line across it means your phone isn't connected. |
| Line status | Shows only when this tab isn't ringing: **Phone is on in another tab**, **Phone moved to …** or **Not ringing here · your line is full**. See [If calls ring in another tab](/dialer#if-calls-ring-in-another-tab). |
| Signal bars | Your connection to Tonet. Click it to run a network test. |
| Bell | Missed calls, voicemails and messages from the last 7 days |
| Question-mark speech bubble (**Contact Support**) | Opens **How can we help?**: Ask Tonet, email support, or these docs |
| ✦ | Ask Tonet's replies. Shows on the Messages page, and when you have unread replies |
| Balance | Opens [Billing](/billing). Owners and managers only. |
| **More controls** (⋯) | On a smaller screen: the clock, your balance and **Contact support**. See below. |
| Your picture | Your status, [Profile](/profile), Usage Data, Billing (owners and managers), [Refer & earn](/refer), [Enable click to call](/features/click_to_call), and Logout |

When we update Tonet, a line above the page says **A new version of Tonet is ready.** Click **Reload**. If you don't, the tab reloads by itself the next time you come back to it, unless you're in the middle of something.

## On a laptop or a phone

**On a smaller screen**, like a laptop, the clock, **Contact Support** and your balance move into **More controls** (⋯) in the top bar. It holds **Account time**, **Balance** (owners and managers) and **Contact support**.

**In a phone's browser**, or a very narrow window, the left menu becomes a tab bar at the bottom: **Overview**, **Calls**, **Messages**, **Contacts** and **More**.

- **More** opens everything else: **Account time**, **Balance** (owners and managers), **Contact support**, the rest of the menu, **Phones**, **Team**, **Report an issue**, and light or dark mode.
- The Tonet logo at the top left takes you to Overview.
- The tab bar hides inside a conversation and in a meeting.

<Callout type="warn">
  In a phone's browser, calls ring only while the Tonet tab is open. To get calls with the app closed or the phone locked, use the [phone app](/phone-app).
</Callout>
