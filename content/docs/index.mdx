| Menu item | What's there | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Today's or this month's calls and texts, and who's online | Everyone |
| **Manage** | Phones, Menus, Schedules, Calendar, Users and Teams | Owners, managers, admins |
| **Calls** | Calls and voicemails, with recordings | Everyone. Admins and regular users see their own and their teams' calls. |
| **Messages** | Texts with customers and chat with your team | Everyone |
| **Contacts** | Everyone you talk to, with notes | Everyone |
| **Knowledge** | What Tonet knows about your business: answers for callers, and where it learns them | Everyone |
| **Meetings** | Voice meetings with your team and guests, with Tonet AI's notes. See [Meetings](/meetings). | Everyone |
| **Analytics** | Answering, dialing and team numbers | Owners, managers, admins |

Owners and managers also see **Account** and **Billing** inside **Manage**.

**Manage** has its own menu. On a wide screen it sits beside the page. On a laptop it takes the left menu's place: click **Back** to leave Manage, or **Show the full sidebar** to see both. On a phone it's a row of buttons at the top of the page.

Every button in the menu and the top bar, and how the dashboard looks on a laptop or a phone: see [Find your way around the dashboard](/dashboard).

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything. Every account has one owner. |
| **Manager** | Everything, including Billing and Account settings |
| **Administrator** | Phones, menus, schedules, calendar, users and teams. No Billing or Account. |
| **Regular User** | Calls, messages and contacts. Sees their own calls and their teams' calls. |

Change someone's role in [Users](/users#change-a-role).

## Ways to take calls

A call to you rings everywhere you're signed in, at once. Answer wherever you are.

- **In the browser.** Nothing to install. Allow the microphone when asked.
- **The [phone app](/phone-app)** for [iPhone](/phone-app/iphone) and [Android](/phone-app/android). Rings on the lock screen, with your business number.
- **The [desktop app](/app).** Keeps your line on with the window closed. Linux now; Windows and Mac are coming.
- **A desk phone.** Any standard SIP phone works. See [IP Phones](/ip_phones).
- **Your cell number,** without the app. See [forward calls to a cell](/guides/forward-to-cell).
