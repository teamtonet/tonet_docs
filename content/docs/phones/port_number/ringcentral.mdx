To move a RingCentral number to Tonet, you need three details exactly as they appear in your RingCentral account: the **company name**, the **service address** and the name of a **Super Admin**. RingCentral doesn't issue personal port-out PINs. If a PIN is asked for, it says to use **0000**. Then fill in **Manage › Phones › Port a Number** in Tonet, and keep your RingCentral account active until the number has moved.

## What you need from RingCentral

| You need | Where to find it in the RingCentral Admin Portal |
|---|---|
| Company name | **Phone System › Company Info** |
| Service address | **Phone System › Company Info › Company Address**. With several sites, pick the site under **Company Info**. |
| A Super Admin's name | **Users › User List › Users with Extensions**. Filter **Roles** by **Super Admin**. Only Super Admins can authorize a port-out. |
| PIN | None. RingCentral doesn't give personal port-out PINs and says to use **0000** if one is asked for. |
| Your latest RingCentral bill | It must show the numbers you're moving. You attach it in Tonet. |

RingCentral says to give these details exactly as they're listed on your account.

## Pick Landline for RingCentral numbers

RingCentral says its numbers are classified as wireline, and it can't change that. In Tonet's form, pick **Landline** for each RingCentral number, or **Toll free** for a toll-free one.

## Is there a port-out lock?

RingCentral doesn't describe one. It has no personal port-out PIN: a Super Admin's name and the account details authorize the move.

## Does RingCentral charge to port out?

No. RingCentral doesn't charge a port-out fee. You still pay for RingCentral service until the port completes.

## Keep your RingCentral account active until the port finishes

- The numbers must be active with RingCentral for the port to go through.
- After the numbers have moved, contact RingCentral support to cancel your service and stop its charges.

<Callout type="warn">
  When a port-out starts, RingCentral swaps temporary numbers into your RingCentral account in place of the numbers that are moving. Any call handling rule that uses a moving number gets the temporary number instead. Before you submit, take the moving numbers out of your RingCentral call handling rules.
</Callout>

## Move the number to Tonet

Owners, managers and administrators can start a port.

1. Note your company name, company address and a Super Admin's name from the RingCentral Admin Portal.
2. In Tonet, go to **Manage › Phones** and open the **Port a Number** tab.
3. Fill in the form as shown below. Tonet fills in some fields from your Tonet account, so check each one against RingCentral.
4. Under **Proof the numbers are yours**, click **Attach a file** and add your latest RingCentral bill.
5. Read the **Authorization** text. The Super Admin you named signs in the box, then types their name in **Print your full name**.
6. Click **Submit porting request**.

| Field in Tonet | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **First name**, **Last name** | A Super Admin on your RingCentral account, exactly as listed there |
| **Organization** | Your company name, exactly as in RingCentral |
| **Street address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip code** | Your RingCentral company address, or the site's address |
| **E-mail address** | Where Tonet sends updates about this port |
| **Residential or business** | **Business** |
| **Current service provider** | RingCentral |
| **Account number** | RingCentral's port-out steps don't ask for one. The form doesn't require it for **Landline** numbers. |
| **Account PIN / passcode** | **0000** |
| **Telephone number** and **Type** | Each number you're moving. **Landline** for a local number, **Toll free** for a toll-free one. |

Put every RingCentral number you're moving in one request. Tonet handles one port request at a time. Once it's approved, click **Port another number** to start the next.

Not ready? Click **Save draft** and come back later.

## After you submit

The **Port a Number** tab shows where your request is:

<PortTimeline />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Tonet has your request and checks it before filing it with the carrier. |
| **In progress** | Your request is being processed. Most ports complete in 7 to 10 business days. |
| **More information needed** | Read the message under **Messages**, type your answer in **Your reply**, attach a file if asked, and click **Send reply**. The request goes back to **In progress**. |
| **Approved** | The numbers appear under **My Phone Numbers** once the transfer completes. |
| **Not approved** | Read the **Reason for rejection**, fix it, and click **Edit and resubmit**. |

Tonet emails the address in **E-mail address** when it receives your request (with a copy of the letter you signed), when it needs something from you, and when the request is approved or not approved.

Your number keeps working with RingCentral until the switch. The date can't be guaranteed.

When the number appears under **My Phone Numbers**, [choose where its calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go) and [who can use it](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number). To text from it, [register it for texting](/phones#texting-from-a-number).

## Why RingCentral ports get rejected

| Reason | How to fix it |
|---|---|
| Company name or address doesn't match | Copy them exactly from **Company Info** in the RingCentral Admin Portal. Click **Edit and resubmit**. |
| The person named isn't a Super Admin | Use the name of a Super Admin, exactly as listed in RingCentral, and have them sign. |
| The number isn't active at RingCentral | RingCentral only releases active numbers. If you cancelled it, contact RingCentral support before you resubmit. |
| A PIN was required | Enter **0000** in **Account PIN / passcode**. |

More reasons and fixes: [Why was my port rejected?](/phones/port_number/rejected)
