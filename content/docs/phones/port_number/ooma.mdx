To move an Ooma number to Tonet, ask Ooma for the details a new provider needs: the **account number**, any **PIN**, and the **name and service address** exactly as Ooma has them. Ooma doesn't publish its port-out steps, so call Ooma before you start. Then fill in **Manage › Phones › Port a Number** in Tonet. Keep your Ooma service active until the number has moved.

## What you need from Ooma

| You need | Where to get it |
|---|---|
| Account number | Ask Ooma which account number to give a new provider for a port. |
| PIN | Ask Ooma whether your number needs a port-out PIN, and what it is. |
| Account holder's name and service address | Ask Ooma for them exactly as they're on your account. |
| Proof the number is yours | A recent Ooma bill or receipt, or a screenshot of your Ooma account showing the number. You attach it in Tonet. |

## Call Ooma first

| | Ooma Office (business) | Ooma Home (residential) |
|---|---|---|
| Ooma support | 866-939-6662, any time | 888-711-6662 |
| **Porting/Change Fee** on Ooma's rates page | Free | $39.99 per number |
| **Residential or business** in Tonet | **Business** | **Residential** |

When you call, ask Ooma:

- Which account number to give a new provider
- Whether the number needs a PIN, and what it is
- The name and service address on the account, exactly as Ooma has them
- Whether you owe anything, and whether there's a fee to port out

## Is there a port-out lock?

Ooma doesn't describe one. Ask Ooma when you call.

## Does Ooma charge to port out?

Ooma's rates page lists a **Porting/Change Fee**: free on Ooma Office, and $39.99 per number on Ooma Home. It doesn't say whether that fee covers moving a number away from Ooma. Ask Ooma before you start.

## Keep your Ooma service active until the port finishes

- Don't cancel Ooma before the number has moved.
- Ooma's terms say that once your service has ended, it can only port your numbers out if you gave it at least **30 days' notice** that you were leaving and taking them, completed the transfer paperwork, and paid any Ooma porting fee.
- Once the number works in Tonet, contact Ooma to cancel.

## Move the number to Tonet

Owners, managers and administrators can start a port.

1. Call Ooma and get your account number, PIN and account details.
2. In Tonet, go to **Manage › Phones** and open the **Port a Number** tab.
3. Fill in the form as shown below. Tonet fills in some fields from your Tonet account, so check each one against what Ooma told you.
4. Under **Proof the numbers are yours**, click **Attach a file** and add a recent Ooma bill or receipt, or a screenshot of your Ooma account showing the number.
5. Read the **Authorization** text. Sign in the box, then type your name in **Print your full name**.
6. Click **Submit porting request**.

| Field in Tonet | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **First name**, **Last name** | The account holder, exactly as Ooma has them |
| **Organization** | The business name on the account. Tonet fills in your Tonet business name. |
| **Street address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip code** | The service address on your Ooma account |
| **E-mail address** | Where Tonet sends updates about this port |
| **Residential or business** | **Business** for Ooma Office, **Residential** for Ooma Home |
| **Current service provider** | Ooma |
| **Account number** | The account number Ooma gave you |
| **Account PIN / passcode** | The PIN Ooma gave you |
| **Telephone number** and **Type** | Each number you're moving, with **Wireless or VoIP**. Click **Add another number** for more. |

The form needs an account number and a PIN for **Wireless or VoIP** numbers. If Ooma tells you there's no PIN, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) before you submit.

Put every Ooma number you're moving in one request. Tonet handles one port request at a time. Once it's approved, click **Port another number** to start the next.

Not ready? Click **Save draft** and come back later.

## After you submit

The **Port a Number** tab shows where your request is:

<PortTimeline />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Tonet has your request and checks it before filing it with the carrier. |
| **In progress** | Your request is being processed. Most ports complete in 7 to 10 business days. |
| **More information needed** | Read the message under **Messages**, type your answer in **Your reply**, attach a file if asked, and click **Send reply**. The request goes back to **In progress**. |
| **Approved** | The numbers appear under **My Phone Numbers** once the transfer completes. |
| **Not approved** | Read the **Reason for rejection**, fix it, and click **Edit and resubmit**. |

Tonet emails the address in **E-mail address** when it receives your request (with a copy of the letter you signed), when it needs something from you, and when the request is approved or not approved.

Your number keeps working with Ooma until the switch. The date can't be guaranteed.

When the number appears under **My Phone Numbers**, [choose where its calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go) and [who can use it](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number). To text from it, [register it for texting](/phones#texting-from-a-number).

## Why Ooma ports get rejected

| Reason | How to fix it |
|---|---|
| Name or address doesn't match | Use the name and service address exactly as Ooma has them. Ask Ooma if you're not sure. Click **Edit and resubmit**. |
| Wrong account number or PIN | Ask Ooma which account number and PIN to give a new provider, and send them to Tonet. |
| The Ooma service was cancelled before the port finished | Contact Ooma. Its terms only allow a port after cancellation if you gave 30 days' notice, completed the paperwork, and paid any porting fee. |

To send a new PIN or account number: if the request shows **More information needed**, send it under **Messages**. If it shows **Not approved**, click **Edit and resubmit**. Otherwise, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).
