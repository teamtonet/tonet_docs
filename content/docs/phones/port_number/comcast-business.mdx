To move a Comcast Business number to Tonet, you need your Comcast Business **account number** and your **account PIN**. For a Business Voice line, the account PIN is the **Voice Security PIN**. For a Comcast Business Mobile line, it's the mobile **security PIN**. Then fill in **Manage › Phones › Port a Number** in Tonet. Keep your Comcast Business service active until the number has moved.

## What you need from Comcast Business

| You need | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Account number | At the top-right corner of your Comcast Business bill. It's also in the welcome email Comcast sent after your installation. |
| Account PIN | Business Voice: see [Find your Business Voice Security PIN](#find-your-business-voice-security-pin). Comcast Business Mobile: see [Comcast Business Mobile lines](#comcast-business-mobile-lines). |
| Account holder's name and address | Your latest Comcast Business bill. Copy them exactly as Comcast has them. |
| Your latest bill | It must show the numbers you're moving. You attach it in Tonet. |

## Business Voice lines and mobile lines

| | Business Voice line | Comcast Business Mobile line |
|---|---|---|
| PIN | The Voice Security PIN | The mobile security PIN, sent by text |
| Where to get it | My Account or the Comcast Business app. Only the account's **Primary Manager** can see it. | My Account, under **Comcast Business Mobile** |
| What one PIN covers | The whole account | Every mobile line on the account |
| Lock to turn off | None described | [Number Lock](#turn-off-number-lock-on-mobile-lines) |
| **Type** in Tonet | **Landline** | **Wireless or VoIP** |

## Find your Business Voice Security PIN

Comcast gives every Business Voice, VoiceEdge Select, Business VoiceEdge and trunking account one account-level Security PIN. Only the **Primary Manager** can view or change it.

- **My Account:** on the **Phone** page, expand the **Account** menu, select **Manager Settings**, then **View Voice Security PIN**.
- **Comcast Business app:** on the **Phone** page, select **CPNI Security Settings**, then **Show**.

Good to know:

- Comcast mails the PIN to the billing address after installation.
- Lost it? Sign in to My Account, go to **Account Details**, select the **Security PIN** link, then **Resend Security PIN**. Comcast emails it if the account and the Primary Manager's email address are more than 30 days old. Otherwise it goes by post, so ask early.
- Trouble with the PIN? Comcast's Security PIN line is (800) 741-4141.

Comcast doesn't publish port-out steps for Business Voice lines. A Comcast employee on its business support forum says you need your account number and account PIN. If you're not sure which PIN to give, ask Comcast Business.

## Comcast Business Mobile lines

Get the security PIN in Comcast Business My Account:

1. Select **Comcast Business Mobile**. If asked, choose the service location.
2. Select **Devices**, then the device with the number you're moving.
3. Next to **Transfer your number, cancel this line**, select **Learn More** and follow the steps to get the PIN by text.

Good to know:

- The same security PIN works for every line on the account.
- When the number is active with Tonet, Comcast cancels that mobile line automatically. You don't need to call to cancel it.
- Still paying off the phone? You can keep paying monthly if you keep another Comcast Business service: Internet, TV or Voice. If you cancel them all, the rest of the device balance is due on your final mobile bill.

## Turn off Number Lock on mobile lines

Number Lock is free. While it's on, a Comcast Business Mobile number can't move to another provider. Turn it off for each line you're moving:

1. Sign in to My Account.
2. Under **Subscribed Services**, select **Comcast Business Mobile**. If asked, choose the service location.
3. Go to **Devices** and click **Turn off Number Lock**.

Comcast emails you when it's off.

## Does Comcast Business charge to port out?

Comcast Business's help pages don't list a port-out fee. If you end a service before its term is up, Comcast's business terms charge termination charges. Check your service order, or ask Comcast Business, before you cancel anything.

## Keep your Comcast Business service active until the port finishes

- Don't cancel your Business Voice service. A disconnected number has to be turned back on before it can move.
- Once your Business Voice number works in Tonet, contact Comcast Business about ending the voice service.
- Mobile lines end by themselves when the number is active with Tonet.

## Move the number to Tonet

Owners, managers and administrators can start a port.

1. Get your account PIN. For mobile lines, turn off Number Lock.
2. In Tonet, go to **Manage › Phones** and open the **Port a Number** tab.
3. Fill in the form as shown below. Tonet fills in some fields from your Tonet account, so check each one against your Comcast Business bill.
4. Under **Proof the numbers are yours**, click **Attach a file** and add your latest Comcast Business bill.
5. Read the **Authorization** text. Sign in the box, then type your name in **Print your full name**.
6. Click **Submit porting request**.

| Field in Tonet | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **First name**, **Last name** | The account holder, exactly as on your Comcast Business bill |
| **Organization** | The business name on the account. Tonet fills in your Tonet business name. |
| **Street address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip code** | The address on your Comcast Business bill |
| **E-mail address** | Where Tonet sends updates about this port |
| **Residential or business** | **Business** |
| **Current service provider** | Comcast Business |
| **Account number** | Your Comcast Business account number |
| **Account PIN / passcode** | Your Voice Security PIN for Business Voice lines, or your mobile security PIN for mobile lines |
| **Telephone number** and **Type** | Each number you're moving. **Landline** for a Business Voice number, **Wireless or VoIP** for a Comcast Business Mobile line. |

<Callout type="info">
  Moving Business Voice numbers and mobile lines together? Put one PIN in **Account PIN / passcode**, then list the other PIN, and the numbers it's for, under **Additional information**.
</Callout>

Put every Comcast Business number you're moving in one request. Tonet handles one port request at a time. Once it's approved, click **Port another number** to start the next.

Not ready? Click **Save draft** and come back later.

## After you submit

The **Port a Number** tab shows where your request is:

<PortTimeline />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Tonet has your request and checks it before filing it with the carrier. |
| **In progress** | Your request is being processed. Most ports complete in 7 to 10 business days. |
| **More information needed** | Read the message under **Messages**, type your answer in **Your reply**, attach a file if asked, and click **Send reply**. The request goes back to **In progress**. |
| **Approved** | The numbers appear under **My Phone Numbers** once the transfer completes. |
| **Not approved** | Read the **Reason for rejection**, fix it, and click **Edit and resubmit**. |

Tonet emails the address in **E-mail address** when it receives your request (with a copy of the letter you signed), when it needs something from you, and when the request is approved or not approved.

Your number keeps working with Comcast Business until the switch. The date can't be guaranteed.

When the number appears under **My Phone Numbers**, [choose where its calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go) and [who can use it](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number). To text from it, [register it for texting](/phones#texting-from-a-number).

## Why Comcast Business ports get rejected

| Reason | How to fix it |
|---|---|
| Number Lock is on | [Turn it off](#turn-off-number-lock-on-mobile-lines) for each mobile line, then resubmit. |
| Wrong PIN | Business Voice lines need the Voice Security PIN. Mobile lines need the mobile security PIN. Send Tonet the right one. |
| The service was cancelled or the number disconnected | Ask Comcast Business to turn the number back on, then resubmit. |
| Name, address or account number doesn't match | Copy them from your latest bill. The account number is at the top right. Or ask Comcast Business for your Customer Service Record (CSR). |

To send a new PIN: if the request shows **More information needed**, send it under **Messages**. If it shows **Not approved**, click **Edit and resubmit**. Otherwise, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).
