To move a T-Mobile number to Tonet, the **Primary Account Holder** turns off Port Out Protection on the line and makes a **Temporary Port Out PIN**. You also need your T-Mobile **account number**. Then fill in **Manage › Phones › Port a Number** in Tonet. Keep your T-Mobile service active until the number has moved.

## What you need from T-Mobile

| You need | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Account number | On your T-Mobile account or your bill |
| Temporary Port Out PIN | T-Mobile's port-out PIN. See [Get a T-Mobile Temporary Port Out PIN](#get-a-t-mobile-temporary-port-out-pin). |
| Account PIN or passcode | T-Mobile lists it with the other two as what to have ready. Keep it handy. |
| Account holder's name and address | Your latest T-Mobile bill. Copy them exactly as T-Mobile has them. |
| Your latest bill | It must show the numbers you're moving. You attach it in Tonet. |

## Get a T-Mobile Temporary Port Out PIN

On a personal account, only the **Primary Account Holder** can make this PIN. If that's not you, ask them to make it.

**In the T-Life app (postpaid).** Use your phone on T-Mobile's network, with Wi-Fi off.

1. Open T-Life and select **Account**.
2. Select the gear icon at the top right.
3. Select **Permission & controls**, then **Transfer PIN**.
4. Select **Create PIN**. Write down the PIN that appears.

**On T-Mobile.com (postpaid).** Use your phone's browser on T-Mobile's network, not a computer. Select your name, then **Profile › Permissions & controls › Transfer PIN**, and select **Create PIN**.

**Prepaid.** Sign in at T-Mobile.com, choose **My T-Mobile › My Profile**, select **Request Transfer PIN**, then **Get a Transfer PIN** if asked.

Good to know:

- The PIN doesn't work while Port Out Protection is on. [Turn it off](#turn-off-port-out-protection) first.
- Getting an error? T-Mobile says to use the phone that has the number, on its network, with Wi-Fi and any VPN (such as iCloud Private Relay) off.
- Need help with the PIN? Call T-Mobile at 1-800-937-8997.
- T-Mobile's help page doesn't say how long the PIN lasts. Make it right before you submit your port in Tonet.

## Get a PIN for a T-Mobile for Business account

Business accounts make the PIN for each line in Account Hub:

1. Sign in to Account Hub and go to **Manage Accounts**. Pick the account if you have more than one.
2. On the **Manage Lines** tab, open the line's **⋯** menu and select **Generate transfer PIN**.
3. Request and validate the one-time PIN, then select **Generate**.

The PIN appears with a transaction number. Can't do this? You may need more permissions from your Account Admin or Business Owner. T-Mobile for Business support: 1-800-375-1126.

| | Personal account | Business account |
|---|---|---|
| Who makes the PIN | The Primary Account Holder | An Account Hub user with permission |
| Where | T-Life, T-Mobile.com, or the prepaid site | Account Hub, one line at a time |

Government accounts must contact T-Mobile to start a transfer.

## Turn off Port Out Protection

Port Out Protection blocks a line from moving to another carrier. It's set line by line, and on a postpaid account only the Primary Account Holder can remove it.

- **T-Life:** select **Account › See Plans**, then **Manage add-ons**. Choose the line, untick **Port Out Protection**, select **Continue**, then **Agree & Submit**.
- **T-Mobile.com:** select **Account**, pick the line under **Lines and Devices**, then **Manage add-ons**. Under **Services**, untick **Port Out Protection**, select **Continue**, then **Agree & Submit**.
- **Prepaid:** change it on prepaid.t-mobile.com under your line's plans and services. Any prepaid line user can also ask T-Mobile to remove it, after an extra security check.

Repeat for each line you're moving.

## Does T-Mobile charge to port out?

T-Mobile's transfer page doesn't list a fee for moving a number away. Any early termination fee or balance in your contract is still yours to pay.

## Keep your T-Mobile service active until the port finishes

Don't cancel your T-Mobile service before the number has moved. Your number keeps working with T-Mobile until the switch.

## Move the number to Tonet

Owners, managers and administrators can start a port.

1. Turn off Port Out Protection and get your Temporary Port Out PIN.
2. In Tonet, go to **Manage › Phones** and open the **Port a Number** tab.
3. Fill in the form as shown below. Tonet fills in some fields from your Tonet account, so check each one against your T-Mobile bill.
4. Under **Proof the numbers are yours**, click **Attach a file** and add your latest T-Mobile bill.
5. Read the **Authorization** text. Sign in the box, then type your name in **Print your full name**.
6. Click **Submit porting request**.

| Field in Tonet | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **First name**, **Last name** | The account holder, exactly as on your T-Mobile bill |
| **Organization** | The business name on the account. Tonet fills in your Tonet business name. |
| **Street address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip code** | The address on your T-Mobile bill |
| **E-mail address** | Where Tonet sends updates about this port |
| **Residential or business** | **Residential** for a personal account, **Business** for a business account |
| **Current service provider** | T-Mobile |
| **Account number** | Your T-Mobile account number |
| **Account PIN / passcode** | Your Temporary Port Out PIN |
| **Telephone number** and **Type** | Each number you're moving, with **Wireless or VoIP**. Click **Add another number** for more. |

<Callout type="info">
  Moving several lines with a different PIN for each? Put one in **Account PIN / passcode**, then list each other number with its PIN under **Additional information**.
</Callout>

Put every T-Mobile number you're moving in one request. Tonet handles one port request at a time. Once it's approved, click **Port another number** to start the next.

Not ready? Click **Save draft** and come back later.

## After you submit

The **Port a Number** tab shows where your request is:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Tonet has your request and checks it before filing it with the carrier. |
| **In progress** | Your request is being processed. Most ports complete in 7 to 10 business days. |
| **More information needed** | Read the message under **Messages**, type your answer in **Your reply**, attach a file if asked, and click **Send reply**. The request goes back to **In progress**. |
| **Approved** | The numbers appear under **My Phone Numbers** once the transfer completes. |
| **Not approved** | Read the **Reason for rejection**, fix it, and click **Edit and resubmit**. |

Tonet emails the address in **E-mail address** when it receives your request (with a copy of the letter you signed), when it needs something from you, and when the request is approved or not approved.

Your number keeps working with T-Mobile until the switch. The date can't be guaranteed: T-Mobile decides when the port completes.

When the number appears under **My Phone Numbers**, [choose where its calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go) and [who can use it](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number).

## Why T-Mobile ports get rejected

| Reason | How to fix it |
|---|---|
| Port Out Protection is on | The Primary Account Holder [turns it off](#turn-off-port-out-protection) for each line. Then resubmit. |
| The PIN is wrong or no longer valid | Make a new Temporary Port Out PIN and send it to Tonet. |
| Your T-Mobile account PIN or passcode is needed | If Tonet asks for it under **More information needed**, send it there. |
| It's a government account | Contact T-Mobile to start the transfer first. |
| Name, address or account number doesn't match | Copy them from your latest bill, or ask T-Mobile for your Customer Service Record (CSR). |

To send a new PIN: if the request shows **More information needed**, send it under **Messages**. If it shows **Not approved**, click **Edit and resubmit**. Otherwise, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).

More reasons and fixes: [Why was my port rejected?](/phones/port_number/rejected)
