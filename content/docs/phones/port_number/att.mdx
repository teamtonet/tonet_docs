To move an AT&T wireless number to Tonet, you need your AT&T **account number** and a **Number Transfer PIN**. The PIN lasts 4 days on a personal account and 14 days on a business account, so get it just before you submit. Then fill in **Manage › Phones › Port a Number** in Tonet. Keep your AT&T service active until the number has moved.

## What you need from AT&T

| You need | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Account number | In your AT&T online account, under **Welcome back** on the account overview, or under **Account info** in your profile. On your bill, it's under the page number or issue date on any page. You can also dial 611 from your AT&T phone. |
| Number Transfer PIN | AT&T's name for a port-out PIN. See [Get your AT&T Number Transfer PIN](#get-your-att-number-transfer-pin). |
| Account holder's name and address | Your latest AT&T bill. Copy them exactly as AT&T has them. |
| Your latest bill | It must show the numbers you're moving. You attach it in Tonet. |

## Get your AT&T Number Transfer PIN

On a personal account, only people with primary online access to the account can get the PIN online or in the app.

- **Online:** go to your account profile, select **Settings**, scroll to **Transfer phone number**, and select **Request new PIN**. The PIN shows on screen.
- **AT&T app:** tap your profile icon, then **Settings**. Select **Manage or remove account**, tap **Settings**, scroll to **Transfer phone number**, and select **Request new PIN**.
- **From your AT&T phone:** call `*PORT` and follow the prompts. You need your account passcode. AT&T texts you the PIN.

Good to know:

- One PIN covers every number on the account.
- Asking for a new PIN cancels the old ones. If you already submitted your port, send Tonet the new PIN.
- Give Tonet the Number Transfer PIN, not your AT&T account passcode.
- If your account is suspended, get the PIN online or in the app. It can't be sent by text.

## Get a PIN for an AT&T business account

On a business account, only the primary user can get the PIN, and it lasts longer.

| | Personal account | Business account |
|---|---|---|
| Who can get the PIN | People with primary online access | Only the account's primary user |
| How | Online, the AT&T app, or `*PORT` | Online, or contact AT&T and a rep emails the PIN to you. The app and `*PORT` don't work for business accounts. |
| What one PIN covers | Every number on the account | Every number on the account |
| How long it lasts | 4 days | 14 days |

## AT&T Prepaid

AT&T Prepaid accounts don't use a Number Transfer PIN. To get your prepaid account number, call AT&T at 800-901-9878, or dial 611 from your AT&T phone. Tonet's form asks for a PIN for wireless numbers, so ask AT&T Prepaid which PIN or passcode to give a new provider.

## Turn off Wireless Account Lock

If Wireless Account Lock is on, AT&T won't transfer your number. To turn it off:

1. Open the AT&T app on a device that's active on your account.
2. Tap the person icon.
3. Select **Wireless account lock** and tap **Unlock the account**.

Only people with primary or secondary access to the account can turn it off. AT&T emails the primary account holder and texts each number on the account when the lock changes.

## Does AT&T charge to port out?

No. AT&T doesn't charge for the Number Transfer PIN or for moving your number.

## Keep your AT&T service active until the port finishes

Don't disconnect your AT&T service. If you disconnect it too soon, AT&T has to reactivate the number before the move can finish.

## Move the number to Tonet

Owners, managers and administrators can start a port.

1. Turn off Wireless Account Lock and get your Number Transfer PIN.
2. In Tonet, go to **Manage › Phones** and open the **Port a Number** tab.
3. Fill in the form as shown below. Tonet fills in some fields from your Tonet account, so check each one against your AT&T bill.
4. Under **Proof the numbers are yours**, click **Attach a file** and add your latest AT&T bill.
5. Read the **Authorization** text. Sign in the box, then type your name in **Print your full name**.
6. Click **Submit porting request**.

| Field in Tonet | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **First name**, **Last name** | The account holder, exactly as on your AT&T bill |
| **Organization** | The business name on the account. Tonet fills in your Tonet business name. |
| **Street address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip code** | The address on your AT&T bill |
| **E-mail address** | Where Tonet sends updates about this port |
| **Residential or business** | **Residential** for a personal account, **Business** for a business account |
| **Current service provider** | AT&T |
| **Account number** | Your AT&T account number |
| **Account PIN / passcode** | Your Number Transfer PIN, not your account passcode |
| **Telephone number** and **Type** | Each number you're moving, with **Wireless or VoIP**. Click **Add another number** for more. |

Put every AT&T number you're moving in one request. Tonet handles one port request at a time. Once it's approved, click **Port another number** to start the next.

Not ready? Click **Save draft** and come back later.

## After you submit

The **Port a Number** tab shows where your request is:

<PortTimeline />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Tonet has your request and checks it before filing it with the carrier. |
| **In progress** | Your request is being processed. Most ports complete in 7 to 10 business days. |
| **More information needed** | Read the message under **Messages**, type your answer in **Your reply**, attach a file if asked, and click **Send reply**. The request goes back to **In progress**. |
| **Approved** | The numbers appear under **My Phone Numbers** once the transfer completes. |
| **Not approved** | Read the **Reason for rejection**, fix it, and click **Edit and resubmit**. |

Tonet emails the address in **E-mail address** when it receives your request (with a copy of the letter you signed), when it needs something from you, and when the request is approved or not approved.

Your number keeps working with AT&T until the switch. The date can't be guaranteed: AT&T decides when the port completes.

When the number appears under **My Phone Numbers**, [choose where its calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go) and [who can use it](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number).

## Why AT&T ports get rejected

| Reason | How to fix it |
|---|---|
| Wireless Account Lock is on | [Unlock it](#turn-off-wireless-account-lock) in the AT&T app, then resubmit. |
| The PIN expired | PINs last 4 days, or 14 days on a business account. Get a new one and send it to Tonet. |
| You asked AT&T for a new PIN after you submitted | The new PIN cancelled the one Tonet has. Send Tonet the new one. |
| The account passcode was given instead of the PIN | Send the Number Transfer PIN instead. |
| The AT&T service was disconnected | Ask AT&T to reactivate the number, then resubmit. |
| Name, address or account number doesn't match | Copy them from your latest bill, or ask AT&T for your Customer Service Record (CSR). |

To send a new PIN: if the request shows **More information needed**, send it under **Messages**. If it shows **Not approved**, click **Edit and resubmit**. Otherwise, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).
