To move a Grasshopper number to Tonet, ask Grasshopper Support for your **Customer Service Record (CSR)**. It shows the details Grasshopper has on file, and comes with a PIN if your number needs one. Copy those details into **Manage › Phones › Port a Number** in Tonet. Keep your Grasshopper account open until the number has moved.

## Before you start

- Grasshopper only releases numbers from **paid accounts with no outstanding balance**. Free trials can't port numbers out.
- There's no waiting period. You can port out any time while your account is paid and active.
- Tonet, as your new provider, starts the port. Grasshopper doesn't approve it or speed it up.

## What you need from Grasshopper

| You need | Where to get it |
|---|---|
| Customer Service Record (CSR) | Ask Grasshopper Support. It can take up to 72 hours to arrive. |
| PIN | Comes with your CSR, if your number needs one |
| Account number | Your Grasshopper business phone number. Grasshopper uses it as your account number. |
| Proof the number is yours | Your latest Grasshopper receipt, or a screenshot of your Grasshopper account showing the number |

## Get your CSR and PIN

Contact Grasshopper Support and ask for your Customer Service Record. Have these ready:

- Your account number (your Grasshopper business phone number)
- Your account's 4-digit **Primary PIN**
- The last 4 digits of the card on file
- The billing address for that card

Grasshopper says the details on your Letter of Authorization (LOA) must match your CSR. Tonet builds your LOA from the port form, so copy the CSR's details into the form exactly.

## Is there a port-out lock?

Grasshopper's port-out page doesn't describe one. If your number needs a PIN to move, Grasshopper sends it with your CSR.

## Does Grasshopper charge to port out?

Grasshopper's help pages don't list a port-out fee. Grasshopper doesn't refund a subscription you cancel early.

## Keep your Grasshopper account open until the port finishes

- Your number keeps working on Grasshopper until the port completes.
- Porting a number out doesn't cancel your Grasshopper account. Your other numbers stay active.
- After the number has moved, contact Grasshopper Support to remove it or to cancel. Only the account's main contact can cancel.
- Grasshopper warns that numbers left on a cancelled account can go to another carrier and be used by someone else.

## Texting: register the number again in Tonet

If your number was approved for SMS registration at Grasshopper, Grasshopper removes that registration when the port starts. Once the number is in Tonet, [register it for texting](/phones#texting-from-a-number) in **Manage › Phones › SMS Registration**.

If you cancel the port, Grasshopper says you must register the number again with Grasshopper too.

## Move the number to Tonet

Owners, managers and administrators can start a port.

1. Get your CSR, and the PIN if Grasshopper sends one.
2. In Tonet, go to **Manage › Phones** and open the **Port a Number** tab.
3. Fill in the form as shown below. Tonet fills in some fields from your Tonet account, so check each one against your CSR.
4. Under **Proof the numbers are yours**, click **Attach a file**. Add your CSR, plus your latest Grasshopper receipt or a screenshot of your account showing the number.
5. Read the **Authorization** text. Sign in the box, then type your name in **Print your full name**.
6. Click **Submit porting request**.

| Field in Tonet | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **First name**, **Last name** | The account holder, exactly as on your CSR |
| **Organization** | The business name, exactly as on your CSR |
| **Street address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip code** | The address on your CSR |
| **E-mail address** | Where Tonet sends updates about this port |
| **Residential or business** | **Business** |
| **Current service provider** | Grasshopper |
| **Account number** | Your Grasshopper business phone number |
| **Account PIN / passcode** | The PIN Grasshopper sent with your CSR |
| **Telephone number** and **Type** | Each number you're moving. **Toll free** for a toll-free number, **Wireless or VoIP** for a local one. |

The form needs a PIN for **Wireless or VoIP** numbers. If Grasshopper didn't send one with your CSR, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) before you submit.

Put every Grasshopper number you're moving in one request. Tonet handles one port request at a time. Once it's approved, click **Port another number** to start the next.

Not ready? Click **Save draft** and come back later.

## After you submit

The **Port a Number** tab shows where your request is:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Tonet has your request and checks it before filing it with the carrier. |
| **In progress** | Your request is being processed. Most ports complete in 7 to 10 business days. |
| **More information needed** | Read the message under **Messages**, type your answer in **Your reply**, attach a file if asked, and click **Send reply**. The request goes back to **In progress**. |
| **Approved** | The numbers appear under **My Phone Numbers** once the transfer completes. |
| **Not approved** | Read the **Reason for rejection**, fix it, and click **Edit and resubmit**. |

Grasshopper doesn't tell you when the port finishes. Tonet emails the address in **E-mail address** when it receives your request (with a copy of the letter you signed), when it needs something from you, and when the request is approved or not approved.

Your number keeps working with Grasshopper until the switch. The date can't be guaranteed.

When the number appears under **My Phone Numbers**, [choose where its calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go), [who can use it](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number), and register it for texting.

## Why Grasshopper ports get rejected

| Reason | How to fix it |
|---|---|
| The account has a balance, or is a free trial | Grasshopper only releases numbers from paid accounts with no balance. Pay it, then resubmit. |
| Details don't match the CSR | Copy the name, business name and address from your CSR exactly. Click **Edit and resubmit**. |
| Wrong account number | Use your Grasshopper business phone number. |
| Missing or wrong PIN | Use the PIN that came with your CSR, or ask Grasshopper Support for it. |
| The Grasshopper account was cancelled before the port finished | Grasshopper says the account must stay active until the port completes. Contact Grasshopper Support. |

More reasons and fixes: [Why was my port rejected?](/phones/port_number/rejected)
