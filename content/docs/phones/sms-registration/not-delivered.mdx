Business texts fail for a few reasons: the number isn't registered for business texting, the carrier filtered the text, the person replied STOP, or their number can't get texts. Tonet shows the reason under the text in [Messages](/chat).

## Check what Messages says

Under each text you send, a small mark shows how far it got. Point at it to see its name.

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| A clock | Still sending |
| One check mark | Sent: the carrier took it |
| Two check marks | Delivered to the phone |
| A red reason, like **Carrier filtered this as spam** | Not delivered: the carrier refused it |

If a text couldn't leave at all, a **Not sent** message pops up and the text stays on screen as failed. See [Texts that say Not sent](#texts-that-say-not-sent).

## What each reason means

| Messages says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Number is not registered for A2P 10DLC** | Your number isn't on an approved registration. See [Your number isn't registered](#your-number-isnt-registered). |
| **Toll-free number is not verified** | See [Toll-free numbers](#toll-free-numbers). |
| **Carrier filtered this as spam** | See [Carrier filtering](#carrier-filtering). |
| **Carrier rejected it without a reason** | See [Carrier filtering](#carrier-filtering). |
| **Recipient replied STOP and is unsubscribed** | See [STOP and HELP](#stop-and-help). |
| **Recipient has blocked this number** | They blocked your number. Call them instead. |
| **Handset unreachable or switched off** | Their phone is off or out of coverage. Try again later. |
| **Unknown or inactive number** | Check the number. |
| **Not a valid mobile number** | Check the number. |
| **Landline, or carrier cannot receive texts** | Call them instead. |
| **Carrier cannot deliver to this number** | Check the number, or call them. |
| **Carrier error** and a number | Look it up in the next table. |

New Tonet accounts text through Plivo. On those accounts, Messages shows the reason as **Carrier error** and Plivo's number:

| Carrier error | What it means |
|---|---|
| 30 | The carrier's spam filter stopped it. See [Carrier filtering](#carrier-filtering). |
| 50 | Not a valid number, or a landline |
| 70 | The number is out of service |
| 80 | Their phone is off or out of reach. Try again later. |
| 100 | Their network can't take this text |
| 200 | They replied STOP. See [STOP and HELP](#stop-and-help). |
| 420 | It expired before reaching a carrier. For US texts, check that your number is registered. |
| 960 | The toll-free number isn't verified. See [Toll-free numbers](#toll-free-numbers). |

## Your number isn't registered

Carriers block business texts from numbers that aren't registered for A2P 10DLC.

- A number that isn't registered shows **Texting not approved** on **Manage › Phones**, and Messages won't send from it. If none of your numbers can text yet, Messages shows **Pending Carrier Verification**. [Register your business](/phones/sms-registration).
- A red **Texting blocked** on a number's card means the carrier refused its recent texts because it says the number isn't registered (or, for a toll-free number, not verified). Calls work as usual. Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).
- Bought a number after you registered? It needs adding to your campaign. See [Add a number later](/phones/sms-registration#add-a-number-later).

## Carrier filtering

Carriers filter texts that look unwanted, even from registered numbers. To stay out of the filters:

- Only text people who agreed to get your texts, and only the kind they agreed to. Consent to reminders isn't consent to promotions.
- Say who you are in each text.
- Don't send anything on the list of [content you can't register](/phones/sms-registration/rejected#content-you-cant-register), like alcohol, gambling or debt collection.
- Don't spread the same text across many numbers to get past filters. Carriers don't allow it.
- Watch your opt-outs. Many STOP replies, or people reporting your texts as spam by forwarding them to 7726, can get a number suspended.

## Links and URL shorteners

- Use full links on your own website.
- Don't use public shorteners like bit.ly or TinyURL. Carriers filter them. If you shorten links, use a short domain only your business uses.
- Avoid links that redirect several times. They can look like phishing.
- Any phone number in a text should be one people can see is yours.
- If your texts have links or phone numbers, tick those boxes when you register. See [Message content boxes](/phones/sms-registration/what-to-write#message-content-boxes).

## STOP and HELP

- When someone replies STOP, END, QUIT, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE, your later texts to them are blocked. You'll see **Not sent — the carrier rejected this message.**, **Recipient replied STOP and is unsubscribed**, or **Carrier error 200**.
- To get your texts again, they text START to your number.
- Don't text them from another of your numbers. Carriers expect you to honor an opt-out.
- HELP doesn't opt anyone out. Your opt-in wording should tell people to reply HELP for help. See [What to write](/phones/sms-registration/what-to-write#sms-opt-in-wording-for-your-form).

## Texts that say Not sent

| Messages says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Not sent — the account balance is empty. Add funds to resume texting.** | An owner or manager [adds funds](/billing#add-funds). Other roles see **Not sent — please contact your administrator.** |
| **Not sent — you're not authorized to text from this number.** | Ask an owner, manager or administrator to add you on the number's [Permissions tab](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number). |
| **Not sent — no valid recipient on this thread.** | Start a new conversation with the number. |
| **Not sent — the carrier rejected this message.** | Check the number. The person may have replied STOP. |
| **Not sent — something went wrong. Try again.** | Send it again. If it keeps failing, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai). |

A text is charged once the carrier takes it, even if it isn't delivered later. A text that says **Not sent** isn't charged.

## Toll-free numbers

Carriers block or limit texts from toll-free numbers that haven't passed toll-free verification. This is separate from SMS registration, and Tonet's form doesn't do it. Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) to ask about texting from a toll-free number. More about [toll-free numbers](https://tonet.ai/toll-free-number-for-business/).

## Still not delivered?

Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) with the number you texted from, the number you texted, and when.
