Carriers approve a registration when the description, the sample texts and the opt-in all describe the same texts. Find your business below, copy the template into **Manage › Phones › SMS Registration**, and replace everything in [square brackets].

## Rules every template follows

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Campaign description** | Say who you are, who gets your texts, and why. At least 40 characters. One word like "Marketing" isn't enough. |
| **Sample message #1** and **#2** | Fill in both, even though #2 is marked optional: registrations need at least two. Name your business in each. Put the parts that change in [square brackets]. |
| Opt-out | At least one sample says "Reply STOP to opt out." Tonet won't submit without it. |
| Links and phone numbers | If your real texts have them, put one in a sample and tick the matching box under **Message content**. Use full links on your own website, never bit.ly or TinyURL. |
| **How do end-users consent to receive messages?** | Describe every way people sign up, with web addresses. 40 to 2,048 characters. |
| **Opt-in auto-reply message** | Only if you fill in **Opt-in keywords**. 20 to 320 characters. Name your business, say the texts recur, and say how to get help and how to opt out. |

## Pick a use case

| Use case | Pick it for |
|---|---|
| **2FA** | Login and verification codes |
| **Account Notifications** | Updates about a customer's account |
| **Customer Care** | Support and back-and-forth with customers |
| **Delivery Notifications** | Order and delivery status |
| **Fraud Alert Messaging** | Warnings about possible fraud |
| **Higher Education** | Colleges and universities |
| **Marketing** | Sales, offers and promotions |
| **Mixed** | More than one of these |
| **Polling and voting** | Surveys and polls, not political ones |
| **Public Service Announcement** | Raising awareness of a topic |
| **Security Alert** | Telling people a system was compromised |

Sending more than one kind of text? Pick **Mixed**, describe each kind in the description, and make your two samples two different kinds.

The use case is separate from the campaign type, which sets your fees. See [What it costs](/phones/sms-registration#what-it-costs).

## Get your website ready

Carriers check the website on your registration. Before you submit, make sure it:

- Loads over https, is about your business, and isn't a parked or login-only page.
- Has an about page, a contact page, a privacy policy and terms.
- Says in the privacy policy that you don't share mobile numbers. For example:

```text
We don't sell or share your mobile number or text message opt-in with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.
```

- Says in the terms who sends the texts, that message and data rates may apply, and to reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

## SMS opt-in wording for your form

Put this next to a checkbox under the phone field, on your website or paper form. Make the box optional, and let people tick it themselves.

```text
Text me [appointment reminders and updates] from [Business name] at this number. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Privacy Policy: [yourwebsite.com/privacy]. Terms: [yourwebsite.com/terms].
```

- Replace [appointment reminders and updates] with the kinds of texts you'll send.
- Promotions need their own checkbox. See [Promotions and offers](#promotions-and-offers).
- Keep a record of each opt-in: when, how, and the number.

## Describe how people opt in

This goes in **How do end-users consent to receive messages?** Use the one that fits, or combine them.

**Website form:**

```text
Customers opt in on our [booking form] at [yourwebsite.com/book] by ticking an optional checkbox under the phone field. It reads: "[your opt-in wording]". The page links to our Privacy Policy at [yourwebsite.com/privacy] and Terms at [yourwebsite.com/terms]. Screenshot of the form: [link to screenshot].
```

**Paper form at your front desk:**

```text
Customers opt in on our paper intake form by writing their mobile number and ticking a box that reads: "[your opt-in wording]". A copy of the form is online at [yourwebsite.com/sms-form]. Privacy Policy: [yourwebsite.com/privacy]. Terms: [yourwebsite.com/terms].
```

**Texting a keyword:**

```text
Customers text [JOIN] to [your Tonet number], which we show on [our website and the sign at our counter] with this wording: "[your opt-in wording]". Photo of the sign: [link]. They get a confirmation text right away. Privacy Policy: [yourwebsite.com/privacy]. Terms: [yourwebsite.com/terms].
```

If people can text a keyword, put it in **Opt-in keywords** and fill in the **Opt-in auto-reply message**:

```text
[Business name]: You're signed up for [appointment updates]. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
```

## Home services

For plumbing, HVAC, cleaning, landscaping and similar. **Use case:** Customer Care.

**Campaign description:**

```text
[Business name] is a [plumbing] company in [city, state]. We text customers who book a job with us: appointment confirmations, arrival updates, estimates, and answers to their questions. Customers opt in on our online booking form.
```

**Sample message #1:**

```text
[Business name]: Your appointment is confirmed for [date], between [time window]. Reply here with any questions. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

**Sample message #2:**

```text
[Business name]: Your technician is on the way and should arrive in about [30] minutes. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## Restaurant

**Use case:** Customer Care.

**Campaign description:**

```text
[Business name] is a restaurant in [city, state]. We text guests who order takeout or book a table with us: order-ready notices and reservation confirmations. Guests opt in on our online ordering and booking pages.
```

**Sample message #1:**

```text
[Business name]: Your order #[number] is ready for pickup at the front counter. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

**Sample message #2:**

```text
[Business name]: Your table for [party size] is booked for [date] at [time]. Reply C to confirm or reply with changes. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

Leave out drinks and happy hours. Alcohol is on the list of content that isn't permitted from local numbers.

## Login codes for your app or website

**Use case:** 2FA.

**Campaign description:**

```text
[Business name] sends one-time passcodes to people signing in to [yourwebsite.com]. Users add their mobile number in their account settings and ask for a code each time they sign in.
```

**Sample message #1:**

```text
[Business name]: Your login code is [code]. It expires in 10 minutes. Don't share it with anyone.
```

**Sample message #2:**

```text
[Business name]: [code] is your verification code. If you didn't ask for it, ignore this text. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## Promotions and offers

**Use case:** Marketing.

Promotions need written consent: a box people tick that clearly says they'll get marketing texts. Consent to reminders doesn't cover promotions.

**Opt-in wording for a marketing checkbox:**

```text
Yes, text me offers and news from [Business name], up to [4] messages a month. Consent isn't required to buy. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Privacy Policy: [yourwebsite.com/privacy]. Terms: [yourwebsite.com/terms].
```

**Campaign description:**

```text
[Business name], a [bakery] in [city, state], sends up to [4] texts a month with offers and news to customers who signed up for marketing texts at [yourwebsite.com/texts] or at our counter.
```

**Sample message #1:**

```text
[Business name]: This week only, [20%] off [item] with code [CODE]. Shop at [yourwebsite.com]. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

**Sample message #2:**

```text
[Business name]: Our [spring menu] is here. Visit us at [location] this weekend. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

Tick **Messages will include embedded links** if your promotions link to your website.

## Message content boxes

Tick every box that matches your texts. The form warns that leaving one out causes rejections.

| Your texts include | Tick |
|---|---|
| A link | **Messages will include embedded links** |
| A phone number | **Messages will include phone numbers** |
| Loans or lending | **Messages include content related to direct lending or other loan arrangement** |
| Age-restricted content | **Messages include age-gated content** |
