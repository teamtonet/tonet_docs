US carriers only deliver business texts from registered numbers. To text from a local Tonet number, register your business once in **Manage › Phones › SMS Registration**. Tonet checks your form, files it with the carriers, and emails you when it's decided. Carrier review usually takes a few business days. You pay one-time fees when it's filed, then a monthly fee.

Owners, managers and administrators can register. Regular users: ask one of them.

This registration is called **A2P 10DLC**: business texting from an ordinary 10-digit number.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Your **legal business name** and **EIN**, exactly as on your IRS letter (the CP 575 or 147C letter).
- A **website** for your business that loads, with an about page, a contact page, a privacy policy and terms.
- How people **agree to get your texts**, for example a checkbox on your booking form.
- **Two example texts** you will really send.

Not sure what to write? Copy from [What to write](/phones/sms-registration/what-to-write).

You can only register numbers you can use. If the tab says **No numbers assigned to you**, add yourself on the number's [Permissions tab](/phones#choose-who-can-use-a-number) first. Owners and managers are added to new numbers automatically.

## Register your business

1. Go to **Manage › Phones** and open the **SMS Registration** tab.
2. Pick a **Campaign type**. Its fees show under it. See [What it costs](#what-it-costs).
3. Check **Business details**. Tonet fills in what it already knows. The **Legal business name** and **Employer Identification Number (EIN)** must match your IRS records exactly.
4. Fill in the **Contact person**: title, name, email and phone number.
5. Under **Campaign**, enter your **Estimated monthly volume**, pick a **Use case**, and write the **Campaign description**, **Sample message #1** and **Sample message #2**.
6. Under **Message content**, tick everything your texts will include: links, phone numbers, lending, or age-gated content.
7. Under **Consent**, fill in **How do end-users consent to receive messages?** Fill in **Opt-in keywords** and **Opt-in auto-reply message** only if people can sign up by texting you a word.
8. Under **Numbers**, pick the numbers that will text.
9. Read the list under **Not permitted from local numbers** and tick **I confirm my messaging does not include any of the above**.
10. Check **E-mail for notifications**. Tonet sends updates there.
11. Type your name in **Type your full name to certify** and click **Submit**.

![The SMS Registration form: how it works, campaign type and business details](/images/phones/sms-registration-form.webp)

Not finished? Click **Save draft** and come back later.

If something is missing or too short, Tonet lists it under **Fix these before submitting** and highlights the field. Nothing goes to the carriers until it's fixed.

<Callout type="info">
At least one sample message must tell people how to opt out, like "Reply STOP to opt out." The form won't submit without it.
</Callout>

## What it costs

| Campaign type | Monthly | One-time brand registration | One-time campaign vetting |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sole Proprietor** | $2.00 | $4.00 | $15.00 |
| **Low Volume Standard** | $1.50 | $4.00 | $15.00 |
| **Standard** | $10.00 | $44.00 | $15.00 |
| **Charity / 501(c)(3) Nonprofit** | $3.00 | Not shown | $15.00 |
| **Emergency Services** | $5.00 | Not shown | $15.00 |

- **Low Volume Standard** is for a mix of uses, up to 2,000 SMS segments and MMS a day to T-Mobile customers.
- **Standard** covers every use case on the form.
- **Charity** needs proof of nonprofit status. **Emergency Services** needs proof of emergency service designation.
- The form shows your account's exact prices when you pick a type.

When you pay:

- **One-time fees:** when your registration goes to the carriers for vetting. They aren't refunded if it's rejected, and a resubmission is charged again.
- **Monthly fee:** on the 1st of each month once your campaign is approved. It shows in [Billing](/billing) as **SMS registration (monthly)**.
- **Texts:** 3¢ each on both plans, on top of these fees. See [plans and prices](https://tonet.ai/business-phone-plans/).

## Sole proprietors

The **Sole Proprietor** type is $2.00 a month, with a $4.00 brand registration and $15.00 vetting. It allows one campaign per brand.

- Tonet's form needs an EIN for every registration, Sole Proprietor included.
- The carriers' Sole Proprietor type is for people without an EIN. If your business has an EIN, they expect **Low Volume Standard** or **Standard**.
- No EIN? Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) before you submit.

## How long it takes

1. **Submitted:** Tonet checks your form and your website before anything is filed.
2. **Under review:** the carriers vet your campaign. This usually takes a few business days. A brand sent to manual review can take 7 business days or more.
3. **Approved:** Tonet emails you. Text from a number once its card shows **Texting approved**.

Charity and emergency registrations may need extra approval from the carriers. Still **Under review** after two weeks? Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai).

## What each status means

| Status | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | Not submitted yet | Click **Continue filling in** |
| **Submitted** | Tonet is checking it before it goes to the carriers | Nothing |
| **Under review** | The carriers are vetting it | Nothing |
| **More information needed** | Tonet support needs something from you | Read the message. Answer under **Messages** and click **Send reply**, or click **Edit and resubmit** |
| **Approved** | Your campaign is registered | Text from numbers that show **Texting approved** |
| **Rejected** | It wasn't approved. The reason shows under **Reason for rejection** | Fix it and click **Edit and resubmit**. See [Registration rejected](/phones/sms-registration/rejected) |

When you answer under **Messages**, you can click **Attach files** to add a screenshot of your opt-in form or anything else support asked for. Your registration then goes back to **Under review**.

Tonet emails your **E-mail for notifications** when it needs something from you, and when your registration is approved or rejected.

## Texting while you wait

- Calls, voicemail and team chat work as usual. Texts people send you still arrive.
- On **Manage › Phones**, a number that can't text yet shows **Texting not approved**.
- In [Messages](/chat), if none of your numbers can text yet, the message box shows **Pending Carrier Verification** where the number goes, and you can't send. A new SMS conversation shows **Pending carrier verification** next to **From:**. Point at it to see why, or click it to open **SMS Registration**.
- If some of your numbers are approved, only those appear under **From:**.

Carriers block texts from unregistered numbers, so Tonet doesn't send them.

## Add a number later

A number you buy after registering can't text until it's added to your campaign.

- If **SMS Registration** shows **Numbers not yet enabled for texting**, tick the numbers and click **Request texting on** (the button counts the numbers you ticked). Tonet support adds them. There's no new vetting fee and no form to fill in.
- Otherwise, email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) with the numbers to add.

## Toll-free numbers

This registration is for local numbers. Texting from a toll-free number needs a different carrier check, called toll-free verification, which this form doesn't do. It can take days to weeks. Email [support@tonet.ai](mailto:support@tonet.ai) to ask about texting from a toll-free number. More about [toll-free numbers](https://tonet.ai/toll-free-number-for-business/).
