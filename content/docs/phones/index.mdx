Your phone numbers live in **Manage › Phones**. Owners, managers and admins can open it.

The page has these tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **My Phone Numbers** | Your numbers and where their calls go |
| **SMS Registration** | Registering your numbers for business texting |
| **Port a Number** | [Moving a number you already have](/phones/port_number) to Tonet |
| **Verified Caller ID** | [Showing an outside number](/phones/verified_caller_id) on your calls. Only some accounts have this tab. |
| **Blocked** | [Numbers you've blocked](/phones/blacklist) |

## Buy a number

Owners and managers can buy numbers.

1. Go to **Manage › Phones** and click **Add a new Phone Number**.
2. Under **Number Type**, pick **Local Number** or **Toll Free**.
3. Under **Area Code**, type 3 digits and click **Search**.
4. Under **Select Numbers**, tick the numbers you want.
5. Click **Submit**.

The number is ready right away. Your balance must cover the price: $2.50 for a local number, $3.00 for a toll-free one. See [Billing](/billing#what-you-pay).

Anyone who can use the new number and doesn't have an outgoing number yet will now call from it.

## Choose where calls go

1. Go to **Manage › Phones** and click the number.
2. Open the **Route & settings** tab.
3. In **Go To**, pick where calls go:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Users** | Rings one person |
| **Teams** | Rings a [team](/groups) |
| **IVRs** | Plays a [menu](/auto_attendant), like "Press 1 for sales" |
| **Voicemail** | Sends callers straight to a person's or team's voicemail |
| **Features** | **Busy** (callers hear busy), or **Phone Number** to forward calls to another number |

4. Click **Save Settings**.

Want different routing at night or on holidays? On the same tab:

- **Schedule:** pick a [schedule](/schedule) to route by day and time.
- **Calendars:** pick [calendar events](/calendar), like a holiday, that override everything else.

When a call comes in, Tonet checks in this order:

1. A calendar event happening now
2. The schedule
3. **Go To**

## Choose who can use a number

People who can use a number can call from it and text from it. They also see its texts in **Messages**.

1. Click the number and open the **Permissions** tab.
2. Under **Phone Access**, click each person to tick or untick them. **Select All** and **Clear All** help.
3. Click **Save Settings**.

This doesn't change who the number's calls ring. That's set in **Go To**.

## Show a different caller ID

On the **Route & settings** tab, **Caller ID** sets the number people see when you call from this number. It is the number itself unless you pick an outside number you've [verified](/phones/verified_caller_id). Only some accounts have Verified Caller ID.

## Name a number

On the **Route & settings** tab, type a **Name**, like "Front desk", and click **Save Settings**. Your team sees this name in the dashboard.

## Texting from a number

Carriers only deliver business texts from registered numbers.

- Each number shows **Texting approved** or **Texting not approved**.
- Register your numbers in the **SMS Registration** tab. Its status shows Draft, Submitted, Under review, More information needed, Approved or Rejected.
- Calls work while a number waits for approval.

## Delete a number

1. On **My Phone Numbers**, open the number's **⋯** menu and click **Delete**.
2. Click **Delete Phone** to confirm.

This can't be undone. The number goes back to the carrier.

## What each number shows

Each number's card shows where its calls go, who can use it, whether texting is approved, and the daily spam check: **Spam check: clean** or **Spam check: flagged**.

Open a number's **Activity** tab to see its calls and texts.
