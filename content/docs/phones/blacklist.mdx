Block a number and it can't reach your business. Its calls end before they ring anyone, and its texts never reach **Messages**. One list covers your whole account and all your numbers.

You can block a number three ways: while it rings, after a call, or from the **Blocked** tab.

## Block a caller while the phone rings

On the incoming call, click **Block**. The call ends and the number is blocked.

A message confirms it, with **Undo** for 8 seconds. Anyone on your account can do this.

## Block a number after a call

When a call ends, its [call card](/calls/after-call-review) shows **Block number** at the bottom. Click it. Once blocked, the card says **Blocked** with **Unblock** next to it.

## Block a number from the Blocked tab

Owners, managers and admins:

1. Go to **Manage › Phones** and open the **Blocked** tab.
2. Click **Block a number**.
3. In **Phone Number**, type the number or pick a recent caller. A US number needs its 10 digits. A number in another country needs its country code.
4. Add a reason if you like, in **Reason (Optional)**.
5. Click **Add**.

You can't block one of your own numbers, or a caller whose number is hidden.

## What a blocked number gets

| They try to | What happens |
|---|---|
| Call any of your numbers | Tonet hangs up without answering. Nothing rings on your side. |
| Text any of your numbers | The text never reaches **Messages**. Tonet only counts it. |

## The Blocked list

**Manage › Phones › Blocked** lists every blocked number. Use **Search numbers or names** to find one.

![The Blocked tab: each blocked number, when it was blocked and an Unblock link](/images/phones/blocked-numbers.webp)

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Number** | The blocked number |
| **Who** | The contact's name if the number is saved (click it to open the contact), or **Unknown**. The reason shows under it. |
| **Blocked** | The date, then who blocked it and how: added here, from a call, from the contact, as a robocaller, or by Tonet support |
| **Texts stopped** | How many texts it has sent you since it was blocked |
| Last column | **Unblock** |

## Unblock a number

Click **Unblock** in its row. Its calls and texts reach you again right away.

Texts it sent while blocked don't come back. Tonet didn't keep them.
