Tonet for iPhone rings like a normal phone call, on the lock screen too, even when the app is closed. It needs iOS 15.4 or later.

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step>

**Install Tonet** from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tonet/id6755547036). It's free, from Tonet LLC.

</Step>
<Step>

**Sign in** with your dashboard **Email** and **Password**, then tap **Login**. No password because you use Google? Tap **Forgot password?** to set one first.

</Step>
<Step>

**Allow what it asks for.** Tap **Allow** for each:

| Permission | Why Tonet needs it |
|---|---|
| **Microphone** | Required to make and receive phone calls |
| **Notifications** | Alerts you to incoming calls and messages |
| **Contacts** | Shows names for incoming calls and messages |

iOS may also ask about the camera. Calling uses the same system as video calls, so iOS asks. Tonet doesn't use your camera.

</Step>
<Step>

**Check it worked.** Open **Profile** and tap **Permissions**: each one should be on. Tap one that isn't to fix it. Then have someone call your extension.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Taking calls

A call shows on the iPhone's own call screen, so you answer it the way you answer any call: slide on the lock screen, or tap the green button.

- The caller shows as their name, then **→** and the business number they called. You can tell a call to your main line from a call to your direct number.
- Answer with AirPods or in the car the same way you do other calls.
- Tonet calls don't go in the iPhone's **Recents**. They're under **Home › call history** in Tonet, and in [Calls](/analytics/call_logs) in the dashboard.
- Missed calls and new texts show as notifications. Tap one to open it.

## If your iPhone doesn't ring

Work down this list:

1. **Is the app signed in?** Open Tonet. If you see the login screen, sign in again.
2. **Check the top of the Home screen.** An icon there means no internet or the phone service isn't connected. Wait a moment on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
3. **Check Permissions.** **Profile › Permissions**. Tap anything that isn't on.
4. **Check Focus.** A Focus like **Do Not Disturb** or **Sleep** silences calls from apps. Add Tonet to the Focus's allowed apps in **Settings › Focus**, or turn the Focus off.
5. **Check iPhone notifications.** **Settings › Notifications › Tonet**: **Allow Notifications** on.
6. **Is your line full?** Your line rings on up to 3 devices. If the dashboard says the line is full, sign out of Tonet somewhere you don't use it. See [one line on up to three devices](/phone-app#one-line-on-up-to-three-devices).
7. **Does the call reach you at all?** If calls to your number go to a team or a menu, make sure you're in that team. See [where calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go).

Still not ringing? Sign out (**Profile › Logout**), sign back in, and try again. Then [contact support](mailto:support@tonet.ai) with your extension and the time of the call.
