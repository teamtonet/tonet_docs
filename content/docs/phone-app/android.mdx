Tonet for Android rings with a full-screen call, on the lock screen too, even when the app is closed. It needs Android 11 or later.

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step>

**Install Tonet** from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.tonet). It's free.

</Step>
<Step>

**Sign in** with your dashboard **Email** and **Password**, then tap **Login**. No password because you use Google? Tap **Forgot password?** to set one first.

</Step>
<Step>

**Allow what it asks for:**

| Permission | Why Tonet needs it |
|---|---|
| **Microphone** | Required to make and receive phone calls |
| **Notifications** | Alerts you to incoming calls and messages |
| **Contacts** | Shows names for incoming calls and messages |

</Step>
<Step>

**Turn on the two Android settings calls need.** Open **Profile** and tap **Permissions**. Tap each of these and allow it:

| Permission | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Unrestricted Battery** | Allows incoming calls when app is closed. Without it, Android can hold calls back to save battery. |
| **Full-Screen Calls** | Shows incoming calls on the lock screen |

</Step>
<Step>

**Check it worked.** Every line in **Profile › Permissions** should be on. Lock the phone and have someone call your extension.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Taking calls

An incoming call fills the screen with the caller's name, **is calling**, and **Answer** and **Reject**. On an unlocked phone it shows as a notification with the same buttons.

- Missed calls and new texts show as notifications. Tap one to open it.
- Calls are in **Home › call history** in Tonet, and in [Calls](/analytics/call_logs) in the dashboard.
- Photos you open in a conversation have a **Save** button that saves them to your gallery.

## If your phone doesn't ring

Work down this list:

1. **Is the app signed in?** Open Tonet. If you see the login screen, sign in again.
2. **Check Permissions.** **Profile › Permissions**. **Unrestricted Battery** and **Full-Screen Calls** matter most. Tap anything that isn't on.
3. **Check your phone's own battery saver.** Some phones, like Samsung, Xiaomi and OnePlus, put apps to sleep on top of Android's own setting. In your phone's **Settings**, find Tonet under **Apps › Battery** and set it to **Unrestricted** (or **No restrictions**). Take it off any list of sleeping apps.
4. **Check Do Not Disturb.** If it's on, add Tonet to the apps allowed to interrupt, or turn it off.
5. **Check the top of the Home screen.** An icon there means no internet or the phone service isn't connected.
6. **Is your line full?** Your line rings on up to 3 devices. See [one line on up to three devices](/phone-app#one-line-on-up-to-three-devices).
7. **Does the call reach you at all?** If calls to your number go to a team or a menu, make sure you're in that team. See [where calls go](/phones#choose-where-calls-go).

Still not ringing? Sign out (**Profile › Logout**), sign back in, and try again. Then [contact support](mailto:support@tonet.ai) with your extension and the time of the call.
