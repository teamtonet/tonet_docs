There are two ways to get your business calls on your cell:

| | [Tonet phone app](/phone-app) | Forward to your cell number |
|---|---|---|
| Rings | In the app, on the lock screen too | As a normal call to your cell |
| Install anything? | Yes, from the App Store or Google Play | No |
| You call out from | Your business number | Your cell number, unless you use the app or dashboard |
| Others can also answer | Yes, a team can ring together | No, the number rings only your cell |
| Call history, recordings, AI summaries | Yes | Yes, for the part of the call Tonet carries |

The app is the better fit for a team. Forwarding suits a number that only you answer, or a phone that can't run the app.

## Forward a number to a cell

Owners, managers and admins:

1. Go to **Manage › Phones** and open the number.
2. Open the **Route & settings** tab.
3. In **Go To**, pick **Features**, then **Phone Number**.
4. In **Forward to Phone Number**, type the cell number and confirm.
5. Click **Save Settings**.

Calls to that number now ring the cell. The caller doesn't hear anything different.

**Know who's calling before you answer.** Turn on **SMS notification on forwarded calls** on the same tab. When a call is forwarded, Tonet texts the cell the caller's details, so you can tell a business call from a personal one.

## Forward only after hours

Keep calls going to your team during the day and to a cell at night:

1. Make a [schedule](/schedule) with your office hours.
2. On the number's **Route & settings** tab, pick the schedule in **Schedule**.
3. In the schedule, send calls outside office hours to **Phone Number** and type the cell.

For holidays, add a [calendar](/calendar) event that sends calls to a **Forward Number** for the day.

You can also put the cell on a [menu](/auto_attendant) key, like "Press 3 for the on-call technician."

## Good to know

- A [team](/groups) can't include an outside number. To ring a team and a cell together, have that person use the [phone app](/phone-app) instead.
- To stop forwarding, change **Go To** back to a person, a team or a menu, and click **Save Settings**.
