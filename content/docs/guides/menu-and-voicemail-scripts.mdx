These are templates for the recordings callers hear: your phone menu (auto attendant), after hours, holidays, voicemail and a recording notice. Copy one, change the words in [brackets], and record it. Each script says where it goes in Tonet, and the setup steps are at the end of this page.

## Before you record

- Say the option first, then the key: "For billing, press 2."
- Put the option most callers want first.
- Keep a menu to four or five options. Need more? Send one key to a [second menu](/auto_attendant#make-a-menu-inside-a-menu).
- Callers can press a key before the greeting ends, so you don't need long pauses.
- Callers can also dial a person's extension during the greeting. If your team has extensions, say so.
- If a caller presses nothing, the greeting plays again, up to 3 times, then the call ends. Set **No_response** to a person or team so these callers still reach someone.
- Record in a quiet room and read a little slower than you talk.

## Auto attendant script examples

### Main menu for any business

```text
Thank you for calling [Business name].
For sales, press 1.
For support, press 2.
For billing, press 3.
If you know your party's extension, you can dial it at any time.
To hear these options again, press 9.
```

In Tonet: keys 1 to 3 go to [teams](/groups), key 9 goes to this same menu, and **No_response** goes to your front desk.

## After-hours scripts

### Closed for the day

```text
Thank you for calling [Business name]. Our office is closed.
Our hours are [Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM].
To leave a message, press 1, or stay on the line.
[For emergencies, press 2.]
We'll call you back [the next business day].
```

In Tonet: an after-hours menu. Key 1 and **No_response** go to **Voicemail** › your team's voicemail. Key 2 goes to the person on call, or under **Features**, **Phone Number** forwards to an outside number.

### Closed for lunch

```text
Thank you for calling [Business name]. We're on a lunch break and back at [1 PM].
To leave a message, press 1, or stay on the line.
```

In Tonet: a menu set up like the one above, played by a [schedule](/schedule) route for your lunch hour. Split the day around it so routes don't overlap: for example 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM for your team, and 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM for this menu.

## Holiday greeting scripts

### Closed for a holiday

```text
Thank you for calling [Business name]. Our office is closed today for [holiday].
We'll be back on [day, date] at [time].
To leave a message, press 1, or stay on the line.
[For emergencies, press 2.]
```

### Closed for the holiday season

```text
Happy holidays from everyone at [Business name].
Our office is closed from [date] through [date], and we reopen on [date].
To leave a message, press 1, or stay on the line, and we'll call you back when we're back.
```

### Open with holiday hours

```text
Thank you for calling [Business name]. For [holiday], we're open until [time] today.
For sales, press 1.
For support, press 2.
```

In Tonet: a holiday menu, played by a [calendar](/calendar) event for those dates. As with the after-hours menu, send key 1 and **No_response** to voicemail, so "stay on the line" leads to a message.

## Voicemail greeting scripts

### Main business voicemail

```text
You've reached [Business name]. We can't take your call right now.
Please leave your name, your number and a short message, and we'll call you back [within one business day].
```

### Team voicemail

```text
You've reached the [Sales] team at [Business name]. We're helping other customers right now.
Please leave your name, your number and how we can help, and someone on the team will call you back.
```

### Your own voicemail

```text
Hi, you've reached [Your name] at [Business name]. I can't take your call right now.
Please leave your name, your number and a good time to reach you, and I'll call you back.
```

### Out of the office

```text
Hi, you've reached [Your name] at [Business name]. I'm out of the office until [date].
Please leave a message, or for help sooner, call our main line at [number].
```

## Call-recording notice

```text
Thank you for calling [Business name]. This call may be recorded for quality and training.
```

Tonet doesn't play a recording notice on its own. Put this line at the start of your main menu greeting.

Do your calls skip the menu and go straight to a person or team? Make a menu whose greeting is only this line, and set its **No_response** to that person or team. Then set the number's **Go To** to this menu. Callers hear the notice, and a few seconds later they're put through.

## Set them up in Tonet

### Menu greetings: main menu, after hours, holidays, recording notice

1. Go to **Manage › Menus** and click **New menu**, or open a menu.
2. Click **Upload/Record Audio...**. Record the script, or upload an MP3. Click **Upload**.
3. Add the keys under **Routes** and click **Save**.
4. Go to **Manage › Phones**, click the number, and open **Route & settings**. In **Go To**, pick **IVRs** and the menu. Click **Save Settings**.

For "press 9 to hear these options again", point key 9 at the same menu. All the details are in [Menus](/auto_attendant).

### After hours

1. Make an after-hours menu with an after-hours script.
2. In **Manage › Schedules**, make a schedule with your open hours. Send those hours to your main menu or your team.
3. On the number, set **Schedule** to it, and **Go To** to the after-hours menu.

Calls outside the schedule's hours go to **Go To**. See [Schedules](/schedule).

### Holidays

1. Make a holiday menu with a holiday script.
2. In **Manage › Calendar**, click **New Event**. Set **Destination** to **IVRs** › your holiday menu, and the start and end.
3. On each number, tick the event in **Calendars** and click **Save Settings**.

See [Calendar](/calendar).

### Voicemail greetings

1. Open **Calls**, click the **⋯** menu, then **Voicemail greeting and email**.
2. In **Select Mailbox**, pick your own mailbox, or a team you're on.
3. Click **Choose Audio File** (WAV, MP3 or OGG, up to 10 MB), then **Upload Greeting**.

To record your own greeting by phone instead, dial **\*865** and follow the prompts. Until you add a greeting, callers hear Tonet's standard one. See [Voicemail](/calls/voicemail).
